MARIUS CODA (NPC)

Who he is Legionary, 3rd contubernium, 6th Legion. Was on watch duty when the Berserker appeared. Has been in combat for ~10 minutes and is dissociating.
State Standing in the middle of the courtyard, sword in hand, staring at the Berserker. Not fighting, not fleeing. Wisdom save DC 10 failed — he is Frightened. He will snap out of it if a party member reaches him and spends a Bonus Action to steady him (Persuasion DC 8 or Medicine DC 8).
Recovered Marius Acts as an ally (Guard stat block). Knows: the cultists appeared from the vault shaft ~20 minutes before the party entered, so something in the vault communicated with them. This is a corruption clue — the Wight or the spear can influence minds above ground.

SURPRISE CONDITIONS

Stealth exit from shaft DC 12 group Stealth check before emerging. Success: party gets a surprise round. The Berserker is focused on the door and Cultists are watching it.
Failure Cultists raise alarm immediately. Berserker turns. Roll initiative normally.

LIGHTING

Night (2nd hour after sunset) Four braziers at corners: bright light 20ft radius, dim light 40ft. The courtyard centre (cart area) is in dim light. The shaft area (SW) is at the edge of dim light from the SW brazier — not fully dark but close. Cultists have no special darkvision.

WHAT TO DRAW

Principia façade Roman stonework, 8ft high ground floor wall visible. Two iron-banded wooden doors (one partly splintered by the Berserker). Inscription over central bay: "LEG VI VIC AVG" cut into lintel stone.
Blood trail Leading from near the shaft opening westward to the barracks door — a legionary dragged themselves that far before collapsing. The body is not there (carried inside). Just the trail.
Fallen legionaries Three bodies in armour. Lorica segmentata, standard kit. One has a broken pilum beside him. They fought and lost quickly — no defensive formation, which means they were caught completely off-guard (Berserker came out of the shaft, not through the gate).
Shaft surrounds Stone ring, knee-height, with iron rungs driven into the sides. Rope still attached from when the original excavation crew tied off. The shaft is obviously recent work — mortar dust, fresh-cut edges.
Night sky Clear autumn sky, full of stars. The constellation Aries (sacred to Mars) is directly overhead if any PC thinks to look up. This is not coincidence.

AFTERMATH

Berserker search Has no pouch, no rations, no personal effects. No legion tattoo. Carries a hand-axe with MARTI scratched into the head in fresh cuts — the cult's mark. He is not from Vindolanda.
Cultists (alive) Frightened. Will bargain for their lives. Know: they were sent here two nights ago to wait near the shaft and "welcome the faithful when they return." They don't know what that means. They were told the vault would open at the correct hour. It did.
Level advancement Party reaches Level 4 after this encounter and a short rest. Mark milestone here — before entering the fort interior and triggering the Legate's inquiry.

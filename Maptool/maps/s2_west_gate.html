WEST GATE — PHASE STRUCTURE

ZoneLocationDraw / AddDM notes
1 Left Tower Scorpio ballista on the walkway (tripod mount, winch, bolt tray). Torches in iron sconces at parapet edge. Arrow slits facing exterior. 2 operators required. 2d10 piercing, range 120/480ft. Can target ram crew. Acrobatics DC 11 to reach via non-standard route when ladder is blocked.
2 Gate Breach Gate doors shown split and cracking. Battering ram (log with iron head, rope suspension frame) at the gap. Door debris visible on interior side. Gate frame still standing but splintered. Phase 2: Ram crews are non-combatant — they die fast to AoE or ballista. Without a crew the ram stops. Gate falls automatically at end of round 3. Phase 3: Vercingetorix walks through the gap — not charging.
3 Right Tower Same as left tower. Defenders with javelins (pila) along the battlements. One defender with a burning oil jar (improvised, +2d6 fire, throws it on ram). Oil jar: Athletics DC 12 to throw accurately at the ram area (exterior), DC 10 to just drop it on the gate mechanism. On hit: ram crew takes 2d6 fire and must spend an action extinguishing. Delays ram by 1 round.
4 Left Flank Interior Barrel barricade (5 barrels stacked 2 high). Varro's position if he's directing this side. Roman soldiers in formation behind it. Half cover behind barrels. Barrels can be kicked over as a free Shove — become Difficult Terrain. Varro fights here (Veteran stat block) if the party doesn't give him other direction.
5 Centre Interior Gate debris scattered across the ground after breach (Difficult Terrain in 20ft radius of gate gap). Stone water trough provides low cover. Open ground otherwise — the chokepoint where Phase 3 fight happens. Chokepoint: gate gap is 3sq (15ft) wide. Maximum 3 creatures abreast. Vercingetorix uses this to prevent being flanked — he stops here and will not advance further until addressed. He can be surrounded on 3 sides if the party moves fast.
6 Right Flank Interior Supply cart (three-quarter cover, solid wooden sides). Additional barrels stacked against the E tower wall. Hay bales that can be set alight (improvised torch, requires bonus action). Three-quarter cover (+5 AC, +5 Dex saves). Cart can be pushed (Athletics DC 14 as Action) to block the gate gap after Vercingetorix enters — effectively closing off the Germanic rearguard. Party advantage on this chokepoint if they do it.

VERCINGETORIX — PHASE 3 ENTRY

He walks. He does not run. His speed is 30ft per round, no bonus action dash. He moves from zone 2 toward zone 5. He stops at the centre (zone 5 edge) and speaks. He is not avoiding combat — he is demonstrating that he doesn't need to rush.
Do not start combat immediately Let him deliver the read-aloud. Let the party make a choice. A PC who attacks first (before he speaks) triggers immediate initiative — but one who waits gains the negotiation option. 10 seconds of table stillness after his read-aloud is correct pacing.
Gladiator stat block (CR 5) HP 112, AC 16. Multiattack ×3 (2 longsword + 1 shield bash). Parry reaction (+3 AC). If the party has 4 PCs at level 4: Medium difficulty vs him alone. Combine with rearguard Tribal Warriors if desired.
Retreat trigger If he reaches the principia doors without anyone talking to him: he stops, places one hand on the door, and pauses. This is the last window before he breaks it open. Persuasion DC 15 while he has his hand on the door still works. After the door opens: combat is effectively unavoidable unless the party prevents his path entirely.

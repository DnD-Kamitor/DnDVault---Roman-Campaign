DM REFERENCE — THE ELDER STAIR (S4 Scene 3, Room 3)

FeatureRuleNotes
Arch — north From Choking Hall Party enters here. 2 sq wide. Gas does not extend past this arch — clean air in this room.
Arch — south (very dark) Exit — stairs descend to antechamber This is where the party must reach. 14×14 room — the stair spiral occupies SE quadrant. The arch exit is at the base of the descent, south wall.
Dry Well (centre) Void — falls 40 ft (4d6 bludgeoning) Stone rim: DC 14 Acrobatics to avoid falling if pushed onto it. The well shaft drops to a sealed cistern — no exit at bottom. Contested Strength to pull back from rim. Visibility: 10 ft into shaft with light source, then darkness.
Stair treads (SE quadrant) Narrow stairs — single-file movement only Two creatures cannot pass each other on the stair arc. Movement rate halved on stairs (difficult terrain). Creatures pushed off the inner edge fall into the well. Stair direction: clockwise descent (NE to S).
Rockfall area (NE quadrant) Brutus' agent triggers rockfall Agent is hiding in NE area. Perception DC 14 (active, scanning before entering) to spot disturbed stone. Agent action: pulls a support pin — Dex save DC 15 or 2d8 bludgeoning. Spear slides toward well shaft on a failure: contested Strength check to hold it. Stone debris = difficult terrain in NE quadrant for remainder of scene.
Hanging chains (NE, near ceiling) Climbable — Str (Athletics) DC 12 Pre-Roman construction chains — rusted but load-bearing. Can be used for traversal across the well shaft (Acrobatics DC 14 to swing). If cut (Str DC 16 or slashing weapon): fall into the rockfall area below attachment point.
Spear near well shaft Contested Strength if sliding On rockfall failure: spear slides 1d4 × 5 ft toward well. Multiple characters can contest — combine their Str modifiers against DC 16. If spear falls into well: Mars DCs +4 in Scene 4. Recoverable with rope and 1 hour.

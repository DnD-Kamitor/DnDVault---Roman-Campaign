DM REFERENCE — THE SUNKEN ARMORY (S4 Scene 3, Room 1)

FeatureRuleNotes
Arch — north (lighter stone) Entry from principia hidden stair Pressure-plate trap 1 sq south of arch — Thieves' Tools DC 13 to disarm. Found on active floor scan (Perception DC 14) during scouting.
Arch — south (dark) Exit to Choking Hall Party cannot see beyond without active light source and investigation. Perception DC 11 (before spear-light fills room) to spot armor arrangement.
4 Pillars Total cover for Medium/Small creatures Animated Armors start in formation: 2 advance (south, between pillars and exit), 2 reserve (behind N pillars). Constructs — no Turn Undead. Arcana DC 14 passive: divine resonance from consecrated armor.
Corroded shield (centre-south floor) Investigation DC 10 to read Latin inscription: "Deus audit cor fortissimum." "The god hears the loudest heart." Clue for antechamber sacrifice decision.
Rubble piles (NW and SE) Difficult terrain (1 sq radius each) Half-cover. History DC 14 passive: armor 80–100 years older than the fort. This room was sealed when Vindolanda was built over it.

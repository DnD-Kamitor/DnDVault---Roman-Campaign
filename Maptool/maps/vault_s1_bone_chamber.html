ENCOUNTER: BONE CHAMBER

EnemyHP / ACStart PositionInitiative Trigger
Ghoul × 2 22 / AC 12 Inside rubble pile NE. Completely buried — no roll to spot them before combat. First PC reaches the room's midpoint (x-line) OR combat begins for any reason.
Ghast × 1 36 / AC 13 Behind the southernmost stone bench (W wall). Crouched, motionless — Perception DC 14 to notice. Same as Ghouls — simultaneous ambush. The stench is detectable 1 round before they surface (Con save DC 10 or disadvantage on all rolls until end of next turn).

TACTICS

RoundWhat happens
1 Ghouls erupt from rubble (difficult terrain in NE corner until cleared). Ghast stands from behind bench. All three move toward nearest warm-blooded PC. Stench already active.
2 Ghouls claw-claw-bite. Ghast uses Claws only (saving Bite for paralysed PC). Ghast targets spellcasters — it smells arcane residue.
3+ If a PC is paralysed: Ghast uses Bite on them exclusively. Ghouls mob the same target. This can be lethal at L3 — watch the table's engagement level.

DIFFICULTY DIAL

AdjustmentEffect
Remove the Ghast Drops from Deadly to Hard. Do this for 3-player parties or if S1 vault has already cost significant HP/spell slots.
Reduce Ghouls to 1 Drops to Medium. Keep Ghast for tension but lose one Ghoul entirely.
Bonus: clay vessel Under the middle bench: unguentum vitale (potion of healing, 2d4+2). A fighter-type PC who specifically looks under the benches finds it with no check.

WHAT TO DRAW

Rubble pile (NE) Broken ceiling slabs, sharp-edged, angled outward from corner. Faint claw marks on the wall above it — they came OUT this way, but long ago. Difficult terrain for the 2×2sq NE block.
Stone benches (W) Heavy Roman military-style benches, identical to those in barracks — someone brought furniture down here. Worn flat on top. Under middle bench: clay vessel with stopper, almost invisible.
Ceiling Draw ceiling lines close — low headroom. One large crack running NW–SE near the NE corner (the collapse source). Dust and loose pebbles fall if anything large hits the E wall.
Strike marks (S wall) Cluster of impact marks at waist height, near the S exit. Mixed — some Roman (pilum tip oval) and some claw. Someone died holding this doorway.

POST-COMBAT

Investigation DC 12 The Ghouls' clothing is Roman military issue — linen tunic, iron fibula with "LEG VI VIC" stamp. They were Legionaries. Recently.
Religion DC 13 The clay vessel under the bench is a funerary deposit (lekythos) — but placed there by someone alive, recently. Someone was tending these dead. Upward: Sextus or a Cultist.

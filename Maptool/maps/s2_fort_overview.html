2 West Gate — BREACH Battering ram (log with iron head, rope pulls) visible through the gate gap. Gate frame cracked and splintering. Torch on each gate tower. Fight concentrated at the chokepoint. Phase 2: Ram breaks in 3 rounds. Phase 3: Vercingetorix walks through. See s2_west_gate.html for the full battle map.
3 Principia (HQ) Double doors barred from inside. Two Praetorian Guards visible through the window (torch backlight). The spear's glow faintly visible through a shuttered window. Tribune + 6 Praetorians are here — not on the walls. That's Clue 2 for the Three Clue Rule. They're protecting the spear, not the fort.
4 East Wall Raiders approach but do not scale — feinting only (torches visible, no ladders). Two defenders standing watch, no engagement. Germanic feint from the east. Purpose is to split defenders' attention. No serious combat here.
5 South Gate Gate closed but unlocked. Vicus visible 200ft south. No raiders from this direction — Rome road. Escape route if party takes the "flee with Vercingetorix" ending. Also where the Tribune's horses are stabled (useful for Scene 4 follow-up).
6 NW Corner Tower Ballista (scorpio) mounted on the tower platform. Requires two operators. 2d10 piercing. Acrobatics DC 11 to reach via non-standard route when main path is blocked. Can target Phase 1 ladders or Phase 2 ram operators.

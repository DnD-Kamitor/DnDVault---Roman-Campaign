DM REFERENCE — THE CHOKING HALL (S4 Scene 3, Room 2)

FeatureRuleNotes
4 Gas Vents Con DC 14 each round in room Gas denser near floor (Nature DC 13 from prep reveals this). Air pockets near ceiling: Stealth DC 13 (moving slow + high) gives advantage on Con saves. Cassia can clear with one spell slot (any abjuration/evocation).
4 Alcoves Shallow cover (half cover vs. ranged) Not safer from gas — same altitude as corridor floor. Useful for positioning only. Left alcoves at rows 3 and 8; right alcoves at rows 5 and 10.
Ceiling bands (darker wall edges) Visual reminder — gas collects low No mechanical effect on its own. Characters who duck/crawl (prone): disadvantage on Con saves but +1 to all movement saves from gas. DM discretion.
Medicine DC 13 (bonus action) Stabilise downed ally without spell slot Standard field triage. Works even in gas (the medic takes the Con save themselves).
Rubble at south end Difficult terrain (2 sq width) Partial ceiling collapse — suggests instability above. Partial cover. Does not block the arch to Elder Stair.
Arch — north (lit) From Sunken Armory Party enters here. Gas already present — start Con saves from round 1 unless prepped.
Arch — south (dark) To Elder Stair Visible as darker opening. Gas thins past the arch — first sq beyond is safe. Incentive to keep moving.

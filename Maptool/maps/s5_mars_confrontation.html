MARS ENCOUNTER STAGES

StageWhat Happens
Round 1-2Mars is a presence only. Radiating cracks pulse (DC 12 Dex or 1d6 necrotic when crossing). Wraiths x2 attack from outer ring shadows.
Round 3Mars fully manifests on dais. Empyrean (adapted): AC 22, HP 260, Multiattack 3 (Maul of Mars +14, 6d6+10 bludgeoning). Corruption field: 60-ft aura, creatures start turn DC 16 Wis or gain 1 corruption.
Option ADestroy contract (AC 12, 20 HP, immune to fire and piercing): Mars weakened. HP 130, loses Legendary Resistance. Party wins by surviving 5 rounds after weakness.
Option BStrike Mars 3 times with weapons consecrated at a non-Mars shrine (Belatucadrus shrine Dc 15 Religion to identify): each strike deals 1d10 radiant extra. 5 such strikes = Mars banished.
Option CReach Corruption Level 5 and embrace the Pact: character becomes Mars-touched. Mars withdraws satisfied. Party achieves a pyrrhic victory. Corruption arc continues into epilogue.

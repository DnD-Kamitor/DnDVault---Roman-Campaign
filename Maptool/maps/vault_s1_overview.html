ROOM REFERENCE

№RoomWhat to draw on topSecrets / DM only
1 Shaft Rope ladder descending centre. Pick marks on clay walls, more careful toward the bottom. 40 ft deep. Torch flame leans downward in draft.
2 Entry Passage Roman pick on floor pointing back to shaft. Fine rubble at wall bases. NOLITE scratched knee-height on left wall — only visible with light held low. Two construction eras: Roman stone over older Germanic stone.
3 T-Junction Passage bifurcates. Torch flame drifts right (cold air from water). Left = Shield Hall (warm, dry). Right = Flooded Gallery (cold, wet).
4 Shield Hall Shields covering every wall floor to ceiling. Mix of tribal symbols. Stone bench left side near entrance. One shield hung backwards on W wall = hidden alcove behind it (Inv DC 15): wax tablet + bone token + stylus. Sicus (wounded soldier) behind the bench.
5 Flooded Gallery Stone ledge on one wall (visible above waterline). Far wall visible beyond the water. 4 × Shadows in the water (Stealth DC 14 to pass). Germanic warrior seated against far wall — 3 centuries preserved, not undead.
6 Bone Chamber Collapsed ceiling rubble NE corner. Three stone benches W wall. Weapon strike marks clustered on S wall at waist height. 2 Ghouls (from rubble) + 1 Ghast (behind benches). DEADLY. Clay vessel under middle bench = potion of healing.
7 Binding Chamber Low ceiling. Chains hanging in rows from ceiling — thick and thin, hooks at ends. Puzzle room. TIWAZ (ᛏ) carved in E wall. No combat. Chain sequence unlocks scroll alcove.
8 Runic Corridor Entire floor covered in carved rune symbols edge to edge (the darker floor texture marks this). Add individual rune symbols when drawing over it. Wrong rune = 1d8 lightning. Safe = OTHALAN (ᛟ), rightmost per row. No combat.
9 Altar Chamber 7 standing stones in a ring. Granite altar at centre. Iron spear on altar. Slack chains at altar base. Lever in wall right of archway. Wight (behind altar) + Cult Fanatic (near entry) + 2 × Shadows (between stones). DEADLY. Exit condition: back away from spear or Persuasion DC 14 with scroll.

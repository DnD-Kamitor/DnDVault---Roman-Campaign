№RoomDraw / AddNotes
1 Shaft Rope ladder descending center. Clay walls with pick tool marks (more careful deeper = workers felt something wrong) 40 ft deep. Torch flame leans downward in draft
2 Entry Passage Roman pick lying on floor pointing back toward shaft. NOLITE scratched on left wall at knee height with tally marks (VII + diagonal) Passage narrows. Air smell: old metal + cedar. Two construction eras visible in wall (Roman stone on top, Germanic stone below)
3 T-Junction Fork marker. Left: warmer air (arrow). Right: colder air, sound of water (arrow). Torch flame drifts right Branch A = Shield Hall (dry). Branch B = Flooded Gallery (water)
4 Shield Hall Shields covering every wall edge-to-edge, floor to ceiling. Dozens. Mix of shapes and tribal symbols (wolf, boar, serpent, three-fingered hand). One shield on W wall hung BACKWARDS (marks hidden alcove). Stone bench near entrance, left side — NPC lying behind it No combat. ★ = Hidden Alcove behind backwards shield. Sicus (wounded soldier, orange S marker) hides behind bench
★ Hidden Alcove Narrow crack in stone behind backwards shield. Inside: wax tablet (cracked, old), carved bone tile (OTHALAN rune), bronze stylus Inv DC 15 to find. Tablet is the first piece of the contract clue chain
5 Flooded Gallery Water fills the middle 60% of room (chest-deep). Stone ledge on E wall (stays at shoulder depth if hugged). Germanic warrior sitting against far S wall, submerged to chest — arms crossed, spear across lap, bone token at throat. Small framea visible above waterline 4 × Shadows in water. Stealth DC 14 group check to pass. Warrior is 3 centuries preserved, not undead — do not make him look monstrous
6 Bone Chamber Collapsed ceiling rubble fills NE corner (jagged pile, not smooth). Three stone benches along W wall (two creatures hiding behind). Clay vessel under middle bench. Wall impact marks (cluster of weapon strikes) on S wall at waist height 2 Ghouls + 1 Ghast. DEADLY at L3. Ghouls emerge from rubble. Ghast behind stone benches. Creatures dormant until party fully in room
7 Binding Chamber Ceiling very low (draw ceiling lines close to head height). Chains hanging from ceiling in rows — thick and thin mixed, hooks and collars at ends. TIWAZ rune carved deep in E wall. One chain (center) runs through a floor ring with iron grip handle. Small hidden alcove (right wall, stone-plug door) Puzzle room. Chain sequence = oldest to newest then center. Alcove contains scroll (contract). No combat
8 Runic Corridor Entire floor covered in carved runes — edge to edge, wall to wall. 8 rows × 5 columns of individual rune symbols, each glowing cold blue-white. The OTHALAN rune (ᛟ) is in every row. No bare floor visible anywhere Wrong rune = 1d8 lightning. Safe rune = OTHALAN (rightmost in each row). No combat
9 Altar Chamber 7 standing stones in a ring (twice head-height, carved spiraling runes that seem to shift). Granite altar at center, older than Roman stonework. Iron spear on altar (8 ft long, black iron, ember-red runes). Chains at altar base, lying slack. Lever set into wall right of the archway entrance. Floor slopes gently toward altar 1 Wight + 1 Cult Fanatic + 2 Shadows. DEADLY. Wight (W marker) starts behind altar. Fanatic already in room when party arrives. Lever seals corridor exit as difficult terrain

W (purple) Wight — guardian of the altar. Does not attack immediately. Persuasion DC 14 (advantage with contract scroll) for communication.

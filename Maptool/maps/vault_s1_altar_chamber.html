ENCOUNTER: ALTAR CHAMBER

EnemyHP / ACStartBehaviour
Cult Fanatic 33 / AC 13 N end of room, near entry. Waiting. Already in prayer. Does not attack immediately. Speaks: "You should not be here. Leave the spear. Leave now." — buys the Wight one free round to emerge.
Wight 45 / AC 14 Behind altar, crouched. Rises on round 1. Speaks in accented Latin: "The spear does not belong to your century." Persuasion DC 14 to negotiate — advantage if party holds the contract scroll. If combat: targets whoever touched/approaches the spear first.
Shadow ×2 16 / AC 12 Between stones NW and SE (light cannot reach them there). Do not act until Wight takes damage OR Fanatic drops. Then move through the stones toward party's rear (Stealth +4, Darkness if within 5ft of a stone).

EXIT CONDITIONS (non-lethal resolution)

Back away from spear If the party retreats 20ft from the altar and shows no hostile intent: Wight holds. Fanatic collapses in relief. Party can leave. The Wight will not pursue out of the chamber.
Persuasion DC 14 With contract scroll: Wight explains the spear is bound to the land, not to Mars. Will stand aside and seal the chamber behind the party. Gives them the bone token (second OTHALAN piece).
Lever (right of entry) Seals the Runic Corridor behind the party as Difficult Terrain — chain-link gate. Activates only from inside the chamber. Reversible. Wight uses it on round 3 if combat is going badly for it.

SPEAR MECHANICS

Touch (no attuned) Corruption +1. Wisdom save DC 13 or stunned until end of next turn (visions of a battlefield).
Pick up Corruption +2. Immediately pulls out of grasp (as if pulled by unseen force) unless Strength check DC 15 succeeds.
Remove from vault All undead in vault become permanently hostile. Wight rises again in 24h. This is the BAD ending for Session 1.

WHAT TO DRAW

Standing stones Twice head-height. Ancient, pre-Roman. Carved rune spirals that seem to shift when you look away. Stone is darker than the floor — brought from elsewhere. No mortar; just set in the floor's stone. Half-cover if crouching behind one.
Altar stone Grey-black granite, older than everything else in the vault. Roman letters have been carved into its base at some point: "MARTI INVICTO" — but under that, far older Germanic runes. The spear sits in a groove cut into the top surface exactly sized for it.
Floor slope Subtle but real — concentric rings worn into the stone show generations of something flowing toward the altar. Old blood channels. Not currently active.
Chains at altar base Six heavy iron chains, lying slack on the floor in a rough ring around the altar base. Hooks but nothing attached. They were used to bind something (or someone) here. Recently enough that the iron still smells of oil.
Ceiling High — almost out of torchlight range. At the center apex, directly above the altar, a hole the size of a fist: a chimney shaft going up through bedrock. Cold air descends from it continuously.

LIGHTING

The spear on the altar is the only light source — dim red glow, 10ft radius. The standing stones block light (treat as walls for Shadows). Without the spear's light: complete darkness past 10ft. Darkvision still works but appears red-tinted. Shadows have advantage in darkness.

# Ursula Günther

**Race:** Half-orc
**Class:** Barbarian (Path of the Berserker) — Level 3
**Background:** Folk Hero
**Role:** Foederatus
**Citizenship:** Peregrinus

---

## Background

Ursula is the daughter of the legendary Marcomanni clan leader — also named Ursula Günther.

Her mother led the Marcomanni through hundreds of raids into Roman territory. She was never defeated in open combat. The Romans, unable to beat her in the field, resorted to treachery: she was tricked, captured, and shipped to Rome. There she was sentenced to die in the Colosseum.

They sent her in with a blunted sword and no armor.

She killed a lion. A tiger. Five gladiators. A pack of wolves. An elephant.

A rhinoceros finally ended her.

The crowd cheered. Rome called it justice.

---

## Motivation

Ursula joined the Roman legions deliberately — to destroy them from within.

Her goal is not revenge for its own sake. It is specific: Rome leaves her homeland, or she dies ensuring it costs them more than it cost her mother. She has no interest in Roman politics, senatorial intrigue, or the empire's distant wars. The frontier is everything. Her people will be free of Rome, or she will not live to see them fail.

---

## The Mask

Around Romans, Ursula performs stupidity.

She speaks Latin with only one- and two-syllable words. She smiles at orders. She acts subservient, slow, harmless. Romans talk freely in front of her — about plans, about orders, about each other — because they believe she does not understand.

She understands everything.

She has been listening for years.

---

## The Conflict

In the legions she has come to genuinely respect some of her fellow soldiers. A few of them she may even like.

This creates real internal conflict. She resolves it by separating the institution from the people inside it: her war is with Rome as a structure, not with individual soldiers. She would not forgive herself if her actions got her friends killed.

She reminds herself of this regularly. Some days it is easier than others.

---

## The Marcomanni Connection

The Marcomanni war is happening now — 175 AD. Her people are fighting Rome on the Danube at this moment, being enslaved, having their children marched south to Roman arenas.

Ursula grew up hearing the old Germanic dialect. She reads Elder Futhark without a check. She recognizes tribal symbols on sight: boar is Marcomanni, three-fingered hand is Cherusci, serpent is Suebi.

She is the only person in her unit who understands what Rome has done — and is still doing — to the peoples on the other side of the frontier.

---

## Key Facts for the DM

- Speaks broken Latin (max 2-syllable words) in front of Romans — fully deliberate
- Understands all Latin spoken around her
- Reads and understands old Germanic dialects (including pre-Roman Belgic Germanic)
- Knows her tribe's history, the tribal symbols, and the cost of Roman expansion personally
- Will not betray her friends but will not abandon her mission
- Feels she can never match her mother's legend — and is not sure she wants to

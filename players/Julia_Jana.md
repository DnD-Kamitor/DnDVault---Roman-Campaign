# Julia Jana

**Race:** Aasimar (Radiant Soul)
**Class:** Cleric (Twilight Domain) — Level 3
**Background:** Noble
**Role:** Optio
**Citizenship:** Civis Romana

---

## Background

Julia is the last of the Gens Jana — a patrician family that traces its divine lineage to Janus, god of thresholds, transitions, and beginnings. The name carries weight in Rome. It also carries expectation.

Her father is Senator Gaius Janus. He has one plan for Julia: first female quaestor, then senator, then whatever comes after that. The Jana name ends with her if she fails. He has made this clear since she could read.

He arranged her posting to Vindolanda himself.

---

## Why Her Father Sent Her North

Senator Gaius Janus did not send his daughter to the frontier as punishment. He sent her because the frontier is where reputations are made when you cannot win wars in the Senate chamber. A campaign on Hadrian's Wall — even a minor one, even attached to a vexillatio of expendables — becomes a line on a record. *Service in Britannia. Decorated.* That line opens doors in Rome that no amount of family name can open alone.

He arranged three servants to accompany her: Primus, Secondus, and Tertius. They are not there to carry her luggage. They are there to make sure she survives long enough to come home useful.

What he did not arrange: that the posting would involve a sealed vault, a divine weapon, a Legate with private ambitions, and a spear that remembers every name it hears.

Whether he knows any of this is a question worth asking eventually.

---

## Julia's Own Reasons

She chose the Twilight Domain because Janus governs transitions — and twilight is a threshold. Dawn and dusk, the moments between states. Her divine calling and her bloodline point at the same thing: she exists at edges, between what was and what comes next.

She did not choose to be the last of her name. She did not choose her father's plan. She chose this posting when she could have chosen an easier one.

She is not entirely sure why.

---

## Key Facts for the DM

- Father: Senator Gaius Janus — well-connected, politically calculating, not to be underestimated
- Three servants (Primus, Secondus, Tertius) hired and placed by the father, not by Julia
- Speaks Latin, Greek, Germanic, and the Sacred Language of the Janus Cult
- Aasimar: something divine is in her bloodline. The Twilight Domain means she is drawn to thresholds — the vault, the spear, and everything in it is exactly her territory
- She is building toward a political career. Everything she does here goes on a record
- Her father sent her. She has not yet decided if she resents that or not

Full Translation

"WHEN THE RED CHIEF COMES, THE GATE WILL OPEN."

What the OTHALAN Rune Means

  • OTHALAN (ᛟ) = ancestral land, inheritance, homeland. In Germanic tradition: what is owed to the clan by blood.
  • In this context: a CONTRACT rune. Something has been promised. This inscription is the seal of that promise.
  • Sextus recognised the mark (see cipher handout). He had seen it before — on a document from Rome.
  • The vault predates the fort by at least 200 years. Someone planned for this inscription to be found.

Who is "The Red Chief"?

  • Vercingetorix is red-haired. This was known to whoever carved the runes.
  • The inscription is prophetic OR it was placed here after Vercingetorix's identity was chosen — by Senator Brutus's agents, who arranged the alliance with the Germanic tribes.
  • Implication: someone opened the gate from the inside. Quintus Flavius (Quartermaster, sleeper agent) had gate access.
  • The party cannot prove this yet. It becomes provable in Chapter 3 when they reach the Germanic village.

Aldric's Reaction (if consulted)

Aldric stares at the tablet. His jaw tightens. "This is a blood-oath rune. My grandfather carved OTHALAN above his door so the gods would know what he owned. This is the same mark, but inverted." He touches the stone and draws his hand back. "It is a gate rune. A passage. Whatever was bound here — it was agreed to." Persuasion DC 14 to get him to name what he thinks it means. On a success: "Someone promised the tribes passage through the fort. In exchange for something. I do not know what."

When to Give This Handout

When the party re-enters the vault after the raid (or during the post-battle investigation scene). The inscription was always there — it was visible in Chapter 1 but unreadable without a Religion check. Now they have context to understand why it matters.

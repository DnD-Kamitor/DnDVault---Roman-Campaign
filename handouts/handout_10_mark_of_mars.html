Print four copies — one per player — give at campaign end
The Mark of Mars
This character has stood before a god of war and lived.
  • Advantage on all Intimidation checks. Other soldiers see something in you they cannot name.
  • Once per long rest: ask the DM "What does Mars want from this situation?" and receive an honest answer.

In exchange: Mars may call on you. Once. At his choosing. You will know when it comes.

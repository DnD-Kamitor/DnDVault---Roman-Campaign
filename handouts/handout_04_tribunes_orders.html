Mandatum Senatus
Auctoritas Extraordinaria — Anno DCCCCXXVIII AUC
Omnibus militibus, centurionibus et praefectis Romani Imperii:

Sciatis Lucium Valerium Maximum, Tribunum Militum Legionis II Augustae, auctoritate plena Senatus Romani armatum esse.

Ei potestas datur custodiendi, transferendi et disponendi rem inventam in Vindolanda — sive arma, sive vasa, sive quodcumque ibi in ruinis subterraneis repertum est. Haec res Romam sine mora transferatur.

Nemo hanc rem a Tribuno sine mandato scripto Senatus Romani removeat aut retineat. Qui huic mandato non pareat, pro hoste Populi Romani habeatur et iudicetur.
To all soldiers, centurions, and commanders of the Roman Empire:

Know that Lucius Valerius Maximus, Military Tribune of the Second Legion Augusta, is armed with the full authority of the Roman Senate.

He is granted power to take custody of, transport, and dispose of the object discovered at Vindolanda — whether weapons, vessels, or whatever else was found in the subterranean ruins. This object is to be transferred to Rome without delay.

No one shall remove or retain this object from the Tribune without written mandate of the Roman Senate. He who does not obey this order shall be judged an enemy of the Roman People.
Datum ex Curia Romana
Idibus Octobribus
Anno DCCCCXXVIII AUC
L. Valerius Maximus, Tribunus

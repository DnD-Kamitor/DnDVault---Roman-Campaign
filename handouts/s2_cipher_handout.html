Decoded Message

SEXTUS HAS SEEN THE MARK. HE SPOKE WITH THE LEGATE. YOU HAVE UNTIL THE KALENDS OF MARS. DO NOT FAIL YOUR PATRON. THE ARTIFACT STAYS BURIED. LEAVE NO WITNESS. YOUR DEBT IS PAID IN FULL UPON COMPLETION. TELL NO ONE. BURN THIS. — THE PATRON

Cipher Key

Caesar cipher, shift +3. The broken wax seal impression on the letter shows the Roman numeral III — this is the shift key. Investigation DC 12 to notice the seal fragment is a deliberate marking, not decoration.

What This Reveals

  • Quintus Flavius (Quartermaster) is a sleeper agent for "The Patron" — Senator Brutus's network.
  • Sextus (the dead man from Scene 0) discovered "the mark" — the OTHALAN contract rune — and reported it to the Legate. Quintus received orders to silence him.
  • "The artifact stays buried" confirms someone knows about the vault under the fort (Chapter 1). The Patron knew about it before the party descended.
  • "The Patron" = Senator Brutus. First concrete written link between the senator and the fort. The party cannot prove it yet (no name, only title).
  • The timing: Kalends of Mars has passed, meaning Quintus completed his mission — but he was never meant to survive the raid to explain it.

When to Give This Handout

After the party searches Quintus's personal trunk. Trunk: Investigation DC 14 to find (or automatic if they arrested and searched him). The false-bottomed compartment inside: Perception DC 15 to notice. If the compartment is simply forced open without noticing it, the letter is crushed and partially destroyed — lose the last two lines.

Quintus's Reaction if Confronted

He denies authorship, claims he found it, claims it was planted. Insight DC 14 reveals genuine fear — he is more afraid of "The Patron" than of the party. He will not name Brutus under interrogation unless the party can credibly guarantee protection from Rome — Persuasion DC 18 (or DC 12 if they can prove they already know Brutus is involved).

Follow-on Thread

If the party keeps Quintus alive and protected, he becomes a reluctant informant for Chapter 3. If executed or if they fail to protect him, the thread goes cold until Chapter 4 (Senator Brutus's first physical appearance).

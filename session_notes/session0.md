# Session 0 Notes

## Player Backgrounds (established at table)

### Tess — Rock Gnome Artificer (player: Tess54607)
Comes from a city that was attacked while unprepared. The trauma left her with a compulsive need to repair things — she cannot leave broken equipment alone. In camp she is always fixing something: a buckle, a cracked shaft, a loose rivet. It is not helpful efficiency; it is anxiety wearing the mask of productivity. She cannot stop because stopping means thinking about what she could not fix in time.

### Iris — Shadar-kai Druid (player: Gyto)
Spartan heritage with a Carthaginian origin. Her adopted family is Carthaginian. She carries two worlds inside her: the Spartan discipline and austerity she was raised in, and the Carthaginian mercantile culture of her adopted kin. The shadow-marked Shadar-kai nature sits on top of both — a third inheritance she did not choose.

### Tanit bint Abdmelqart — Halfling Bard (player: Tanit)
Carthaginian heritage. Her family is in the purple dye trade (Tyrian purple — the most valuable commodity in the ancient world, a Phoenician monopoly). She carries the memory of a civilization Rome destroyed. Her name is Carthaginian. Her family still profits from a Roman empire that erased her people's history. The tension between survival and identity is the engine of her character.

### Julia Jana — Aasimar Cleric (player: Julia)
Roman noble of the Gens Jana, lineage traced to the god Janus. Her father is a senator. She is the last Jana. She joined the army to build a career — one campaign, then back to Rome for a political appointment, eventually the first female quaestor and senator. She has three servants (Primus, Secondus, Tertius) hired by her father. Twilight domain chosen because Janus governs transitions and thresholds — twilight is a threshold. Alignment LN.

## Contubernium Dynamics (noted at table)

- Tess and Tanit both have Carthaginian-adjacent backgrounds — potential bond
- Iris (Spartan + Carthaginian) bridges military discipline and mercantile culture
- Julia is the Roman establishment; the others are from peoples Rome conquered or destroyed
- This unit contains the full spectrum of Roman imperial tension: conqueror and conquered, serving the same legion

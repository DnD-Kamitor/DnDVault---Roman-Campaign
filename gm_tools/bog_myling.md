# The Myling — Farbog Encounter

## What It Is

A Myling is the spirit of someone who died unmourned and unburied, doomed to wander until
a living person carries them to consecrated ground. This one is small — a teenager, maybe
younger. One of the cult's test victims: a boy ordered into the bog water who was too young
to refuse and too young to survive. His body is still in the marsh. His spirit has been
calling since the night he drowned.

He does not want to kill anyone. He wants to go home.

---

## How It Starts

**DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** as the party crosses the causeway: a sound in the bog, low
and wet, like a child crying with water in their lungs. It's coming from the left side of
the causeway, about thirty feet out.

**If ignored:** the Myling pulls itself onto the causeway behind the last party member.
**If approached:** it surfaces from the water — grey, waterlogged, eyes open but not
quite focused. It reaches up.

**Read aloud (if approached):**
> *It is a boy. Twelve, maybe thirteen. His skin is the colour of the bog and his clothes
> are Germanic work, months rotted. He is not aggressive. He is holding his arms up the
> way a small child holds their arms up to be carried. He says one word, over and over,
> in Germanic. The same word.*

The word is **"home."**

---

## The Mechanic — The Burden

If a PC lets the Myling climb onto their back (or the Myling successfully grapples, DC 13
Strength contested):

- The PC's **speed is halved**
- Each round carrying him, **DC 10 + rounds carried** Strength check or speed drops to 0
  (the Myling grows heavier the further from the bog he gets)
- **He cannot be shaken off** by force alone — attacking him causes him to shriek and
  become hostile (see below)
- The only way to set him down willingly is to reach holy ground

**Holy ground options:**
- The **Belatucadrus shrine** in the sacred grove (north of village)
- Any **consecrated space** created by the party's Cleric or Druid (10 min ritual)
- The **village burial mound** if one exists (DC 13 Investigation to find it)

**When he reaches holy ground:**
> *He slides down from your back and stands in the shrine's shadow for a moment, very
> still. Then he looks at whoever carried him — really looks, for the first time. He
> says something in Germanic.*
> *"It doesn't hurt here."*
> *He sits down, closes his eyes, and is gone. Not dramatically. Just gone.*

**Reward:** the PC who carried him gains the **Myling's Rest** blessing for the session:
advantage on death saving throws and immunity to the Frightened condition.

---

## If the Party Attacks or Ignores Him Completely

He becomes hostile. Treat as a **Specter** (MM p.279, CR 1, AC 12, HP 22):
- Incorporeal Movement
- Life Drain: +4 to hit, 2d6 necrotic, DC 13 Con save or max HP reduced
- Sunlight Sensitivity (daytime — disadvantage on attacks)

He shrieks when hostile — **DC 13 Wisdom save or Frightened for 1 minute.** The shriek
may also wake the Wights if they haven't risen yet (DM call — use it if the party is
breezing through).

---

## DM Note

This encounter is entirely optional and entirely about tone. A party that stops to help a
drowned child in a cursed bog is a party that will feel the weight of what the cult did
before they ever reach the dungeon door. Do not force it. If the party doesn't notice or
doesn't engage, let it go. If they do — let it breathe.

Session 4: The God's Demand

GM Reference Card — All content DM-only unless marked — Imperial Stakes — Level 5 → 6 — Siege Day
Beat 1 — Three Simultaneous Crises

Alarm horns at the north gate. At the same moment: smoke rising from the granary district. At the same moment: a shout from the direction of the shrine. Three things. One party. No time to split evenly.

"You hear the horn first. Then the smoke catches the corner of your eye. Then someone is running from the shrine direction, shouting your name."

THE MOMENT — Triage

Commit immediately. The players must decide in the first thirty seconds of play which threat each character addresses. Do not let them deliberate at length — the siege doesn't pause.

The signal to read (Perception DC 12, active): The smoke plume from the granary runs wrong — northwest, against the prevailing east wind. Set from inside, not from a fire outside the wall. The north gate assault is the feint. The granary fire is misdirection. The shrine theft is the primary objective.

Shrine insider: A figure at the shrine avoids the threshold rune Paterculus placed deliberately. Insight DC 14 (active, watching the shrine specifically) — someone inside the fort briefed him. This is Brutus's agent. If caught alive, he's an interrogation asset for Scene 1.

Beat 3 — The Dust Settles

Gate held or breached (clocks affected). Granary saved or partially burned (Food Clock). Shrine intruder caught, fled, or subdued. What the party controlled and what slipped past them shapes every subsequent scene. Note what each clock is at before Scene 1.

Five Senses — Cold Open

Sight
Dawn smoke from the granary — pale grey, running the wrong direction. The north gate ram crew visible from the wall. The shrine figure moving with purpose, not panic.
Sound
Alarm horn, once. Then the rhythmic thud of a battering ram — slower than expected, methodical. Civilians screaming in the vicus. The shrine: a crash of overturned votive stands.
Smell
Smoke — grain smoke has a sweet, heavy quality distinct from wood. Iron, dust, the smell of a fort that hasn't slept in two days.
Feel
The vibration of the ram through the stone underfoot. The gate trembles. Not breached yet — but the rhythm tells a trained soldier that the crew knows what they're doing.
Taste
Grain smoke at the back of the throat, sweet and choking. The taste of a fort under siege: everything rationed, including air.

Beat 1 — The Survivors Convene

The principia audience chamber. Corvinus is already seated at the head of the table — he arrived first, by design. Lucius stands near the door. Varro is against the wall, not seated. Cassia is in the augur's corner. The room holds every surviving leader and mutual suspicion between each of them.

The pressure: The ram is still working at the north gate. Smoke is visible through the slit window. This council has maybe thirty minutes before the siege demands physical decisions, not verbal ones.

THE MOMENT — Cassia's Augury

Cassia speaks before anyone else can control the agenda. She slept after the augury. She was waiting for this moment.

"I saw three figures kneeling before an altar. A spear, levitating. I heard the ram. Then I heard nothing — the ram, the horns, the shouts. All of it stopped. Not because the siege ended. Because something larger than the siege had spoken."

Long silence. Then Corvinus: "What does that mean, augur?" — He's asking because he needs to know, not because he believes. That distinction matters.

This is when the council's decision tree opens. Three paths emerge from this moment. The party's next actions — who they address first, what they reveal, whether they use Insight on Corvinus — determine which path becomes viable.

Beat 3 — The Path Is Chosen

One of three escort paths is committed to before Scene 2. Note which — it determines escort composition, tunnel dynamics, and Session 5 opening conditions. All three paths reach the antechamber. The difference is who's in the room when they get there.

Five Senses — Scene 1

Sight
Corvinus reorganizing objects on the table as Cassia speaks — a cup moved two inches. Lucius: completely still. Varro: eyes on the party, not the speaker. The slit window shows smoke still drifting.
Sound
The distant, rhythmic thud of the ram — always in the background. Voices in the principia measured against it. When Cassia finishes, the ram hits three times in the silence.
Smell
Smoke from the granary district. Candle wax — the principia's working candles burning low after a siege night. Varro: sweat and iron.
Feel
The vibration of the ram reaches even here. The table trembles slightly on each strike. Everyone feels it. Nobody comments on it.
Taste
Nothing. The room has been breathing the same recycled smoke air since the siege began. It coats everything.

Beat 1 — The Preparation Window

Three preparation actions available before the procession must move. Time is not infinite — the ram is still working. Each preparation changes what the tunnels cost or what the antechamber offers. Choose wisely.

Handout 9 distributed here — the sealed siege orders with the escort route. Players can cross-reference with the tunnel map from Varro's office.

THE MOMENT — The Stair Opens

The hidden stair in the principia, exactly where the tunnel map said it would be. It opens downward. The air that comes up is cold and old — not the cold of a cellar, the cold of something that has been sealed for a long time.

History DC 14 (passive): pre-Hadrianic Roman military engineering. This is not Vindolanda's basement. This is what Vindolanda was built on top of. They are going somewhere older than this fort.

"Cassia lights a lamp. The flame bends toward the stairwell, not away. Something below is pulling the air. Something is breathing."

Beat 3 — Into the Tunnel

Preparations locked in. Escort assembled by the chosen council path. The clocks are where they are. The stair is open. Down.

Five Senses — Scene 2

Sight
The hidden stair: stone, dry, old — each step worn in the center, not by the current fort's traffic but by something before. Cassia's lamp flame bending toward it.
Sound
Above: the ram, always. From below: silence — the total silence of sealed spaces, more complete than any natural silence. It absorbs sound from the stairwell edge.
Smell
Old stone. Mineral. The faint smell of marsh gas — faint here, concentrated below. Cassia's incense from the blessing rite, still clinging to the group.
Feel
Cold air rising from below, notably colder than the siege-warm fort above. The transition is sharp — five degrees in three steps. The stair stones are bone dry despite the cold.
Taste
The beginning of the mineral taste that will intensify in the Choking Hall. Barely perceptible here. The body is already registering.

Beat 1 — Sunken Armory

The first tunnel chamber opens into an armory — armor in full combat formation, not scattered. Perception DC 11: they are standing at attention, not fallen. Varro: "Romans don't leave armor like that. They fall in armor. Or they're ordered to stand." — He's right. They were left like this deliberately.

Arcana DC 14 (passive): divine resonance, not undead. No undead vulnerabilities. These are constructs in Mars' service. Hesitation draws their attention; decisive movement does not.

History DC 14 (passive): pre-Hadrianic armor, 80–100 years older than Vindolanda. The chamber was sealed when the current fort was built on top of it.

THE MOMENT — Choking Hall

The gas vents. One character goes down first — whoever is smallest or has the worst Constitution. They simply stop and sit, unable to coordinate their limbs. Their eyes are open.

The choice: Continue with a downed character, or stop in a poisoned hall to treat them. Neither answer is obviously correct. The antechamber is ahead. The siege is above.

Con saves DC 14 each round. If gas type was mapped in Scene 2 (Nature DC 13 passive): halve the required saves. Air pockets findable with Stealth DC 13 (moving slowly, locating by temperature differential).

Medicine DC 13 to stabilize a downed character without spell slot. Patient continues after one round of treatment. Without Medicine: the downed character takes 1d4 per round from the gas.

Beat 3 — Elder Stair / Brutus's Agent

The stair rises toward the antechamber. Midway: rockfall, deliberately triggered. Brutus's agent — one of the escort soldiers, if the party didn't catch the insider before scene start — pulls a support stone.

Dexterity save DC 15 (or Acrobatics DC 13 if bracing explicitly). Fail: 2d8 bludgeoning, spear drops. Contested Strength to keep it from falling into the shaft if it drops.

Insight DC 13 (active): the spear's weight shifts toward the altar chamber before it can be seen — direction specific, consistent. Window closes entering the antechamber. Noting this: Advantage on the first Persuasion check with Mars.

Five Senses — Scene 3

Sight
Armory: armor at attention in perfect formation, torch shadows wrong for their positions — they move slightly, not from air currents. Choking Hall: visibility limited by gas shimmer. Elder Stair: older stone, better carved — this part of the structure was built with intention.
Sound
Armory: the creak of metal joints when the constructs activate. Choking Hall: a hiss from wall vents — then coughing. Elder Stair: the crack of a support stone pulled, then the cascade of rockfall ahead.
Smell
Choking Hall: the gas — sweet-rot smell, not sharp. Sulfuric would be obvious. This is subtler. Below face level it's denser. The floor is the danger.
Feel
Armory: the air pressure changes when the constructs activate — a slight outward push, like something drawing breath. Elder Stair: the spear's weight shifts forward, toward something ahead. Whoever carries it feels it.
Taste
Choking Hall: the gas — sweet in the mouth before it hits the lungs. The body tries to exhale but the gas stays. Metallic undertone that intensifies toward the antechamber.

Sunken Armory Combat

Use Animated Armor (MM p.19) with +1 AC (old Roman construction, not magical). Two constructs — one per side of the chamber. Formation means they move as a unit.

Calling out formation to Varro before activation (Perception DC 11): first defender gets Advantage on first attack roll.

Hesitating visibly (spending more than 30 seconds discussing in a 6-second round): first construct activates. Decisive movement through the chamber without attack posture: constructs do not activate — they are guarding, not hunting.

Beat 1 — The Antechamber

Stone walls, no torches — light comes from the altar itself. The space is older than the tunnels; older than Vindolanda; older than Rome at this latitude. The altar is at the far end. The spear, when placed or carried here, levitates two inches above its surface, rotating slowly.

"The sound of the siege reaches you as if through water — distant, muffled, not urgent. Something in this room is more important than what is happening above."

Iris (magic-sensitive): she feels it first — the arrival of attention. From above, from below, from the walls. Concentrated here. Has been concentrated here for a very long time.

THE MOMENT — Three Figures Must Kneel

Mars does not appear. He is already here — the room is his attention made architectural. When the demand comes, it does not come as sound.

Three figures. One Roman. One Germanic. One spear-touched. Kneel. Or we do not continue.

The party decides who kneels:

  • One volunteer: Mars allows it. Session 5 begins with one extra consequence already in effect.
  • Two volunteers: He accepts with reservations. Session 5 begins normally.
  • Three correct volunteers: Mars is satisfied. One Session 5 trial drops one difficulty tier — GM's choice, pick the one that makes S5 most interesting.

Then: Sacrifice type declared (see below). The spear responds to the type — blood rises it, glory steadies it, obedience aligns it with the altar.

Beat 3 — The Pause in the Siege

Whatever sacrifice is accepted: the spear levitates fully. Light from the altar pulses once — felt through the stone, through the floor above, through the boots of every Germanic warrior at the gate. The ram stops. The horns go silent. Not because the siege is over — because something has spoken.

The party has a window. What they do with it determines how Session 5 opens.

Five Senses — Scene 4

Sight
Light from the altar — amber-gold, no source. The spear levitating two inches above the stone, rotating slowly. The walls have carvings: some older than Rome, some newer, some that appear to be adding themselves as the party watches.
Sound
The siege muffled, as through water. Then, when the demand comes, not through sound — through understanding, the way you understand you are being watched without hearing footsteps.
Smell
Hot iron — the antechamber smells of forges and blood and the clean metal smell of new weapons. Specific, not unpleasant. Martial. This is a war god's space.
Feel
The floor is warm. Not from below — from within the stone itself. The warmth is consistent: uniform, ancient, not fading. Whoever carries the spear feels the warmth in their hands first.
Taste
Metal — sharp, clean, like a sword just drawn. The taste intensifies when the sacrifice type is declared. On blood: metallic. On glory: nothing. On obedience: the taste of anticipation.

Sacrifice Types

Blood: 10 HP per volunteer. Mars removes one corruption stage from that character. Visible: a cut appears on the volunteer's palm, bleeds into the altar, stops. The spear rises.

Glory: Swear publicly to bear the blame for everything that follows. Advantage on Persuasion vs. legionaries; become scapegoat in Session 5 political fallout. Visible: the spear glows steady — a soldier's salute.

Obedience: Promise to carry out Mars' Call to War in Session 5. Guarantees a favorable start to the final trial. Visible: the spear aligns with the altar precisely, hovering at a specific angle — it is pointing somewhere specific.

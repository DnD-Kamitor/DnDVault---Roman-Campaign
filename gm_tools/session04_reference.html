Session 4: The God's Demand — GM Reference

Scene Flow

  1. Cold Open — Three simultaneous crises: gate breach (battering ram), granary fire (Brutus agent), shrine desecration attempt (steal spear case). Party must triage. The north gate attack is the feint — the insider shrine theft is the primary objective.
  2. Council in the Principia (Scene 1) — Survivors convene: Corvinus, Lucius, Varro, Cassia, party, Vercingetorix's envoy. Decide escort composition, oath terms, and sacrifice type. Three faction paths (Roman Authority, Shared Custody, Secret Transfer).
  3. Preparing the Procession (Scene 2) — Scout the stair, secure the vicus, call in favors, receive blessings. Handout 9 distributed. Up to three preparations allowed.
  4. The Procession Below (Scene 3) — Three tunnel scenes: Sunken Armory (Animated Armor), Choking Hall (gas, Con saves), Elder Stair (Brutus' agent triggers rockfall). Who leads, guards civilians, carries the spear.
  5. Antechamber of Mars (Scene 4) — Mars demands three figures kneel in submission. One Roman, one Germanic, one spear-touched. Party declares the sacrifice type. Spear levitates. Siege pauses. Germanic horns go silent.

Three Clue Rule: Mars' Demand

Clue 1 — Shrine Conflagration: Witness Cassia performing augury during the opening bell. Mars shows the spear on the hidden altar. Three figures kneeling. Someone must volunteer to kneel.

Clue 2 — Tunnel Map: Investigation DC 14 (active: Varro's office) or receive from Vercingetorix's envoy. Sealed stair connects to collapsed Roman water tunnel exiting near the south ditch. A rune symbol marks where Thusnelda first sealed the spear.

Clue 3 — Saboteur Confession: Intimidation DC 15 (active: interrogating a captured Brutus sleeper agent from Scene 0). He reveals Brutus ordered the fort burned to prevent Mars being appeased — confirming the god can be bargained with if the spear reaches the ritual site.

Council Decision: Escort Path

Roman Authority (Corvinus): Extra legionaries but Disadvantage on Persuasion with Vercingetorix later.
Shared Custody (Varro + Cassia): Three kneeling witnesses chosen (can include PCs). Advantage on future dealings with Mars but fewer escort numbers.
Secret Transfer (Lucius): Attempt to fake the ritual and destroy the spear. Betrayal path — prepare separate complications for Session 5.

Council reading checks:
— Insight DC 13 (Corvinus while Cassia speaks): assembling arguments, not listening. 5 above: he's already hand-signaling his bodyguard captain with a contingency plan.
— Deception DC 14 (convince Corvinus' captain that escort route is already secured by Varro's orders): buys time. Fail = captain reports immediately.
— Intimidation DC 14 (address captain directly about party's track record): success = captain recommends deferring to party; Corvinus overrules more slowly, accepts Shared Custody instead of Roman Authority.

Three Kneeling Figures (Scene 4)

Mars demands one Roman, one Germanic, one spear-touched. Party fills these roles.

One volunteer: Mars allows it, started disappointed. Session 5 begins with one extra consequence already in effect.
Two volunteers: He accepts with reservations. Session 5 begins normally.
Three correct volunteers: Mars is satisfied. One Session 5 trial drops one difficulty tier (GM's choice — pick the one that makes S5 most interesting).

Sacrifice type in the antechamber:
— Blood: 10 HP per volunteer. Mars removes one corruption stage.
— Glory: Swear to bear the blame. Advantage on Persuasion vs. legionaries; become scapegoat if things go poorly.
— Obedience: Promise to carry out Mars' Call to War (Session 5). Guarantees favorable start to final trial.

Siege Resources (Running Clocks)

Food Clock: Starts at 3. Loses 1 per failed granary fire roll. At 0: civilians riot, escort suffers Disadvantage on initiative inside the tunnels.
Morale Clock: Starts at 2. Loses 1 if Corvinus bullies or civilians panic. At 0: same as Food Clock result.

Keep pressure visible during talking scenes: smoke in the audience chamber, tremors as battering rams strike, civilians chanting prayers.

State at Session Start / Contingency Checks

Confirm: Who holds the spear? (That character becomes focus of all NPC interaction; Mars speaks through them in Scene 3.) Is Tribune Lucius alive? Did Varro survive Session 3? (If not: all soldiers take -1 to Wisdom saves this session; party must appoint another Centurion.) Is Cassia alive? (Without her: harsher omens, fewer answers.) Is Vercingetorix allied? (Tunnels and diversion; or hostile = threatens to take spear once it leaves the vault.)

If Corvinus felt sidelined: He arrests Cassia at session end, forcing a rescue in Session 5's opening.
If Lucius' sabotage succeeded: Spear damaged; Mars has Disadvantage on initial checks but is enraged.
If Vercingetorix was honored: His warriors guard the fort through the night; party gets a long rest.

Commendationes This Session

1: Holding the wall during the gate breach — the character whose action determines whether the gate holds or falls. Varro files without being asked.
2: Abandoning a tactically superior position to protect vicus civilians. Corvinus resents it and files the paperwork anyway.
3: Holding the alliance with Vercingetorix against Corvinus/Lucius pressure. No commendatio from Corvinus. Reward is different: Vercingetorix names the party in a specific way at session end.

Citizenship window (Session 4): Tribune Lucius offers a personal attestation in his dispatch to Rome — available to any character with Latini standing and 3+ commendationes. Approach must be party-initiated. Offer not made twice; closes if session ends without follow-up.

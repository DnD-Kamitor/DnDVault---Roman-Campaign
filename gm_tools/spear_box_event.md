# The Box Event — Spear + Greataxe

## What Happened

The artificer built a containment box for the spear. It was working: suppressing the
pulse, holding Mars's hunger inside. Then someone placed the greataxe in with it.

Two weapons of war overwhelmed the containment. The box cracked. The suppression failed.
The spear's aura now bleeds outward, mixed with the greataxe's rage energy — stronger
than before the box existed.

## The Aura Effect

The group now radiates Mars's presence.

- Animals flee without provocation
- Villagers step back without knowing why
- Germanic people who can read spiritual signs see something standing behind the party
  that has no face

## Mechanical

- **+2 to Intimidation** for the rest of the session
- **Disadvantage on calm diplomacy** (Persuasion to reassure, calm, or comfort)
- **Undead in the dungeon sense the party from further away** — no surprise rounds against
  undead enemies unless the party is invisible or silenced

## To Fix It

Remove the greataxe from the box, or repair the containment.

- **DC 14 Arcana or Tinker's Tools**, 10 minutes of work
- On success: the box reseals, aura suppressed again, effects end
- On failure: box stays cracked, try again after a short rest

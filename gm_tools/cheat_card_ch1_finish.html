Chapter 1 Finish — GM Cheat Card

Players are at Scene 4. They just came out of the vault.
The story in 3 sentences: The players are Roman soldiers who found an ancient spear in a vault under Fort Vindolanda. The spear is supernatural — proximity to it makes people violent and fanatical. Two factions want it: a corrupt Roman senator who will use it for political power, and Germanic tribes who want it back because it is sacred to them.

Scene 4 — RIGHT NOW

Trigger: Players climb out of the vault shaft.

What's happening: Marius Coda (Gallic stone-hauler, gone berserk) stands over 3 fallen legionaries. He's holding two bloody gladii. He wants the spear. Two cultist legionaries stand near him — not attacking yet, just muttering.

What the players need to do: Stop Marius. Three ways: kill him, subdue him (Athletics DC 13 with 2 helpers), or move the spear 60ft away so he loses focus.

Marius yells: "The spear calls, the spear drinks, the spear wants a champion." He only attacks whoever is holding the spear.

Marius: Berserker CR 2, AC 13, HP 67, attacks with 2 gladii.
Cultists (Titus + Gnaeus): CR 1/8, AC 10, HP 9. They DO NOT attack unless directly threatened.

Ends when: Marius is dead, subdued, or loses sight of the spear. Cultists snap out of it once Marius is dealt with.

Scene 5 — Immediately After

Trigger: Scene 4 resolves.

What's happening: Silence. Legionaries watch from the edges of the courtyard. Then the principia door opens and Legate Corvinus walks out.

Corvinus says: He saw what happened. He wants the spear locked away under guard tonight. He's suspicious of the party. He's not accusing them yet — he's watching.

Key moment: Corvinus asks directly: "What is that weapon and where did it come from?" The players decide how much to tell him.

If they show him the contract scroll from the vault: He goes quiet. He knows the OTHALAN name. He says nothing and walks away.

Ends when: Corvinus leaves. Players have the night to rest and talk. Chapter 1 is done. Chapter 2 starts next session.

Chapter 1 — Finish (Scenes 4 & 5)

Players just climbed out of the vault. Scene 4 starts immediately.
The Story (3 sentences)
The players are Roman soldiers who found an ancient spear in a vault under Fort Vindolanda. The spear is supernatural — proximity to it makes people violent and fanatical. Two factions want it: a corrupt Roman senator who will use it for political power, and Germanic tribes who want it back because it is sacred to them.
Scene 4 — Marius Coda (RIGHT NOW)
Trigger: Players climb out of the vault shaft. Night air. Then the shouting.

What's happening: Marius Coda (Gallic stone-hauler) stands over 3 fallen legionaries holding two bloody gladii. He is berserk. He wants whoever is holding the spear. Two cultist legionaries stand near him — not attacking yet, muttering.

What he yells: "The spear calls, the spear drinks, the spear wants a champion." He focuses only on the spear-bearer.

Marius: Berserker CR 2, AC 13, HP 67, 2 gladii attacks.
Cultists (Titus + Gnaeus): CR 1/8, AC 10, HP 9. DO NOT have them attack unless threatened.

Three ways to end the fight:
  • Kill him — normal combat. He dies saying "Mars."
  • Subdue him — Athletics DC 13 with 2 legionary helpers. He collapses weeping, remembers nothing.
  • Move the spear 60ft away — he freezes, confused, then breaks down sobbing.

Alive legionary on the ground: Aelius Rufus, face-down, shallow wound. Medicine DC 12 to stabilize. If saved, he appears in Chapter 2 as someone who owes them.
Ends when: Marius is stopped. Cultists snap out of it. Silence falls.
Scene 5 — Corvinus (Immediately After)
Trigger: Scene 4 resolves. Legionaries watching from the edges. Principia door opens.

What's happening: Legate Corvinus walks out. He saw. He wants order restored and the spear secured under guard tonight.

He asks: "What is that weapon and where did it come from?" Players decide how much to tell.

If they show him the contract scroll from the vault: He goes quiet. He recognises the OTHALAN name. He says nothing and walks away. This is a big moment — let the silence breathe.

He does not accuse them. He is watching. He is afraid of what he's seen.
Ends when: Corvinus leaves. Night. Short rest. Chapter 1 done.

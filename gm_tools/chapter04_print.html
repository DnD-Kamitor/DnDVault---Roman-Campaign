Chapter 4
The God's Demand
Shadow of Mars Campaign — Session 4 of 5
Level 6 → 7  |  3–4 Hours  |  6 Players
Siege + Politics + Dungeon Escort
Vindolanda has endured six days of siege. Three simultaneous crises force triage while Mars demands the spear be carried below by nightfall. The party must survive the siege, navigate Roman politics, escort the spear through haunted tunnels, and kneel before a god.

Resource Clocks

Food Clock (starts at 3): Loses 1 each round the granary fire is not controlled. Hits 0: civilians riot; escort has disadvantage on initiative in the tunnels.

Morale Clock (starts at 2): Loses 1 if Corvinus bullies unchallenged, or if civilians panic without a party member intervening. Hits 0: all soldiers take –1 to Wisdom saves for the rest of the session.

Scene 0: The Bell and the Breach

You are already running when the bell rings. Smoke pours from the west workshops. The north gate creaks, splinters, and finally gives. A Germanic battering ram lurches through the gap. Varro shouts for shields to form. Cassia staggers out of the shrine with fresh blood down her chin.

"He demands the spear. If we do not take it below by nightfall he will feed the fort to his wolves."

Three simultaneous crises. The party engages two. The third resolves by NPC competence.

Reading the assault (Perception DC 12, active from the wall): The gate attack is a feint. The fire plume goes the wrong direction for an external source. The real threat is the shrine. Calling this out lets the party cover all three crises optimally.

5-Above Results

  • Athletics 5+: Spot the ram-crew commander — Germanic warrior, amber war-charm carved with TIWAZ on his belt. Sigrun can identify this as a specific chieftain's people (not the united warband). Matters in Ch5.
  • Survival 5+: Find the slow-burn candle arson setup before it's consumed. Physical evidence. Linkable to the raven message.
  • Insight 5+: Agent avoids a specific threshold rune. Someone briefed him on the ward system. Someone inside the fort told him.

Scene 3: The Procession Below

The flagstones of the principia grind apart. Cold damp air rises carrying the smell of old blood and iron. Torches gutter sideways even though there is no wind. The spear pulses once with a deep red glow. Mars has opened the way.

At each room: who leads, who guards civilians, who carries the spear? These choices affect Session 5 corruption checks.

Room 1 — The Sunken Armory

Armor pieces animate when touched by spear-light. 4 Animated Armors. Constructs — no Turn Undead, no undead vulnerabilities.

  • Perception DC 11 (active, before spear-light reaches room): Pieces are in formation — advance rank, reserve rank. A soldier who calls this out: first defender following the layout has advantage on their first attack.
  • Arcana DC 14 (passive): Divine resonance, not necromancy. Consecrated legionary armor responding to the spear.
  • Clue under a corroded shield: "The god hears the loudest heart."

Room 2 — The Choking Hall

Poisonous marsh gas vents from below. Constitution DC 14 (active: each round in the hall).

  • If gas mapped during preparation: Dense near floor, air pockets near ceiling. Stealth DC 13 (moving slow and high) gives advantage on Con saves.
  • Cassia (if in escort): Can spend one spell slot to clear the hall entirely.
  • Medicine DC 13 (active: treating unconscious PC): Stabilizes without expending a spell slot.

Room 3 — The Elder Stair

Narrow steps around a dry well. Brutus' agent triggers a rockfall.

  • Dexterity save DC 15 or Acrobatics DC 13 (active: bracing against the wall). Use whichever the player declares first.
  • Failure: 2d8 bludgeoning and the spear slides toward the shaft. Contested Strength check to hold it.
DM: Brutus' agent
If captured here: Intimidation DC 15 extracts the confession (granary fire and Cassia's death, both ordered). On 5 above: agent names a second fort contact before catching himself. Write that name — it surfaces in Ch5 epilogue.

Three Kneeling Figures

At the final door, Mars demands three mortals kneel: one Roman, one Germanic, one tied to the spear. If the party refuses: one extra Stone Golem trial added in Session 5.

Scene 4: The Antechamber of Mars

The chamber beyond is circular, carved before Rome existed. The air hums like taut bowstrings. In the center stands the same granite altar where the spear once rested.

Above it hangs a vision of Vindolanda seen from the clouds: burning, flooded, broken.

Mars' voice vibrates in your bones rather than your ears. "Bring me what is mine and speak your price."
The callback: This is the same chamber as Session 1. The same altar. The same chain anchors. The Wight's remains if the party left them. The campaign begins and ends in the same circle of stone. Narrate the recognition explicitly: "The altar. The chains. You've been here before."

After the offering: the spear levitates to the altar. Mars: "Tomorrow you stand before me." Cut immediately to the fort above. The siege pauses. Germanic horns fall silent.

Conclusion

Night falls. The rain stops. In the distance wolves begin to howl and then silence themselves, one by one, as if someone stronger demanded their quiet. Vindolanda breathes for the first time in a week. Tomorrow a god arrives.

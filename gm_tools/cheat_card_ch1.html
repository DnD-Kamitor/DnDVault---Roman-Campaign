Ch1: Blood and Omens

L3 → 4 at end  ·  3–4 hrs  ·  Fort Vindolanda, the vault beneath the principia
The players are Roman soldiers ordered into a sealed vault beneath their own fort to retrieve an ancient spear that killed a god's son. Something has been guarding it for three centuries, and it does not want to let go. What they bring back up will decide who they answer to for the rest of the campaign.

Duration: Scene 0 is a 30-second visceral hook. Do not run it as a separate scene: cut straight into Scene 1's briefing.
Now
Three nights ago, everyone had the same dream: blood underfoot, a glowing spear, a voice that says "MEUM." (MINE.) By dawn every dog in the fort is dead. Now: the principia, thick with incense and rot, Corvinus assigning the mission.
Read aloud: the dream (speak slowly, with dread)
"Three nights ago. You dream. All of you, the same dream. You stand in darkness, ankle-deep in blood that doesn't ripple. In the distance, a spear stands upright in the earth, glowing like a dying star... A voice, old, vast, furious, speaks a single word: 'MEUM.' (MINE.) You wake. Your tent reeks of copper and smoke. By dawn, every dog in Fort Vindolanda is dead."
Read aloud: the principia
"The Legate speaks. His voice is iron scraping stone: 'You've been chosen. Not for honor. For survival... We've lost four men already: two to madness, two to each other's blades.' He unrolls a rough sketch of underground ruins. 'You're going down there. You're bringing it up. You're not asking questions.' Across the table, the Augur whispers: 'Mars aversus est.'"
Lucius arrives mid-briefing, unannounced, hands Corvinus a sealed dispatch. Give Handout 1 when Corvinus sets the wax tablet down. → Scene 2.
Briefing facts (from Corvinus)
  • Three days ago a work crew found ancient ruins beneath the fort while digging a well
  • They unearthed an iron spear covered in runes
  • That night all camp dogs died
  • Augurs found black livers, reversed bird flights
  • One worker touched the spear, went mad, killed two companions
  • Corvinus wants the artifact secured and brought to him
How to run Cassia here
She does not oppose Corvinus directly: she cannot win that fight. One line, addressed to the party, not him: "The augury was clear. What is sealed below is sealed for a reason. If you go down, go knowing that." Then she stops. Her face is not fear; it's someone who has already decided, watching the party decide.
How to run Varro here
Skepticism about the timeline, not about destroying the spear (he doesn't know what it is yet): "We don't know what sealed it or who is on the other side of that seal." Corvinus: "Noted. Dismissed." Varro stays near the door. Corvinus doesn't press it.
Lucius arrives: no introduction
Enters without knocking, sealed dispatch satchel, imperial crest. Hands Corvinus the letter, waits, sits uninvited. Says nothing to the party. If asked who he is: Corvinus answers flatly. Cassia's face when he enters: she's seen him before, isn't surprised.
Insight DC 14: temporal gate (Corvinus' fear, Scene 1 only)
Window closes when Lucius arrives. On success: Corvinus is afraid of something specific, not just ambitious: he looks at the staircase twice in two minutes. On 5-above: it's not about the spear's power. It's what the spear represents to a man with a debt.
Refusal contingency (quick reference)
Polite refusal → doubles pay, then sends another unit (doesn't come back). Citing the augury → removes Cassia, gives an hour (she finds them privately). Outright defiance → DC 18 Persuasion; below: Centurion escort (Varro); at/above: he backs down, gives 24 hrs; 5-above: respects them, assigns as lead unit.

Room 1: The Shaft (40 ft descent)
Rope ladder, poorly anchored. Torch flames lean downward in an unnatural draft (no mechanical effect, just wrong). Survival DC 11 (active, reading the draft): the pulse means two chambers with different pressure; cedar smell means deliberate sealed storage, not collapse.
At the bottom, scratched at knee height: seven scratches, one diagonal line through all seven, and NOLITE (Do not). Left by a previous crew member. Not a puzzle: just a warning.
Room 2: The Entrance Passage (40 ft)
Roman-over-Germanic construction. 15 ft in: an abandoned pick, not rusted, handle broken not decayed, pointed back toward the shaft (Perception DC 10 passive). DC 12 Investigation connects the warning's maker to the pick. Librarius/Haruspex/Explorator/Soldier all get a flavor read (see book for full text).
Ends at a T-junction: left = warmer air, dry stone (Shield Hall); right = colder air, slow water (Flooded Gallery).
Sketch the T-junction on scrap paper. Ask which branch first. Both merge at the Binding Chamber. → 2b or 2c.

Room 4: The Shield Hall
Dozens to a hundred Germanic shields hung edge to edge: four tribes that never coexisted in the same region, arranged in a deliberate hierarchy with Mars's wolf highest. History 13/15, Religion 14, Perception 11, Nature 12, and Athletics 12 (grip cut from inside, decommissioned, not spoils) all give passive/active reads. No combat: the shields don't move, they watch.

Ursula Günther note: the boar is her mother's tribe. She reads Elder Futhark without a check and needs no rolls for tribal symbols. Give her Handout 15 privately and wait: do not tell her how to react.

Sicus: one of the lost men
Missing lorica, wound at shoulder, stable but dehydrated. Perception 11 finds the drag trail; Medicine 9 confirms stable. Water/healing → conscious in a round; else DC 12 Medicine + 2 min.
"They came down before you. Three of them. Red cloaks, oil lamps... Short man, shaved head. He pulled something on the wall near the last chamber: a lever. Heard it grind. He left by the passage on the left."
Gives: cultists were here first (advance knowledge); a lever exists ahead; presses the second OTHALAN bone token into a character's hand (advantage on Wight communication check later, stacking with the contract scroll). He cannot fight and won't follow into combat.
The Hidden Alcove
Investigation DC 15 (backwards-facing shield) reveals a narrow gap: wax tablet ("Corvinus knows nothing of the bargain... Warn the..." obliterated), an OTHALAN bone token (one half of a contract pair), and a bronze stylus. Give Handout 12 to whoever made the check: let them share it, don't read aloud.
Anti-Rome framing (DM voice, use if it lands)
Rome didn't build this vault: it inherited it, violated the bargain, and is sending soldiers to retrieve a weapon it was never permitted to have. Every shield belongs to a people Rome has since destroyed, displaced, or erased.

Room 5: The Flooded Gallery
Chest-deep black standing water, 30 ft. Survival 12 (read the waterline): east wall is shallower, advantage on the Athletics 11 stealth wade. Four Shadows lurk, drawn to light/sound: group Stealth 14 (extinguish torches) passes for free.
Far wall: a preserved Germanic warrior, seated, seiðr death (voluntary, not violence). Carries a framea and a second OTHALAN bone token. Disturbing the body disrespectfully wakes one Shadow with no warning.
Room 6: The Bone Chamber
ENCOUNTER: 2 Ghouls + 1 Ghast: Hard/1,700 XP for 5 players
Ghouls dormant under rubble, activate once party is fully inside (give one free round of looking around). Ghast emerges last from behind stone benches. Ghast Stench (DC 10 Con or poisoned) on emergence. Paralyzed target + Ghast attack = advantage + auto-crit: intentional.
Escape route: back through the Flooded Gallery always available; Shadows don't reactivate for a loud retreat.
After combat: clay vessel offering (grain + pre-Roman Mars coin): someone has been feeding this place until recently. → merges at Binding Chamber.

5-player note: run full encounter as written: one Ghoul on ceiling (Wall Crawl Drop), one from floor, party can't shield-wall both. Death Scream when Ghast drops.
DM: who has been feeding this place
Titus Sempronius Caecilius, Cassia's predecessor as augur, maintained this offering cycle for decades. Died of fever in AD 173: his death broke the chain. Cassia inherited his papers but not his understanding. Session 2 thread.
The preserved warrior
Investigation 12: died deliberately, sat down, crossed arms, no violence. Perception 12: re-tempering marks and controlled-retreat foot posture show a veteran, not a sacrifice. If a character takes the framea: real Germanic craftsmanship, Cassia will want to see it in Session 2.
Room 7: The Binding Chamber (both branches merge)
Chains in ranks, none collar-sized for a human. TIWAZ carved on the far wall; below it, scratched Latin: "We turned back here." One chain carries steady tension from below the floor ring right now (Athletics 12).
The chain sequence puzzle
Investigation DC 14 reveals the oxidation-pattern order: oldest chain first (left wall), newest last (right wall), then the centre. Wrong pull without the sequence: DC 13 Str save or restrained 1 min (no damage). Correct sequence opens a hidden alcove: oil lamp + oilskin scroll.
Scroll (DC 15 Intelligence): the binding terms: tribes sealed the vault for 3 Roman generations, Rome agreed never to dig within 50 ft of the principia's east wall. The excavation broke it the moment the first shovel hit.
Room 8: The Runic Corridor
30 ft, 8 rows of 5 rune tiles each. Wrong tile = 1d8 lightning, no save, PLUS on the FIRST wrong step: advance the Altar Chamber guardians one free prep round (Wight draws weapon, Fanatic flanks, one Shadow coalesces).
Safe rune is always OTHALAN: column shifts every row. Three independent ways to know it. → Altar Chamber.

Now
Perfect circular chamber. Granite altar, spear resting on stone pegs, chains slack around the base "as though they gave up trying to hold it." Within 5 ft of the altar, air stops moving.
The Wight speaks first: do not start combat immediately
"The bargain was broken. Not by you. But by your kind. I am still bound. You are not."
Highest-Wisdom character understands the dialect without knowing why. Ursula understands regardless: grew up hearing it. She may choose not to share immediately.
ENCOUNTER: 1 Wight (CR3) + 1 Cult Fanatic (CR2, adapted) + 2 Shadows (CR1/2): 1,800 XP, near-Deadly at L3 depleted
Exit condition: back beyond 15 ft of the altar → Wight holds, Shadows stop. Communication check DC 14 Persuasion/Insight (advantage w/ contract scroll) → Wight pauses, gives one more line, combat still resumes after.
Cult Fanatic (adapted)
AC 13, HP 33. Gladius +4/1d6+2. Spells: sacred flame, thaumaturgy, bane, inflict wounds, shield of faith, hold person, spiritual weapon. Acts Initiative 10, flanks the spear-holder round 1. Already pulled the lever (difficult terrain at the corridor exit) before the party arrived.
OTHALAN token (tactical target)
Pinned at the Wight's sternum, AC 13, 5 HP, shatters on one hit. If destroyed/removed: Shadows lose binding for 1 round (-2 attack, no Strength Drain), then act independently.
Wight trait: Dark Resolve
Regains 2 (1d4) HP/turn while any creature within 30 ft is Frightened (Shadow drain, failed spear save, spell: any source). He cannot suppress it.

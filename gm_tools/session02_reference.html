Session 2: The Tribune's Gambit — GM Reference

Scene Flow

  1. Cold Open — Sentry Sextus found dead, throat cut, "ROMA CADIT" carved. Trumpet announces Tribune's arrival before investigation can begin.
  2. Tribune Arrives (Scene 1) — Lucius Valerius Maximus takes command via sealed Senate orders. Legate suspended; party "promoted" to Senate envoys.
  3. Investigation Window (Scene 2) — Several hours before departure. Party pursues Sextus murder, Tribune's true agenda, Cassia + Varro intelligence.
  4. The Raid (Scene 3) — That night, Vercingetorix leads Germanic assault on three sides. Praetorians protect the spear, not the walls.
  5. Tribune's Move (Scene 4) — After the raid: Tribune falsely accuses party of treason. Varro steps up. Decision point: expose, fight, negotiate, intimidate, or flee.

Three Clue Rule: Tribune's True Agenda

Clue 1 — The Encoded Letter: Stealth DC 13 or Deception DC 14 (Tribune's quarters) + Investigation DC 16 or Thieves' Tools DC 14 (dispatch box). Letter names "G.C.B." — Gaius Cassius Brutus — ordering "permanent removal."

Clue 2 — His Behaviour During the Raid: Perception DC 14 or Insight DC 14 (watching Tribune specifically during alarm). Praetorians protect the spear, not the walls. Tribune pushes to accelerate departure after.

Clue 3 — Varro's Network: Ask Varro directly (no check). "That Tribune was on the supply embezzlement commission six months ago. Guess who was embezzling." History DC 13 (passive) identifies "G.C.B." = Gaius Cassius Brutus.

Three Clue Rule: Sextus Murder

Clue 1 — The Wound: Perception DC 11 (active: reading the body — he didn't turn; he knew his killer). Medicine DC 13 (active: one stroke, left-to-right, right-handed attacker shorter than Sextus).

Clue 2 — Boot Print: Survival DC 12 (active: approach path came from inside fort, left toward stables). Investigation DC 14 (active: inside-heel wear pattern). Perception DC 15 (watching departure) to match Quintus Flavius' walk.

Clue 3 — Varro: "Forty men knew that schedule." + "One man asked for transfer papers this morning. Fast. Quintus something. Twelve-year man." Does not pursue without proof.

Scene 4: Confrontation Options

Expose: Produce decoded letter or evidence. Praetorians hesitate; Varro intervenes; Tribune trapped.
Fight: Tribune + 6 Praetorians (CR 2 each). Win = fugitives from Rome.
Negotiate: DC 16 Persuasion with clue in hand. Tribune offers private deal: "Help me destroy it my way and this goes away."
Intimidate: DC 15 Intimidation (controlled demonstration of what the party has survived, stated calmly). Success: Praetorian hand-signal changes from "arrest" to "hold." Same deal offered from weaker position. 5 above DC: Praetorians disengage entirely.
Flee with Vercingetorix: If treated with respect. Forest refuge; party becomes fugitives with Germanic ally.
Surrender spear: Party confined to quarters. Session 3 starts as prisoners being transported to Rome.

Vercingetorix Trust Ladder

Persuasion DC 14 (acknowledge Germanic claim) = Low trust: "The spear has a name — 'the thing that should not exist.'"
Intimidation DC 14 (martial competence show, not threat) = Medium trust: Location of the grove, one day into Teutoburg Forest.
Persuasion DC 14 + treating as equal = High trust: Grove requires a willing sacrifice; grandfather failed because sacrifice was unwilling.
Proved by action = Complete trust: "I am dying. This is my last campaign. That is enough for me." Unlocks full ally Sessions 3–4.

Paths to earn respect: Persuasion DC 14, Intimidation DC 14 (audacia), Insight DC 13 (reading assessment not surface conversation).

Vacancy / State at Session Start

Aquilifer (Gaius Metellus) still vacant from Session 1. Morale checks at Disadvantage until filled. The murder of Sextus is a morale pressure for any role with perimeter/patrol duties.

Corruption carry-over: Any character who touched the spear in Session 1 has dreams again. Before Scene 3, the player with the highest corruption level describes their dream to the table: the fort burning, comrades dead, themselves holding the spear. Don't make it mechanical yet. Make it felt.

Corruption — Session 2

No new corruption events this session unless a character voluntarily touches or attunes to the spear. Watch for any player choosing to interact with the spear during the investigation window.

Frumentarius trigger: If the party identifies Quintus Flavius (the sleeper agent) and captures him or produces definitive proof to Varro, award Senior Frumentarius status immediately. If Flavius escapes identified but uncaptured, he surfaces in Session 4 as a potential contact to turn.

Commendationes This Session

1: Coordinating fort defense during the raid with demonstrable leadership (rallying soldiers, holding a sector). Corvinus awards grudgingly.
2: Exposing the Tribune's false accusation through physical evidence (decoded letter, boot print, Clue 3) — not argument alone. One commendatio to the character producing the decisive piece.
3: Reaching Complete Trust with Vercingetorix. Not a standard commendatio — but he sends a runner to Corvinus establishing the party's word is good with his people.

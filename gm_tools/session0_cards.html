Session 0 Player Cards — Print and cut. Fatum + Sacrifice cards go to players. Cover story + Corruption cards are DM-only.

Do not show them again until Session 3. Cover story cards (blue border): fill in, give to each player individually — they don't compare notes. Corruption profile cards (grey border): DM-only. Fill in privately after Session 0.

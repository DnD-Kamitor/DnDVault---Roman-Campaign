Session 5: The Wrath of Mars — GM Reference

Scene Flow

  1. Cold Open — Dawn. Rain stops mid-drop. Germanic arrows freeze in air. Parade ground becomes black sand. Fort-height armored figure on a throne of weapons. "MORTALS. I WATCHED. I ARRIVED. NOW YOU ANSWER." All checks close when Mars addresses the party.
  2. Mars Specifies the Trial (Scene 1) — He offers three paths simultaneously: Option A (Champion Trial), Option B (Trial of Blades), Option C (Trial of Voice). No preference stated. If party hesitates more than one minute of table time, he chooses Option B.
  3. The Trial (Scene 2) — Run the chosen option. Arena weapon cards (Handout 11) placed face-down, flipped at combat start. Environmental phases in for Option B (barracks buildings materialize each round).
  4. The Price (Scene 3) — Mars approaches regardless of outcome. Consequences menu: Mark of Mars, the Chosen, the Debt, the Transformation, Prophecy. He is satisfied, not appeased.
  5. Return to Vindolanda (Scene 4) — Black sand recedes. Witnesses rush forward. Player epilogue speeches. Citizenship document arrives. NPC final states.
  6. Epilogue: One Year Later (Scene 5) — Each player: Where are you? How often does the mark glow? What rumors follow you? NPC updates. Close narration.

Option C — Arguments That Work / Fail

Arguments that move Mars:
1. Honor argument: The spear dishonored him by sowing madness (corrupted worker, Cassia's visions, Varro's men).
2. Discipline argument: Destroying it preserved the disciplined war Mars demands (reference sacrifices, escort kneeling).
3. War vs. murder: They chose war's harsh honor over easy cruelty (sparing Vercingetorix, saving civilians, capturing traitors).

Arguments that anger him: "It was too dangerous." "We had no choice." "Rome needed this."

Failure recovery — Virtus demonstration: Step into the fighting circle without a weapon and wait. Wisdom save DC 14 (holding still without flinching). Success: "That. That is what I asked for." Trial resets to neutral; Option C available again. Failure: they flinch. Trial continues at normal parameters. Do not punish the attempt.

Corruption Level 5 Guidance

Option A: DC 16 Wisdom save each round to act freely. Three successes reduce to Level 4.
Option B: Mars targets them preferentially until party calls them back by name three times (recreating Session 3 mechanic).
Option C: Mars uses them as his mouthpiece — "What do you want?" Truthful self-awareness = no penalty. Selfish answer = -5 to Persuasion DC.
After the session: Cannot shed corruption without performing a selfless deed unseen by any authority within one year.

Consequence Menu

Mark of Mars: Handout 10, as written.
The Chosen (1 or 2 on crit success): Benefits + Call to War obligation. When he calls, they come. Refuse = mark sears, lose benefits until quest completed.
The Debt: If they bargained, one future favor owed. "When I call, you will come."
The Transformation: Offer to any player wanting a noble exit — become a guardian spirit of the arena. Fort earns permanent Advantage on morale checks.
Prophecy: Vindolanda becomes a pilgrimage site for a century. Rome will fall from within in three generations.

Citizenship resolution (Scene 4): If Lucius sent the dispatch in Session 4, a response arrives during the aftermath — carried by a rider who has been waiting at the gate since dawn. Imperial epistula: sealed with the Emperor's office stamp. Lucius reads it first and hands it to the relevant character. One sentence in a different hand: "Distinguished service on the Germanic frontier, confirmed by two independent sponsors. The Emperor's office takes note."

Commendationes Final Tally

No citizenship catch-up mechanic in Session 5. The paperwork was either filed or it was not.
Opp. 1: Surviving and completing the divine trial. Filed by Cassia as religious witness — the only commendatio issued by a religious authority in this campaign.
Opp. 2: Explicit choice not to use lethal force when justifiable (Option A or during Mars' judgment call on a specific character), and that choice costs them mechanically. Varro awards personally outside normal paperwork.

Final count: 6+ = exceptional record; 3–5 = expected range; fewer than 3 = closes the citizenship track for this campaign.

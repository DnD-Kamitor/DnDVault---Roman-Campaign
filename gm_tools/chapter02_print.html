THE SHADOW OF MARS · CHAPTER 2
The Tribune's Gambit
A Roman Adventure for Five Characters, Level 3–4
3–4 hours · Fort Vindolanda · 175 AD
Scenes: 5 · Combat: 1 · Handouts: 2 · Maps: 2
Dawn breaks over Fort Vindolanda with a dead sentry and a sealed letter from Rome. The Tribune who arrives at first light carries legitimate Senate authority — and a hidden master who wants the spear at any cost. The Germanic drums begin at nightfall.

Adventure Background

The Story So Far

The characters are members of a special vexillatio at Fort Vindolanda on Hadrian's Wall. In Chapter 1, they descended into a vault beneath the fort and recovered an ancient spear — a weapon that whispers, corrupts, and drives men to violence. A possessed stone-hauler named Marius Coda attacked them with it still in hand. Legate Corvinus has seen the aftermath and is watching them carefully.

What the Players Don't Know

The Tribune serves Brutus. Senator Marcus Julius Brutus controls Rome's financial investigation committee. Six months ago he learned a relic of immense power lay under Vindolanda. He sent Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus with genuine Senate orders — orders Brutus manipulated into existence — to retrieve it. Lucius may not fully understand what he is retrieving or why it matters.

Quintus Flavius is the killer. Brutus pre-positioned a sleeper agent inside the fort six months ago. Quintus killed the sentry Sextus when Sextus caught him trying to steal the spear the night before. He is now hiding inside the Tribune's entourage.

Vercingetorix wants it destroyed. The Germanic chieftain leading the raid lost his father to the spear's corruption. He wants the relic returned — and then shattered. He is not an enemy if the players are willing to listen.

Running This Adventure

This chapter has one combat scene (Scene 3). The rest is roleplay, investigation, and one high-stakes decision. The investigation paths all lead to the same truth — the Tribune is corrupt. The players do not need to find all three clues, but more clues give them more leverage in Scene 4.

The chapter ends with an irreversible choice. Do not steer them. Let them make it and commit to the consequence.

Adventure Synopsis

  1. Scene 0 — Cold Open: Dead sentry. ROMA CADIT carved on the wall. Tribune's column spotted at dawn.
  2. Scene 1 — The Tribune Arrives: Legitimate authority. Corrupt purpose. Three clues planted.

Level & Rewards

Characters begin at Level 3. They advance to Level 4 at the end of this chapter regardless of choices made. Milestone advancement — no XP tracking needed.

The Adventure

Scene 0: Cold Open

Duration: 5 minutes. No choices. Hook only.

The characters are roused before sunrise. A sentry is dead at the north gate.

The body of Sextus — north gate watch, third rotation — lies slumped against the gate timber. His throat has been cut with a single stroke: professional, unhesitating. Beside him, carved into the stone with something sharp and patient: ROMA CADIT. Rome falls. The letters are deep and deliberate. Whoever wrote this had time.

No investigation is possible yet — there are no useful tracks and the killer is long gone. Give the players a moment to react, then cut to the trumpet call.

DM Note: Do not answer questions about who did this yet. The answer is Quintus Flavius. He killed Sextus last night when Sextus caught him attempting to steal the spear. Quintus is now inside the Tribune's entourage, arriving in minutes.

Scene ends: A trumpet sounds from the south — the Tribune's column has been spotted on the road.

Scene 1: The Tribune Arrives

Duration: 30 minutes. Roleplay. All three clues planted here.

Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus enters through the south gate with six Praetorian Guards in formation. He presents sealed Senate orders to Legate Corvinus at the gate.

He is younger than you expected. Late twenties, clean-shaved, armour polished to a mirror. Six soldiers behind him stand with the stillness of men who have been told to be impressive and have practiced it. The Tribune does not wait for an invitation. He hands the scroll to Corvinus, watches him break the seal, and speaks before Corvinus finishes reading. "Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus. I am here for the artifact recovered from beneath this fort. Senate authority. Your command is suspended pending review. Full cooperation is required."

Corvinus reads. His jaw tightens. He says nothing. He hands the scroll back.

The Three Clues

Plant all three here, passively. Do not draw attention to them.

Clue 1 — No Roll (Handout 4): Give the players Handout 4 when the Tribune shows his credentials. Let them handle it. The seal date is six weeks before the Senate officially heard about the spear's discovery. How did Brutus know to send someone?
Clue 2 — Auto (Cleric/Priest only): Tribune Lucius skips the ward gesture during the formal arrival oath. Any character with Religion proficiency recognises this as a significant omission — he is not properly consecrated for a military inspection. He either doesn't know the rites or doesn't respect them.
Clue 3 — Perception DC 15: Quintus Flavius stands third row in the entourage, slightly offset, already oriented toward the stables. He knows this fort's layout. He has been here before. Label this token "Soldier" on the map — do not name him yet.

If players confront the Tribune directly: He is polite, immovable, and slightly bored. He wants the spear brought to him by midday. He retires to the praetorium with his escort.

Corvinus, privately, to the party: "I cannot say openly what I think of this man. But you are not under his command. You are under mine — for as long as that means anything." He will not explain further.

Scene ends: Tribune sets a midday deadline and withdraws. Investigation window opens.

Scene 2: Investigation Window

Duration: 45–60 minutes. Exploration. Three parallel paths.

The party has the morning. The Tribune is in the praetorium. They can split up and investigate freely.

Path A — Tribune's Quarters

Stealth to get past the Praetorian at the door (DC 12), then Investigation DC 13 to find the dispatch box. Thieves' Tools DC 14 or Mage Hand to open it without damage.

Contents: Give Handout 5 — an encoded letter. Cipher is Caesar shift 3. Decoded: "THE ARTIFACT MUST NOT REACH ROME. BY ANY MEANS NECESSARY. OUR MUTUAL FRIEND'S AMBITIONS DEPEND IT. M.J.E." — M.J.E. is a known alias of Senator Brutus.

Path B — Cassia Liviana (Augur)

Found at the shrine, mixing herbs. She speaks freely if approached respectfully.

She has been tracking unusual deaths along the road from Rome for two months. All connect to a man who controls the Senate's financial investigation committee — she does not know his name, but she knows his methods. She asks for bog valerian root from north of the wall in exchange for more. She will explain why in Chapter 3.

Path C — Centurion Varro

At the principia, reviewing rosters. He is unsettled and will talk if asked about the Tribune's men.

Tribune was posted to the financial investigation commission six months ago — the same time Quintus requested a transfer here. A man named Quintus, "twelve-year man, quiet," requested transfer papers this morning — very fast, before the gate opened. Insight DC 11: Varro suspects Quintus is connected to Sextus's death but has nothing solid.

All paths lead to the same truth: The Tribune is Brutus's agent. The players don't need all three clues — one is enough. More clues give more leverage in Scene 4.

Scene ends: Drums from the ridge to the north. Torchlight in the dark. The raid has begun.

Scene 3: The Raid

Duration: 45–60 minutes. Combat. Three phases.

The drums start low — you feel them before you hear them. Then the torches appear on the ridge, moving fast, spreading left and right along the tree line. Thirty? Fifty? Hard to say. The horn on the wall calls stations. Soldiers run. The Tribune's Praetorians do not move from the praetorium door. They form a ring around it. They are protecting one man, and it is not Corvinus.

Phase 3 — Vercingetorix Enters

When Phase 1 is resolved, Vercingetorix enters through the breach. He does not attack. He speaks.

He is a tall man — taller than most in the courtyard, red-bearded, with a scar from jaw to collarbone that has healed badly. He walks through the gate with no weapon drawn and holds up one hand. His Germanic is heavily accented Latin: "We did not come for blood. We came for what was stolen from our fathers. Give us the spear. We leave. You live."

He will parley if the players engage. He wants the spear returned to his tribe. His real goal is to see it destroyed — the spear killed his father — but he will not say this unless he trusts the party.

Persuasion DC 14 (advantage if players mention Handout 4 or the Tribune's corruption): He stands down his remaining men and waits outside the wall while the players deal with the Tribune.

On failure: He withdraws anyway — he is not suicidal and his raid force is spent. He leaves a carved bone token. Investigation DC 10: it is a meeting marker. He will be one hour north of the wall at dawn.

Scene ends: Raiders gone. Tribune appears in the courtyard — armour clean, untouched. He watched from a window.

Scene 4: The Tribune's Move

Duration: 20–30 minutes. Roleplay. Irreversible decision.

He is in the courtyard before the last raider clears the gate. Armour spotless. Six Praetorians at his back. He does not look at the bodies on the ground. He looks at the party. "I witnessed your exchange with the Germanic leader. Praetorian Guard, all six, willing to testify. That is communication with an attacking force during an active raid. Under Senate authority — hand over the artifact. Now. Or I bring charges of collaboration before you reach Rome."

The bluff: Lucius is scared. He knows the party investigated him. He is moving first to control the narrative. Insight DC 14: his hands are too still — he is performing calm.

NPC Reference

Tribune Lucius
Wants: Spear delivered, no complications, out tonight.
Behaviour: Polished, cold, controlling. Will not fight personally.
Secret: Serves Brutus, not the Senate. The orders are real but corrupted in purpose. He may not know the spear's full nature.
Legate Corvinus
Wants: His command back. Order. The spear locked away.
Behaviour: Cold, watchful, military precision. Does not beg.
Secret: Knows the OTHALAN name. Was ordered to burn a document about it years ago. He knows this is bigger than a political dispute.
Cassia Liviana
Wants: Truth about the spear's origin. Party's trust.
Behaviour: Careful, precise, gives more than she says.
Secret: Has been tracking the cult for months. Knows the spear connects to something older than Mars worship.
Centurion Varro
Wants: To protect his men. Loyalty to Corvinus.
Behaviour: Blunt, honest, slightly out of his depth.
Secret: Suspects Quintus killed Sextus. Has no proof.
Vercingetorix
Wants: Spear returned, then destroyed. Tribe safe.
Behaviour: Controlled fury, willing to negotiate, commands respect.
Secret: The spear killed his father. He is terrified of it. He does not want to keep it — he wants it gone.
Quintus Flavius
Wants: To leave with the Tribune's entourage. Undetected.
Behaviour: Quiet, professional, avoids eye contact with the party.
Secret: Killed Sextus. Brutus's agent, not Lucius's. Expose: Deception +4 vs. Insight.

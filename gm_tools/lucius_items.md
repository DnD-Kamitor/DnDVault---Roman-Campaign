# Tribune Lucius — Items on His Body

## The Lockbox (iron, key on cord around his neck)

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Citizenship commendation letters | One per PC, already signed and sealed by Lucius. Posthumous — deliverable to Corvinus or Rome. Each letter names the bearer by name and recommends elevation to Latini or Civis status for distinguished frontier service. |
| Carved wooden horse | Germanic craft, small enough to fit in a palm. Personal memento — likely a gift from someone in his past across the frontier. No monetary value. |
| Amber rune token | Single rune carved into amber. OTHALAN — the same rune from the Session 1 vault. He knew. |

## What These Mean in Play

- **Commendation letters** = the citizenship track closes here, in the party's favour, regardless of what Corvinus does in Session 5.
- **Sealed orders** = the party now knows Corvinus wanted the spear destroyed. If they confront him in S5, he cannot deny it.
- **Seal ring** = a tool. Use it carefully. Impersonating a Tribune is a capital offence if caught.
- **Amber OTHALAN token** = Lucius knew about the vault symbol before Session 1. He never said. Why?

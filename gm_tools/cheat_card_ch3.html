Three clues — plant all three passively
Clue 1 (Perception 13 / Medicine 13): Eyes red-tinged in direct light. Rubbing same arm spot. Sleeping face toward the spear even when it moved. Medicine: fever + pre-combat arousal pupils.
Clue 2 (Insight 14 active): Advocates for keeping spear stronger than before, can't say why. Agitated when others move it. Micro-pause before "Are you alright?"
Clue 3 (Arcana 13 / Religion 14 passive): Spear physically leans toward them. Cassia sees it immediately if present.
Influenced player — 3 behaviors to plant (coach before session)

Setup
Map: NatureGoddessTemplePublic.jpg
Pre-entry: Survival 11 (active): two cover positions, north + south stones. Each delays a golem by 1 full round.
Read aloud
"Seven standing stones ring a granite altar. Each stone is twice your height, carved with spiraling runes that don't hold still when you look at them directly — they seem to shift, rearranging themselves."

"The air is different here. Heavier. Older. The spear pulses in long, slow throbs, like a heart slowing toward sleep."

"Thusnelda kneels at the altar. She doesn't speak immediately. Then: 'Leave nothing at the door. What you carry in, you carry to the altar. All of it. The weapon, the doubts, and whatever part of you this journey has taken.'"
Ritual — 4 requirements (Thusnelda explains these)
1. Spear on altar — unwrapped, bare iron on stone.
2. Blood from a warrior — voluntary, 1d6 damage, cannot heal until ritual ends.
3. All in unison: "We unmake what was made in darkness." Latin or Germanic — both work.
4. A sacrifice willingly offered — something of genuine value.
Grove signals: genuine offer = altar warms. Insufficient = stays cold. "The grove is not a toll booth." "That is not a sacrifice. That is a bet."

Influenced NPC — crisis at ritual start
Connection intensifies as ritual begins.

If caught + supported: Group WIS check DC 14. Success: they participate, corruption cleared. Fail: they grab for spear — Athletics grapple DC 12 OR call their name three times. If stopped: collapses, weeping, no memory of last hour.

If not caught: Increasingly urgent arguments to stop. At climax: attempts to take spear and run.
Mars — read aloud
"MORTALS."

"The word is not a sound. It moves through you like a wave, like a bell struck inside your chest."

"YOU DARE UNMAKE WHAT A GOD FORGED."

"The standing stones crack. Not shattering — cracking, like old bones."

"Thusnelda doesn't move: 'Finish the ritual. Do not look up. Whatever you see, do not look up.'"
Three options (run simultaneously or pick one)
A — Defensive hold: 3 Stone Golems (120 HP each, avatars, not killable at L5). Some PCs hold golems while one holds ritual. Cover positions delay each golem 1 round. Golems fade when ritual completes.
B — Skill challenge: 4 successes before 3 failures. One PC holds ritual per round:
Religion 14 (conviction) · Arcana 15 (channel grove) · Athletics 12 (hold spear) · Insight 14 (resist spear's final promise)
Each failure: +1 animated stone. Arcana: advantage if took arcane spear damage this session.
C — Bargain: Persuasion 17 or Religion 16 (address Mars directly): "Great Mars, what was done in your name dishonored what you stand for." Long silence. "...CONTINUE. I WILL NOT MAKE IT EASY. BUT I WILL NOT STOP IT." Gives advantage on all skill challenge rolls.
Conclusion
Success: Iron shatters — sound like an exhalation, something finally resting. Influenced NPC's eyes clear. Thusnelda seals fragments in clay pot. "Go before he finds his voice again." Corruption below 5 reduced by 1. Advance to Level 6.

Failure: Spear intact. Mars' presence intensifies — does not leave until ritual completes or spear leaves grove. Flee into forest. Session 4 harder.

Corvinus aftermath: Within a week, sends a clerk to party with commendationes paperwork and no explanation.
Ritual done. Level 6. Record handoff notes (see NPCs tab). → Ch4.

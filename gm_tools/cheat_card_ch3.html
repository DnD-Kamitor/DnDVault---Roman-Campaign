Chapter 3 — Through the Dark Forest

Level 5 → 6 at end. Players carry corrupting spear into enemy territory. One NPC is already influenced.
The Story (3 sentences)
The party crosses into Germanic territory carrying an artifact that is slowly possessing one of their own. A Germanic völva named Thusnelda holds the only knowledge needed to destroy the spear at the sacred grove. At the grove, Mars himself objects — the spear is his weapon, and he does not release it willingly.

Scene 0 — Cold Open (5 min)
Trigger: Start of session, no preamble. Dawn camp. Someone was standing by the spear at night.

The image: Torchlight. The spear's wrappings are on the ground, loose. One PC or NPC stands over it, awake, staring. They don't remember getting up.

What to say: "You wake to the sound of the canvas moving. The spear is unwrapped. [Name] is standing over it, eyes open, not blinking."

Handout 7 (Bloody Message): Give at this moment OR hand it pre-session. "MARS VIDET OMNIA / I did not want to do this." — carved into a tree at the camp's edge.
Ends when: Party reacts to what they see. Cut to Scene 1 immediately. Do not resolve the influence question here.
Scene 1 — Something is Wrong
Trigger: Travel begins. The forest closes in. One member of the party is not quite right.

Three clues the influenced NPC gives themselves away:
Clue 1 — Scene 1: They are first to suggest the sleepwalking was "probably stress." Helpful tone, slightly too fast.
Clue 2 — Scene 2: When Thusnelda says "your friend there has a thread in them" and looks at them, they break eye contact first.
Clue 3 — Scene 3: During the river crossing's worst moment, they go unnaturally still — listening to something no one else hears.

Active checks to detect:

Medicine (active: physical exam)13Pupils dilated, skin slightly cooler than ambient temp
Insight (active: watching during spear conversation)14They flinch — not at the topic, at a sound no one else heard
Arcana/Religion (passive: reading the spear's aura)13/14A second thread attached to the spear leads to someone in the party
If they catch it early: Group Wisdom check DC 14 at any point to break the connection through human contact. Success: NPC is devastated but free. If they don't catch it: Scene 4 — NPC grabs for the spear at ritual climax. Grapple DC 12 or call their name three times to stop them without combat.
Ends when: Party moves on. The forest gets darker. Germanic watchers visible in treeline.
Scene 2 — Thusnelda's Scouts
Trigger: 1–2 hours into forest travel. Movement in the trees. Then silence.

What's happening: 12 Tribal Warriors + 2 Scouts in a pincer from the flanks. Not blocking the path — surrounding it.

Perception DC 12 (active, before initiative): undergrowth disturbed on both flanks, not ahead. Party knows the threat is flanks, not front — advantage on first initiative if a PC covers a flank.

Three ways to end without combat:
  • Drop weapons + Persuasion DC 14 with any Germanic phrase from the player guide
  • Show the spear — scouts recognize it and stop (they know what it is)
  • Vercingetorix is with the party — raises a fist, scouts pull back instantly
If combat goes 3+ rounds: Vercingetorix arrives regardless and ends it.

Thusnelda: Appears after the ambush ends, from behind the scouts. She already knows why they're here.
CheckDCResult

Handout 6 (Thusnelda's Map): Give when she agrees to guide. Hand-drawn on rough paper — never geometric. Smudge the fourth river. Cross-spears over grove.
Ends when: Thusnelda agrees (or refuses) to guide. Party moves toward the river.

Scene 3b — The Farbog
Trigger: River behind them. Ground goes soft. Grass disappears. The path narrows to a single hummock trail through dark water.

The bog spirit (unquiet dead, drowned): Follows 20ft back. Does not attack unless ignored past 10 minutes.

Three ways to handle it:
Ignore: After 10 min, it weeps. DC 13 Wisdom save or Frightened 1 hour (source: the bog, not the spirit).
Acknowledge (Religion DC 12): Say "Unnamed one, we see you" and drop something of value in the water beside it. Spirit brightens, nods, stills. Thusnelda exhales.
Ask Thusnelda: She knows the rite. She'll do it — but costs her one ritual prep action in Scene 4 (one extra round).

Deeper in the bog — Vættir (Will-o'-Wisps, CR 2, modified):
  • Do not attack unless the party leaves the hummock path
  • Dispersed by: every party member simultaneously drops one item of value. DC 10 Religion (group) to coordinate in time.
  • A character with 2+ disrespects against the bog: Vættir target them first
Cult of Mars clue: Offering pole in the bog — new bark wrappings, fresh grain, and a Roman sandal buckle. Religion DC 15 (active): two traditions active here — one old, one hot and angry. The Cult has been claiming Germanic holy ground for Mars.
Ends when: Party reaches the far edge of the bog. Standing stones visible ahead.
Scene 4 — The Sacred Grove
Trigger: Stone circle. Seven standing stones. The spear pulls toward the altar at the center.

Survival DC 11 (active: before entering): Two natural cover positions — north stone and south stone. Using them displaces animated golem movement by one full round if Scene 5 triggers.

The ritual — four checks, any order, any character:
CheckDCFailure by 4 or less
Religion: words of unmaking (active, conviction)14Words land as intent, not invocation. Not wasted — try differently.
Arcana: channel the grove's power (active, surrender control)15If took arcane spear damage this session: advantage on retry.
Athletics: hold the spear steady as it fights back (active)12Spear twists — advantage to next person who tries Athletics this round.
Insight: resist the spear's final promise (active)14"They'll abandon you. Take me and they won't have to."

Influenced NPC — if not caught earlier: Attempts to stop ritual at climax. Athletics grapple DC 12 or call their name three times. They collapse weeping with no memory of the last hour. Corruption cleared by grove's power.
If ritual succeeds: Spear destroyed. Iron fragments remain (Thusnelda seals them in clay for Session 4 return to the vault altar). Mars is not finished — Scene 5 triggers.
If ritual fails: Party can address Mars directly — Persuasion DC 17 or Religion DC 16. He will not destroy the spear but may accept a binding.
Scene 5 — Mars Intervenes
Trigger: The moment the ritual completes (or fails). The stones move.

What's happening: 3 Stone Golems (120 HP each, CR 10 stat block — avatars of Mars, not killable at this level). The grove is Mars' contested territory. He did not authorize the destruction.

Survival options:
  • Cover positions (north/south stones spotted with Survival DC 11 earlier): displace golem movement by one full round each
  • Stealth DC 13: reposition behind stones — golem wastes one movement action pivoting. Ritual holder has advantage that round.
  • Address Mars directly (Persuasion DC 17 or Religion DC 16): name what the spear has done in his name. On success, golems freeze. Mars is angry but listening.
  • Flee: The grove boundary is 200ft. Golems do not pursue beyond the stones.
Mars satisfied: Spear destroyed, fragments returned to vault altar in Ch4 — Mars accepts this. Session 4 Antechamber ritual uses "remains of the spear."
Mars not satisfied: One character must make a formal oath at the vault altar before Session 4's final scene. Breaks the oath = corruption stage 4 immediately.
Ends when: Party escapes the grove or Mars is addressed directly and accepts. Level 6. Session ends.

Segment 1 — Sunken Armory
ANIMATED ARMOR ×4 — CONSTRUCTS, NOT UNDEAD
Animate when touched by spear-light. Target anyone who hesitates. Decisive movement does not draw their attention.

Perception 11 (active, reading armory floor before spear-light reaches it): Pieces in combat formation — two files, advance rank, reserve rank behind pillar. Calling this out: first defender who follows the layout gets advantage on first attack vs. any armor.

History 14 passive: Pre-Hadrianic, 80-100yrs older than current fort. This armory was sealed when Vindolanda was built over it.

Arcana 14 passive: Divine resonance, not undead animation. Spear-light activating dormant Mars consecration. No undead vulnerabilities.

Clue hidden under corroded shield (after combat): Latin inscription — "The god hears the loudest heart."
Segment 2 — Choking Hall
Poisonous marsh gas. CON save DC 14 each round in the hall unless party uses rune map to find air pockets.

If Nature 13 prepped: Gas denser near floor — breathe high, move fast. Halves saves required.

Stealth 13 (active, moving slowly): Locate air pockets by feel without needing the rune map. Find two safe positions.

Medicine 13 (active: treating unconscious PC): Stabilize without spell slot — Healer's Kit or Medicine proficiency. Patient continues after 1 round. Without Medicine: 1d4 damage/round until moved to air.

Cassia can spend a spell slot to clear the hall entirely.
Segment 3 — Elder Stair
Narrow steps descending around a dry well. Brutus' agent springs his trap: triggers rockfall to bury the spear.

Dexterity save DC 15 (active: reacting to rockfall): 2d8 bludgeoning on fail and drops the spear → contested STR check to stop it sliding into the shaft.

Acrobatics DC 13 (active: explicitly bracing/catching handhold): Alternative to DEX save. Use this if player describes the action.

If Lucius is in the escort (Secret Transfer path): He is the agent. He will deny it. Insight 15 (active: watching him specifically when the trap springs) catches the guilty look one round before he composes himself.
The agent (Quintus if alive, or a new Brutus plant) has a sealed wax token in his belt pouch. Thieves' Tools 12 to take it without him noticing. Opening it post-session: a second name inside the fort. Seeds for S5 epilogue.
Three Kneeling Figures — Mars' requirement
When they reach the final door Mars demands three mortals kneel in submission. Cassia insists: one must be Roman, one must be Germanic, one must be tied to the spear.

Two volunteers: He accepts with reservations. S5 starts normally.
Three correct (Roman + Germanic + spear-touched): Mars satisfied. One S5 trial drops one difficulty tier — your choice which. Pick the one that makes S5 most interesting.

Glory: Swear to bear the blame for whatever follows. Gain advantage on Persuasion vs. legionaries. Become the scapegoat if things go poorly.
Obedience: Promise to carry out Mars' Call to War (S5 mechanic). Guarantees favorable start to final trial but obligates them.

Ch4 — The God's Demand

L5→6  ·  4–5 hrs  ·  Farbog → Village → Sacred Grove → Sacrifice Circle → Skull Entrance → Ossuary
The party crosses the Farbog alone carrying Lucius's locked dispatch box and the corrupted Spear. At the Sacred Grove, they must race against the clock to halt Thusnelda's ritual suicide before the blood circle activates. Defeating the Bone Nagas at the skull threshold, they breach the curated Ossuary to advance to Level 6.

Scene 1 — Farbog Crossing  map: s3_farbog_crossing.html
The forest floor turns to water. Dead trees lean at angles, draped in something grey that used to be moss. Ahead, a path of flat stones vanishes under the surface and reappears on the far bank.
Bog hazard: Off the stone path — DC 12 Str or sink, restrained; DC 14 Athletics to pull free. The stones are always safe but narrow (single file). Box Aura: Undead sense the party from double range; group Stealth DC 14 at disadvantage if box carried unshielded.
Encounter — Bog Spirits
1 × Wight (Vaettir, HP 45, AC 14) + 1 × Will-o-Wisp (HP 22, AC 19) + 1 × Specter (HP 22, AC 12): Will-o-Wisp lures one PC off-path first (Insight DC 13 to recognize bait). Vaettir rises mid-crossing. Specter circles. Radiant damage ends the Vaettir permanently.
Life Drain (Wight)+4 to hit, 1d6+2 necrotic, DC 13 Con vs max HP reduction
Life Drain (Specter)+4 to hit, 3d6 necrotic, DC 10 Con vs max HP reduction
Shock (Will-o-Wisp)+4 to hit, 2d8 lightning. Ephemeral: can become incorporeal (resist all except force/radiant)
Clue: Worn rope on far bank — tied recently, not rot-frayed. Someone guides traffic across this bog.
Scene 1 — Germanic Village  map: s3_germanic_village.html
The village smells of woodsmoke and old bread. Longhouses stand solid, but half the doors stand open on empty rooms. A woman stops grinding grain. A child runs inside. An old man at the fire does not look up. Whatever took this village apart was not a raid; it just thinned.
Dramatic Question & Villager Intel

Dramatic Question: Can the party earn the villagers' trust to discover where Thusnelda went before his sacrifice is complete?

  • Thusnelda's Departure: Their venerable völva (he/him) took a ritual blade and walked north to the sacred grove before dawn to "settle the gods' debt". Three villagers followed and returned terrified.
  • The Chieftains: Two clan chieftains led their warbands south months ago down the old offering path and never returned.
  • DC 13 Persuasion / History: Thusnelda is of ancient lineage and has read the omens unerringly for twenty years. His conviction is total and deliberate, not madness.
The party travels alone. Tribune Lucius's locked dispatch box and signet ring in their pack carry Roman citizenship letters and Corvinus's secret mandate to destroy the spear.
Scene 2 & 3 — Sacred Grove & Sacrifice Circle  map: s4_sacred_grove.html
Ancient oaks rise around a weathered stone shrine with two faces carved into one rock — Roman Belatucadrus over older Germanic marks, smeared with fresh ash. Just beyond, seven standing stones ring a blood-soaked clearing.
Scene 2: Belatucadrus Shrine
  • Religion DC 13: Celtic/Roman syncretism. Ash obscures Roman features.
  • OTHALAN Rune: Matches Lucius's amber token and Session 1 vault seal.
  • Consecration: 10-minute prayer grants Belatucadrus blessing: advantage on damage rolls vs blood cult creatures; weapon counts as magical in the Ossuary.
Scene 3: The Ritual Sacrifice Circle

Dramatic Question: Can the party stop Thusnelda's ritual suicide before the blood circle activates?

Pressure Cooker: Started at dawn; 3 rounds to fatal cut. DC 13 Wis save vs 1 temp corruption each round inside circle.
Intervention Paths (Thusnelda, Völva Seer) — Box Penalty: Disadv on Persuasion if within 30 ft!
Path A: Evidence (DC 14 Persuasion)Show cult cloth, tally stick, or symbol (advantage). Convinces him the hunger is manufactured by cult rites, not gods. Disadv if Artificer's box within 30 ft! He stops and waits at dungeon mouth.
Path B: No Evidence (DC 18 Persuasion)Very hard. Overcomes 20 years of prophetic conviction through sheer rhetorical force. Disadv if box within 30 ft!
Path C: Physical Restraint (DC 13 Ath/Acro)Grapple/Disarm knife. He struggles without attacking party; buys time for Path A dialogue.
Path D: No Intervention / FailureCompletes cut. Dies in 1 hour without healing magic. Grove blessing lost; party loses guide.
Intel Unlocked (if stopped): Dungeon entrance is south down the old offering path. "The first room below makes the dead stay. Someone sorted the bones... someone is proud of that work."
Scene 4 — Dungeon Approach  map: SkeletonFortressEntrancePublic.jpg
The trees thin out and the ground goes soft underfoot before you see why. Ahead, the hillside opens in a mouth — not a metaphor. A skull carved from the living rock... A causeway of bone and old skulls runs down into a wide, glowing green marsh.
Marsh hazard: DC 12 Con save/round off the causeway, or 1 exhaustion + 1 temp corruption. The causeway itself is always safe.
Two supply caches (DC 11 Investigation)
  • Rations gone green, a tally-marked stick (the cult's been here a long time)
  • Second cache: torn cloth matching the chieftains' warband colors — 3rd confirmation this is the right place

What the crystal is (Arcana/Religion DC15, or automatic post-Vercingetorix)

The cult's real altar and the marsh's real source. Every drop of blood below was tribute funneled here. The rooms weren't separate encounters — one working, always heading here.

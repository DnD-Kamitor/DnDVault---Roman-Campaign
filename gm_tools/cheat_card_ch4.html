Scene 1 — Farbog Crossing  map: s3_farbog_crossing.html
The forest floor turns to water. Dead trees lean at angles, draped in something grey that used to be moss. Ahead, a path of flat stones vanishes under the surface and reappears on the far bank.
Bog hazard: Off the stone path — DC 12 Str or sink, restrained; DC 14 Athletics to pull free. The stones are always safe but narrow (single file).
Encounter — Bog Spirits
1 × Wight (Vaettir, HP 45, AC 14) + 1 × Will-o-Wisp (HP 22, AC 19) + 1 × Specter (HP 22, AC 12): Will-o-Wisp lures one PC off-path first (Insight DC 13 to recognize bait). Vaettir rises mid-crossing. Specter circles. Radiant damage ends the Vaettir permanently.
Life Drain (Wight)+4 to hit, 1d6+2 necrotic, DC 13 Con vs max HP reduction
Life Drain (Specter)+4 to hit, 3d6 necrotic, DC 10 Con vs max HP reduction
Shock (Will-o-Wisp)+4 to hit, 2d8 lightning. Ephemeral: can become incorporeal (resist all except force/radiant)
Clue: Worn rope on far bank — tied recently, not rot-frayed. Someone guides traffic across this bog.
Scene 2 — Germanic Village  map: s3_germanic_village.html
Longhouses around a central fire pit. Children stop. A woman comes out from the largest building, unhurried. She already knows why you are here.
Thusnelda — First Contact
ObjectiveConfirm the party intends to end the spear, not take it
GoalGive them what they need to reach the dungeon and survive it
SecretHer bloodline sealed the spear 200 years ago. She is the last who knows the chain-winch mechanism.
Voice"You have already paid. I want to know if you know what for."
Intel she shares (freely, if party is honest)
  • Geography: Grove is north — sacred, do not disturb unless you need to. Dungeon entrance is south, half a day.
  • The cult: Two chieftains from the raid. They have been feeding something in that dungeon for months. She does not know its name. Neither does the party — and that is correct.
  • The dungeon: Vercingetorix knows the layout. She knows the seal mechanism — four iron chain winches at the granite altar, Caesar's engineers built them. That is how the spear was bound.
  • Warning: Whatever is at the bottom is not Mars. It answers to his name. That is not the same thing.
Vercingetorix and Thusnelda know each other. They have been on the same side of this for 200 years from opposite directions. Let that land quietly.
Scene 3 — Sacred Grove (optional, north of village)  map: s4_sacred_grove.html
Ancient oaks. Something has been here far longer than the village. A standing stone at the centre carries the same rune Vercingetorix has not spoken aloud since Alesia.
What's here
  • Dryad guardian (HP 22, AC 11): Does not fight unless the grove is threatened. Speaks to druids or those who approach without weapons drawn. She confirms the dungeon is the wound the grove has been bleeding around for two centuries.
  • Belatucadrus shrine: A weapon consecrated here deals radiant damage to undead for 1 hour. Relevant for the Vaettir in the bog (if not yet fought) and Vercingetorix Bound in S5.
  • OTHALAN rune on standing stone: Religion DC 13 — rune of inheritance. Vercingetorix will go very still if he reads it.
Don't force it: Grove is optional. If party wants to push south immediately, let them. The Belatucadrus weapon blessing is a nice-to-have, not a required unlock.
Scene 4 — Dungeon Approach  map: SkeletonFortressEntrancePublic.jpg
The trees thin out and the ground goes soft underfoot before you see why. Ahead, the hillside opens in a mouth — not a metaphor. A skull carved from the living rock... A causeway of bone and old skulls runs down into a wide, glowing green marsh.
Marsh hazard: DC 12 Con save/round off the causeway, or 1 exhaustion + 1 temp corruption. The causeway itself is always safe.
Two supply caches (DC 11 Investigation)
  • Rations gone green, a tally-marked stick (the cult's been here a long time)
  • Second cache: torn cloth matching the chieftains' warband colors — 3rd confirmation this is the right place

Wail (1/day)DC 13 Con: drops to 0 HP on failure; 3d6 psychic on success.
Tactics: Rise mid-causeway, ~1/3 across. Stagger Wails across rounds (never trigger two in the same round).

Scene 2 — Blood-Growth Rooms
Dark red matter, wet-looking, veined, fed on blood deliberately over time.
Medicine/Nature DC12 (act)Organic, fed on purpose
Yochlol (HP 136, AC 15, MM p. 65) + 2 × Dretch (HP 18, AC 11, MM p. 57): Yochlol uses Mist Form, Web (DC 15), and Slam (+6, 1d6+3 + 6d6 poison). Dretches emit Fetid Cloud (10-ft radius, DC 11 Con). Focus fire to drop quickly.
Scene 3 — The Minor Altar
Four pillars ring a pulsing dais. A rehearsal for the real thing, not the real thing.
Religion DC14 (act)Never completed a working. Every mark is a false start.
3 × Shadow Demon (HP 66, AC 13, MM p. 64): Vulnerable to RADIANT damage, Sunlight Weakness (disadvantage on attacks and checks in bright light). Lighting the 4 corner braziers (DC 11 Dex / object interaction) floods the room with bright light, shutting down Shadow Stealth and exploiting sunlight weakness for an easy 2-round win.

SolutionCyan → Green → Yellow → Magenta ("beginning, growth, harvest, blood")

Breather beat — no combat by default. A fled Dretch can ambush only if pacing needs it.
Read Aloud
Rows of worn stone mark where dozens once knelt, facing a table still laid with offerings gone black. Something is still standing at that table.
3 × Wraith (Chieftains) — CR 5 (HP 67 each, AC 13, MM p. 302)
Life Drain+6 to hit, 4d6+3 necrotic, DC 14 Con save vs max HP reduction
Create SpecterHumanoid slain rises as specter under wraith control
TraitsIncorporeal Movement, Sunlight Sensitivity. Resist: acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder, nonmagical B/P/S
Land this as tragedy: First round: recognition, not aggression ("we asked —" / "it heard us"). Focus fire and radiant spells dispatch them cleanly.
Read Aloud
Four stone faces watch from the walls. At the center, a crystal shaped like a broken star burns steady and cold. The only thing in this dungeon that looks cared for.
What the crystal is (Arcana/Religion DC15, or automatic post-Vercingetorix)

The cult's real altar and the marsh's real source. Every drop of blood below was tribute funneled here. The rooms weren't separate encounters — one working, always heading here.

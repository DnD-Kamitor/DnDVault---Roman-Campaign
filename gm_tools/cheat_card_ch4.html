Ch4 — The God's Demand

L6 (advance to 7 at end)  ·  4–5 hrs  ·  The Blood Cult Dungeon
A causeway of bone crosses a glowing marsh into a dungeon two chieftains have spent months curating for something they believe is Mars. This session is that dungeon, start to finish. What's at the bottom is worse than what they expected to find.

Read Aloud
The trees thin out and the ground goes soft underfoot before you see why. Ahead, the hillside opens in a mouth — not a metaphor. A skull carved from the living rock... A causeway of bone and old skulls runs down into a wide, glowing green marsh.
Marsh hazard: DC 12 Con save/round off the causeway, or 1 exhaustion + 1 temp corruption. The causeway itself is always safe.
Two supply caches (DC 11 Investigation)
  • Rations gone green, a tally-marked stick (the cult's been here a long time)
  • Second cache: torn cloth matching the chieftains' warband colors — 3rd confirmation this is the right place

Scene 1 — The Ossuary
Round room, floor a carpet of femurs and ribs, stacked with care.
History DC13 (act)Sorted by size/age — curated for months, not years
2 × Bone Naga (HP 58, AC 15, MM p. 233) + 4 × Skeleton (HP 13, AC 13, MM p. 272): Both vulnerable to BLUDGEONING! Nagas cast Hold Person / Ray of Enfeeblement. Blunt weapons (maces, shields, warhammers) crush them in 1-2 fast rounds.
Scene 2 — Blood-Growth Rooms
Dark red matter, wet-looking, veined, fed on blood deliberately over time.
Medicine/Nature DC12 (act)Organic, fed on purpose
Yochlol (HP 136, AC 15, MM p. 65) + 2 × Dretch (HP 18, AC 11, MM p. 57): Yochlol uses Mist Form, Web (DC 15), and Slam (+6, 1d6+3 + 6d6 poison). Dretches emit Fetid Cloud (10-ft radius, DC 11 Con). Focus fire to drop quickly.
Scene 3 — The Minor Altar
Four pillars ring a pulsing dais. A rehearsal for the real thing, not the real thing.
Religion DC14 (act)Never completed a working. Every mark is a false start.
3 × Shadow Demon (HP 66, AC 13, MM p. 64): Vulnerable to RADIANT damage, Sunlight Weakness (disadvantage on attacks and checks in bright light). Lighting the 4 corner braziers (DC 11 Dex / object interaction) floods the room with bright light, shutting down Shadow Stealth and exploiting sunlight weakness for an easy 2-round win.
Scene 4 — The Rune Lock
SolutionCyan → Green → Yellow → Magenta ("beginning, growth, harvest, blood")
FailureDC12 Dex save or 2d6 force, sequence resets. No lockout, ever.
Breather beat — no combat by default. A fled Dretch can ambush only if pacing needs it.
Read Aloud
Rows of worn stone mark where dozens once knelt, facing a table still laid with offerings gone black. Something is still standing at that table.
3 × Wraith (Chieftains) — CR 5 (HP 67 each, AC 13, MM p. 302)

Create SpecterHumanoid slain rises as specter under wraith control
TraitsIncorporeal Movement, Sunlight Sensitivity. Resist: acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder, nonmagical B/P/S
Land this as tragedy: First round: recognition, not aggression ("we asked —" / "it heard us"). Focus fire and radiant spells dispatch them cleanly.
Read Aloud
Four stone faces watch from the walls. At the center, a crystal shaped like a broken star burns steady and cold. The only thing in this dungeon that looks cared for.
What the crystal is (Arcana/Religion DC15, or automatic post-Vercingetorix)

The cult's real altar and the marsh's real source. Every drop of blood below was tribute funneled here. The rooms weren't separate encounters — one working, always heading here.

The crystal cannot be destroyed here. Absorbs damage and hums ominously — Session 5's job. Party takes a safe Long Rest in secured Core Room and advances to Level 7 before descending.

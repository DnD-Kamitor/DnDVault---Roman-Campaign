Ch2 — The Tribune's Gambit

L4 → 5 at end  ·  3–4 hrs  ·  Starts after Ch1 short rest at dawn
The Tribune arrives from Rome with sealed Senate orders and strips the Legate of command. That night Germanic raiders attack in force demanding the same spear. The Tribune works for Brutus, not the Senate — the party must expose him, survive the raid, and decide who to trust.

Three clues — plant all three, passively
Clue 1 (no roll): Handout 4 seal date is 6 weeks before the Senate officially heard about the spear. How did he know to come?
Clue 2 (Religion 13 passive): Tribune skips the ward gesture during arrival oath. Julia Jana notices automatically. The oath is technically unsworn.
Clue 3 (Perception 15 active): "Soldier" in 3rd row is already oriented toward the stables. He knows this fort. He was briefed.

Varro (quiet, to party only): "Afraid men do stupid things."
Praetorian formation (Perception 12)
Two files of three, not ceremonial pairing. Lead pair hands on weapon hilts. Rear pair maintaining clear strike lanes. This is an execution formation, not an honor guard.

She mentions (not formally): needs bog valerian — 5 stalks, grows in wetland clearings north of the wall. Nature 13 in Session 3. Without it her wards run at half strength in Session 4.

Aelius Rufus — if alive from Ch1
In medicus. No check — he recognises the party and talks. Key intel: Marius said "Give it to me and I will keep you safe" while looking directly at whoever held the spear. Cassia does not have this detail yet. Pass it to her for a bonus reaction.

Read aloud
"Well. That was unfortunate." [surveys the carnage]

[to Praetorians] "Arrest them."

"These soldiers conspired with the raiders. They signaled the attack timing from the walls. I have witnesses."

From across the courtyard, the Legate steps forward: "I concur."

Varro takes one slow step to stand beside you. His hand moves to his gladius. "Do you," he says.
Insight 14 — the bluff
He hesitates before "enemies of Rome." His eyes move to Varro before he continues. He has been given lines and is not fully committed to them. This is a bluff — he does not want a trial. Catching it: player can invoke the hesitation directly in negotiation.
Party acts. Tribune leaves. Level up to 5. Fill in the consequences table below.
Decision options
Expose him (Handout 5 or Clue 2/3 evidence) → Praetorians hesitate, Varro intervenes. Tribune trapped. Political fallout continues into Ch3.
Negotiate (Persuasion 16 + at least one clue) → Tribune offers private deal: "Help me destroy the spear my way and I make this disappear."
Intimidate (Intimidation 15, controlled) → same deal but Tribune is warier. DC 5+ above: Praetorians disengage without a direct order.
Give spear to Vercingetorix → Roman crisis begins. Germanic crisis ends. Valid. Let it happen.
Fight → Tribune + 6 Praetorians (CR 2, AC 18, HP 32). State the numbers before initiative. This is survivable but costly.
Flee with Vercingetorix (if trust built) → he offers forest refuge. Fugitives with a powerful Germanic ally.
Surrender → spear goes with Tribune. Party confined to quarters. Ch3 opens as prisoners en route to Rome.

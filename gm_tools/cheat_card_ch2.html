Chapter 2 — The Tribune's Gambit

Players finish Chapter 1 → short rest → Chapter 2 starts at dawn. | Level 3 → 4 at end.
The Story (3 sentences)
A spear was found under the fort. It is supernatural — proximity makes people violent. Two factions want it: a corrupt Roman senator (Brutus) who will use it for political power, and Germanic tribes who consider it sacred. The players are caught between them and must choose.

Chapter 2 central question: "Can you trust a man who is breaking the law if his goal is right?"

What players need to accomplish this chapter: Survive the Tribune's arrival, figure out he is corrupt, survive the Germanic raid, and make a choice about who they trust.

Scene 0 — Cold Open: Dead Sentry (5 min)
Trigger: Session starts. Dawn. Party is called to the north gate.

What's happening: Sextus, a gate sentry, is dead. Killed during the night. On the wall next to the body: ROMA CADIT carved into the stone (Rome falls). No one saw anything.

What this tells players: Someone inside the fort killed him. The Tribune arrives this morning. These two facts are connected.

No choices needed. Just describe the scene. Let it sit. Cut to the trumpet call announcing the Tribune's column.
Ends when: Trumpet sounds. Tribune's column spotted at the south gate.
Scene 1 — The Tribune Arrives (30 min)
Trigger: Trumpet call. All officers to the south gate.

What's happening: Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus rides in with 6 Praetorian Guards and presents Senate orders. He claims authority over the spear. He tells Corvinus his command is suspended pending Senate review. He is calm, polished, and absolutely in control.

The three clues are here — plant all three, passively:
Clue 1 — No roll needed: Handout 4 shows the seal date. It's 6 weeks before the Senate officially heard about the spear. How did he know to come?
Clue 2 — Julia Jana only, auto: Tribune skips the ward gesture during the arrival oath. Any cleric/priest character recognises this as a serious omission — he is not properly consecrated for this visit.
Clue 3 — Perception DC 15: Quintus Flavius, third row of the entourage, is already oriented toward the stables. He knows this fort. He was briefed. Label him "Soldier" on the map. Don't name him yet.

Give Handout 4 here. Let a player physically break the wax seal. Read it aloud or pass it around.

Corvinus's reaction: He accepts the orders coldly. He does not argue. He is seething. He will find the party privately later and tell them he does not trust the Tribune — but he cannot say so openly.
Ends when: Tribune demands the spear be brought to him by midday. Investigation window opens.
Scene 2 — Investigation Window (45–60 min)
Trigger: Tribune sets a deadline. Players have the morning to act.

What's happening: Players investigate. Three independent paths lead to the same truth: the Tribune works for Senator Brutus, not the Senate.

Path A — Search the Tribune's Quarters
How: Stealth + Investigation DC 13 to get in and search without being caught.
Find: A locked dispatch box (Thieves' Tools DC 14 or Mage Hand). Inside: Handout 5 — the encoded letter.
Give Handout 5 folded. Say nothing. Shift 3 Caesar cipher. "shift 3" written faintly on the back.
Decoded: "THE ARTIFACT MUST NOT REACH ROME. BY ANY MEANS NECESSARY. OUR MUTUAL FRIEND'S AMBITIONS DEPEND IT. M.J.E."
M.J.E. = Marcus Julius Equitius, a known Brutus alias.
Path B — Talk to Cassia Liviana
Where: Shrine, augur quarters.
What she tells them (freely): She has been tracking unusual deaths along the road from Rome for two months. They all connect to a man named Brutus who controls the Senate's financial investigation committee.
What she asks in return: She needs bog valerian root from north of the wall. She will explain why in Chapter 3.
Path C — Talk to Centurion Varro
Where: Principia.
What he tells them: Tribune was posted to the financial investigation commission six months ago. A man named Quintus "twelve-year man, quiet" requested transfer papers this morning — very fast.
Insight DC 11: Varro suspects Quintus is somehow connected to Sextus's death but has no proof.
All three paths point the same direction: Tribune = Brutus's agent. The spear was the target all along.
Ends when: Drums start from outside the walls. Night is falling. Scene 3 begins.
Scene 3 — The Raid (45–60 min, combat)
Trigger: Drums. Torches on the ridge. Germanic warriors attacking the north wall.

What's happening: Germanic raiders attack the fort. The Tribune's Praetorians do not help — they form a ring around the praetorium and protect Lucius. Corvinus is directing the defense but is outnumbered.

Phase 1 (Wall, Map 1): 8x Tribal Warrior (CR 1/8) + 2x Berserker (CR 2) scaling the north wall.
Phase 2 (Gate, Map 2): Breach at the west gate. Same token types.
Phase 3 (Courtyard): Vercingetorix enters through the breach.

Vercingetorix — the critical NPC moment
He enters when Phase 1 is over. He does not attack immediately. He speaks.

What he says: "We did not come for blood. We came for what was stolen from our fathers. Give us the spear. We leave. You live."

Trust track — based on Chapter 1 events:
What happened in Ch1His attitude

What he wants: The spear returned. He doesn't want Roman deaths — he wants the relic.
His secret: The spear killed his father. He is terrified of it. He wants it destroyed, not kept.
Ends when: Raiders driven off or deal struck with Vercingetorix. Tribune appears in the courtyard — immaculate, untouched, having done nothing.
Scene 4 — The Tribune's Move (20–30 min)
Trigger: Raid ends. Tribune walks into the courtyard.

What's happening: Lucius accuses the party. He says they made a deal with the raiders. He has witnesses (his Praetorians). He demands the spear be handed over immediately or he brings them up on treason charges.

The real situation: He knows the party investigated him. He's moving first.

Insight DC 14: He's scared. The accusation is a bluff. He doesn't actually want a trial — a trial means scrutiny. He wants the spear and to leave.

What the players decide:
Hand over the spear: Tribune leaves with it. Corvinus is suspended. Brutus gets what he wants. Party gets Senate favor — for now.
Refuse and stand with Corvinus: Tribune leaves empty-handed and furious. Party is technically in defiance of Senate orders. Chapter 3 they are being hunted.
Expose him (need evidence from Scene 2): If they have Handout 5 or Cassia's testimony, they can call his bluff publicly. He backs down. Corvinus keeps his command. Tribune retreats but is not finished.
Give the spear to Vercingetorix instead: Radical. Tribune is furious. Corvinus is shocked. Germanic crisis ends. Roman political crisis begins. This is valid — let it happen.
Ends when: Players act. Tribune leaves. Chapter 2 is done. Level up to 4.

If Things Go Wrong
SituationWhat to do
Players attack the Tribune6 Praetorians respond immediately (CR 1, AC 18, HP 32 each). This is unwinnable at level 3. Lucius does not fight — he watches. Make the numbers very clear before initiative.
Players ignore the investigationThey won't have evidence for Scene 4. The Tribune's bluff succeeds. That's fine — consequences play out in Chapter 3.
Players try to ally with Vercingetorix mid-raidPersuasion DC 14 with Handout 4 as leverage ("the Tribune is corrupt, not all Rome"). On success he stops his men. On failure he's not hostile — just skeptical. Try again.
Players flee the fort entirelyValid. Vercingetorix does not chase. Tribune reports them as deserters. Chapter 3 they are outside the walls with no Roman backing. Adjust opening accordingly.
Quintus exposed before he leavesHe tries Deception (+4) against Insight. If caught: he admits to killing Sextus, implicates the Tribune, then tries to flee. This is a big gift for the party in Scene 4.

Everyone levels to 4. Do this at the end, not in the middle of the session.

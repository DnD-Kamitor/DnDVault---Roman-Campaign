Session 3: Through the Dark Forest — GM Reference

Scene Flow

  1. Cold Open — Camp is wrong: total silence, spear glowing, an NPC stands motionless beside it. "MARS VIDET OMNIA" carved on a tree. Cut to Scene 1 immediately.
  2. Something Is Wrong (Scene 1) — Three dead birds near perimeter. Handout 7 found carved in a tree. Private note passed to influenced player. Identify the influenced NPC via three clue paths.
  3. Thusnelda's Scouts (Scene 2) — Germanic scouts find the party. With Vercingetorix: safe passage offered, toll = Roman troop intelligence. Without: ambush (12 Tribal Warriors, 2 Scouts). Thusnelda arrives. She knows everything. She will guide them.
  4. River Crossing (Scene 3) — Vitulans river, 90 ft wide, spring melt. The Legate's pursuit arrives mid-crossing. Party splits across banks. Moral choice: legionaries just following orders.
  5. The Sacred Grove (Scene 4) — Seven standing stones, granite altar. Ritual requirements. Influenced NPC crisis. Sacrifice decision.
  6. Mars Intervenes (Scene 5) — Divine presence, not physical. Three options: combat golems (Option A), skill challenge 4-before-3 (Option B), bargain before starting (Option C).

Three Clue Rule: Identifying the Influenced NPC

Private note to that player at session start: "You woke last night and stood beside the spear for two hours. You don't remember. The spear feels warm. It has been arguing quietly that the power in this weapon could protect the people you care about."

Clue 1 — Physical Signs: Perception DC 13 (passive, during travel) or Medicine DC 13 (active examination): eyes have faint red tinge in direct light; rubbing same spot on arm; sleeping face-toward the spear even when it moved. Medicine: elevated heart rate, dilated pupils, slight fever — pre-combat arousal, not illness.

Clue 2 — Behaviour: Insight DC 14 (active: watching during spear discussion). Advocates keeping it more strongly than before but can't say why. Agitated when others touch it. Micro-pause before "Are you alright?"

Clue 3 — Spear Reacts: Arcana DC 13 (passive) or Religion DC 14 (passive): spear physically tilts toward the influenced character. Cassia if present: "There's a thread between that person and the spear. I missed it. I'm sorry."

Main Contingency: Ritual Failure / Interrupted

If the ritual fails or is interrupted: spear remains intact. Mars' presence intensifies. Party must flee with a partially-completed ritual, a god's attention, and a cursed weapon. Session 4 begins in a worse position.

Influenced NPC undetected: At the grove, they attempt to stop the ritual, then try to grab the spear at the climax. Stop with group Wisdom check DC 14 (holding them, calling to them — human contact). Fail: they run. Caught by Athletics grapple DC 12 or calling their name three times. If stopped, they collapse weeping, no memory of the last hour.

Sacrifice insufficient: Altar stays cold. No drama. The absence of response IS the response. "The grove is not satisfied. Try again. You have time." Second attempt: stones cool, light dims. "Something must be given or we leave here and come back."

Confirm before play: Is Tribune Lucius alive and with the party? Is Cassia with the party? Is Vercingetorix allied? What is the Legate's status (pursuing?). What is each character's corruption level (affects Scene 1 cold open intensity).

Session 2 → 3 transition: Tribune exposed = free but politically exposed. Fought Praetorians = fugitives in the forest. Deal made = Tribune travels with party. Fled with Vercingetorix = Roman soldiers in a Germanic war camp. Surrendered = prisoners en route to Rome.

Corruption effect: Characters entering with corruption 2+ must make DC 11 Wisdom save when the spear goes quiet (d8 travel event 7) or feel the urge to check it. Characters at level 5 remain at that level even after successful ritual.

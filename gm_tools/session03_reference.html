Session 3: Through the Dark Forest

GM Reference Card — All content DM-only unless marked otherwise — Regional Stakes — Level 4 → 5
Beat 1 — The Silence

"You wake before the watch calls it. Not because of sound. Because of its absence. No insects. No wind. The fire has burned to ash with no one feeding it. The camp breathes — the shallow breath of people sleeping — but there is nothing outside that breathing. Nothing at all."

THE MOMENT — Motionless

Who sees it first matters. The character keeping watch, or the lightest sleeper. They see the influenced party member standing beside the spear — upright, motionless, eyes open. The spear is pulsing: a slow amber-red, like breathing.

When approached: No response. When touched, they blink once, slowly, and turn their head. "I was just checking the watch." They were not on watch. They don't know how long they've been standing here.

Do not call it out mechanically. No roll yet. Play the moment — it is eerie, not dangerous. Then cut.

Beat 3 — The Carving

Ten paces from the fire's edge, where the trees begin: three words carved into bark. MARS VIDET OMNIA. The cuts are pale, not yet weathered. Done tonight. Cut to Scene 1 immediately.

Five Senses — Cold Open

Sight
Predawn darkness. The spear's amber-red pulse throwing no shadows. The influenced character standing like a post — perfectly still.
Sound
Nothing. No insects, no wind. Sleeping bodies breathing. The silence has weight — the forest is holding its breath.
Smell
Cold pine, dead ash from the fire, something metallic that wasn't there at dusk.
Feel
Cold earth under bare feet. Air thick and pressured, like before a storm that hasn't decided whether to arrive.
Taste
Nothing. The mouth goes dry. The body understood before the mind caught up.

Pre-Session: Deliver This First

Private note to the influenced player — pass before session starts:

"Last night your character woke, walked to the spear, and stood beside it for nearly two hours. You have no memory of this. Your character feels fine — better than fine: rested, certain, focused. The spear has been very quiet today. Almost polite."

Don't tell the player who saw them. Let it emerge. If asked how to play it: "Play it as though your character genuinely doesn't remember and isn't lying."

Beat 1 — Three Dead Birds

Morning. Packing camp. Three birds — ravens — dead in a perfect arc forty paces from the perimeter. No marks, no predator signs. Thirty paces from the birds: Handout 7 carved into a pine trunk. Fresh cuts, still pale.

Vercingetorix if present: He looks at the birds for a long moment. "My grandfather's men found the same sign, three days before the ritual failed." He says nothing else.

THE MOMENT — The Investigation Reveals the Influenced Character

The player has been holding the private note since before the session. This is when others find the thread. Three clue paths — any order, any character:

  • Physical (Perception/Medicine DC 13, passive): Red tinge in the eyes in direct light. Same spot on the left arm rubbed repeatedly. Face oriented toward the spear even when the body is turned away. Medicine: pre-combat arousal — elevated heart rate, dilated pupils. Not illness.
  • Behaviour (Insight DC 14, active during spear discussion): Advocates keeping the spear more strongly than before, without being able to say why. Micro-pause before "I'm fine." Agitated when others touch the spear.
  • The Spear (Arcana DC 13 / Religion DC 14, passive): The spear tilts toward that person. Not falling — tilting. Cassia if present: "There is a thread between that person and the spear. I should have seen it. I'm sorry."
Beat 3 — Moving Out

Investigation wraps because staying is dangerous. Corvinus is moving. The influenced character must be handled — or not — by the group as they travel. The spear goes with them regardless.

Beat 1 — They Were Already There

No approach, no warning. The undergrowth on both sides becomes scouts — twelve Tribal Warriors, two Scouts. They were positioned before the group arrived. One crow call from above. Then silence. Then: standing figures.

Without Vercingetorix: Ambush — pincer. Perception DC 12 identifies flanks as the threat. Persuasion DC 14 (any Germanic phrase, or demonstrating intent to destroy the spear) stops it before weapons engage.

With Vercingetorix: He walks forward, hand out, empty. The senior scout watches him. Nods. Safe passage. Toll negotiated: Roman patrol intelligence. Vercingetorix looks at the party. "It is their information to give or not."

THE MOMENT — Thusnelda Arrives

From the north, on foot. No hurry. She has been waiting at this point for two hours. She sits on a fallen log as if she owns the forest — because for the purpose of this ritual, she does.

"I know why you have come." — not a question. — "I know what the spear has done to one of you. I know what it will do at the grove if you are not careful. Sit down. Not you" — she points at the influenced character — "you stand where I can see you."

Do not soften this. She is not hostile. She is a doctor performing triage. She will guide them — but she is not asking permission.

Beat 3 — Trust Built or Not

Thusnelda guides them to the grove. How much she tells them depends on what they offer — a genuine gift, Vercingetorix vouching, or sparing a scout. She does not respond to flattery or rank. She responds to honesty and to things that cost something.

Five Senses — Scene 2

Sight
Shapes coalesce from undergrowth — they don't step out, they become visible. Thusnelda walks from the north, no escort, staff clicking on roots.
Sound
One crow call. Then silence. Then they are simply there. Thusnelda speaks slowly — translated in her head before the words come.
Smell
Woodsmoke from somewhere behind the scouts. Pine resin, lanolin. Thusnelda: beeswax, herbs, the earthy warmth of deep forest — a preview of the grove itself.
Feel
The pressure of being watched from multiple angles. Not hostile — observational. When Thusnelda arrives, that pressure recedes and focuses on her.
Taste
Adrenaline, dry mouth. The body understood the situation before the mind finished the inventory.

Beat 1 — The River

The Vitulans in spring melt: ninety feet wide, brown, fast, cold. No ford. Three options visible: swim, fallen-oak bridge, or string a rope line. Thusnelda says "The crossing is before the grove." She does not help — this is the party's trial.

Perception DC 11 (active) at the north bank: a boot print. Military sandal, size 10, made within two hours, heading north. Corvinus has scouts ahead. Window closes when rain starts (scene end).

THE MOMENT — Split Across the Banks

Midway through the crossing — some across, some still in the water — ordered movement from the south. Armored men on a forest path. Corvinus's vanguard: 8 soldiers and a centurion. Corvinus himself is behind.

The centurion does not want to arrest Romans. He has orders. He can see the party is half in a spring-melt river. He shouts across: "In the name of Legate Corvinus, halt. Return to the southern bank." He sounds tired.

The moral question: These soldiers are doing the same job the party used to do. They are not villains. They are the bureaucracy of empire in motion. The centurion is waiting to see what kind of people are on the other bank.

Beat 3 — No Clean Resolution

Crossed: fugitives confirmed. Stayed: prisoners. Struck a deal: watch it hold. The centurion either enters the river or stands on the bank and watches them go.

Beat 1 — The Grove

Seven standing stones, twice the height of a person, moss-covered granite. No trees inside the ring — the space was cleared before any living person was born. Altar at center: granite, stained dark. Utterly silent.

"The air inside the stone ring is warmer than outside. Not warm — warmer. And still. The birds do not come here. The spear, for the first time since you found it, is silent."

Thusnelda stands outside the ring. She does not enter. "Stand in the places I show you. The grove accepts your presence or it doesn't. You'll know." This is not her ritual.

THE MOMENT — The Influenced Character Acts

The ritual begins. The spear on the altar strains. This is when the influenced character moves.

Not grabbing — interposing. "Wait. Wait. This is wrong. We should think about this." Eyes wrong. Voice theirs, certainty the spear's.

Group Wisdom check DC 14 — not to fight them, to call them back. Each character contributes:

  • Hold them physically: Athletics DC 12
  • Say their name three times: automatic, once only
  • Name the specific thing they care about most: from the pre-session private questions — automatic if used sincerely

If reached: They collapse weeping. No memory of the last ten minutes. The grove clears their corruption — it recognizes the effort. They can participate.

If not reached: They grab the spear. Athletics DC 12 grapple to stop them. Failure = spear leaves the altar, ritual interrupted (see Quick Ref contingency).

Beat 3 — The Sacrifice

The altar requires something. Thusnelda does not specify what. "The grove knows the value of what you offer. You cannot trick it. You cannot give something you do not truly care about." The altar's response is the verdict. See Sacrifice Table in Quick Ref.

Five Senses — Scene 4

Sight
Seven stones twice the height of a person, no trees inside the ring, altar at center with old dark stains. No birds. No movement. The spear is motionless and silent for the first time.
Sound
Absolute stillness. No wind inside the ring. Even footsteps sound muffled. The influenced character goes quiet when they enter the stones.
Smell
Green wet moss, old stone, cold granite. Something sweet underneath — not flowers — organic, old, and sweet the way old blood is sweet.
Feel
The air inside is warmer — specifically, measurably warmer. A few degrees. Enough to notice. The hairs on the arm rise. The grove is aware of them.
Taste
Copper at the back of the tongue. Consistent, not fading. The grove is tasting them as much as they are tasting the air.

Environment

Ring: Approximately 60 ft diameter. Two natural cover positions behind north and south stones — Survival DC 11 before ritual begins. Advantage on first defensive roll if golems animate.

Altar: Granite, waist-height, 4 ft × 2 ft. Dark stained — multiple offerings over centuries. Thermally neutral despite the warmer air.

Northwest stone: Thusnelda DC 18 trust reveals a hidden depression behind it. Contains a sealed clay vessel — her fail-safe, never used. Breaks the ritual cleanly but releases a fragment of the spear's power rather than destroying it. Use only if catastrophic failure is otherwise certain.

If Ritual Fails Here

Spear remains intact. Mars' presence intensifies. Party flees with a partially-completed ritual, a god's full attention, and a cursed weapon. Influenced character's corruption locks at current level — the grove cannot help them now.

Session 4 opens in degraded position: Corvinus closer, spear still active, Mars already watching.
Beat 1 — Divine Attention

The ritual is underway. The spear blazes — not burning, radiating. The seven stones respond, glowing at their bases. The air holds one clear tone from no direction. The sky above the grove is simply open in a way that open sky usually is not.

The influenced character — recovered or monitored — can feel it arrive. "Something is here. Something has been watching."

THE MOMENT — Mars Is Here

Not a body. Not a voice from a mouth. A presence — the way a fully-armed legionary fills a doorway, except the doorway is every direction and the legionary is whatever Mars is when he pays attention.

Every character who ever held the spear feels a hand on their chest. Evaluative. Not aggressive.

"You have come far. You have not been cowards." The tone is not approval. It is acknowledgment. These are different things for a war god.

Three options, presented implicitly:

  • Option A — Fight: Two stone golems from north and south stones. Maintain ritual under attack — Concentration DC 14 if ritual holder takes damage.
  • Option B — Skill Challenge: Four successes before three failures — Religion DC 14 (speak words), Arcana DC 15 (channel grove), Athletics DC 12 (hold spear), Insight DC 14 (resist promise). Interrupt with Stealth DC 13 (reposition — ritual holder gets advantage that round).
  • Option C — Bargain first: Persuasion DC 17 / Religion DC 16 before anything begins. Success: "Continue. I will not make it easy. But I will not stop it." Advantage on all skill challenge rolls from here.

Beat 3 — The Spear's End

Successful ritual: the spear becomes light, then becomes nothing. The grove goes completely quiet. Thusnelda's shoulders drop. "Fifty years." She doesn't say more. Vercingetorix watches it end. He nods once.

Mars, before leaving: No speech. He marks each person who completed the ritual (Handout 10 — Mark of Mars). Then he is gone. The grove is just a grove again.

Five Senses — Scene 5

Sight
Spear blazing, radiating not burning. Seven stones glowing at bases. Sky above: open, intentionally so. If golems form: slow, angular, made of the same stone as the standing stones — purposeful, not monstrous.
Sound
One clear tone from no direction, held without wavering. Then: a presence that does not speak so much as make language happen in the air.
Smell
Hot iron — a smithy, blood on armor after a long fight. On success: rain that hasn't fallen yet. Ozone. Something clean.
Feel
Gravity doubles briefly — everyone pressed down. Not painful. Significant. On success: the warmth inside the ring dissipates slowly over minutes until inside and outside are the same temperature.
Taste
Metal — like a sword hilt held too long. On success: gone. Replaced by ordinary air. Notable by its absence.

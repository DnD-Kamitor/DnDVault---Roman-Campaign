Chapter 3 — NPC Reference

Through the Dark Forest. Primary: Thusnelda, Vercingetorix, Cassia. Secondary: Hilde, Aldric, Arnulf. Background: Corvinus (pursuit only).

Thusnelda
Völva — Germanic seeress. Primary guide. Cherusci tribe. Frame: grey → gold.
"Your friend there has a thread in them. I see it. Do you?"
Objective:Destroy the spear before its corruption spreads to the forest itself
Goal:Lead the party to the grove and complete the rite exactly as taught — she does not yet know "as taught" was never going to be enough
Agenda:Tests the party's respect at every scene. One disrespect: grudging guide. Two: she stops and demands a price. Three: she leaves them in the bog.
Secret: She lost a younger sister to the spear's influence two winters ago. The sister walked into the bog and did not come back. She does not volunteer this. If pressed (Insight DC 15), she shows it.
Stat: Druid (CR 6). AC 11, HP 65. Spells: Entangle, Speak with Animals, Call Lightning, Conjure Animals. Melee: staff +4, 1d6+2. Will not fight unless the grove is directly threatened.

Vercingetorix
Gaul-Germanic warlord. Returning from Ch2. Frame: gold.
"I gave them my word. I expect you to make me look good."
Objective:Keep the alliance alive; ensure Rome does not recover the spear
Goal:Shadow the party until they need him, then appear — never early, never late
Agenda:If the party fails to earn Thusnelda's trust, he steps in — but it costs him standing with her tribe. He will not use this card twice.
Secret: He made a promise to the Marcomanni chieftain — if the spear is destroyed, the tribes halt Wall raids for one year. He has not told the party. If they learn this (History DC 14 or Insight DC 13 when he speaks of "obligations"), it reframes his entire involvement.
Stat: Gladiator (CR 5). AC 16 (chain + shield), HP 112. Attacks: spear +7 (2d6+4) and shortsword +7 (1d6+4). Parry reaction: +3 AC until next turn.

Augur Cassia
Aasimar augur, travelling with the party. Frame: gold.
"There's a thread between that person and the spear. I missed it. I'm sorry, I missed it."
Objective:Understand what the spear has been doing to the omens, and to her
Goal:Identify the sacrifice the grove will accept -- and privately, decide whether it has to be her
Agenda:Reads every omen honestly, even the ones pointing at herself. Completes her bog valerian herb quest at the sacrifice site without announcing why. Sees the spear's aura connection to the influenced NPC unaided (Clue 3).
Secret: She has become increasingly certain the grove's "genuine" sacrifice is her own death, and has not told anyone. Whether she acts on that depends entirely on how Scene 5 plays out.
Stat: Acolyte (CR 1/4), Aasimar traits. AC 12, HP 13. Spells: Guidance, Sacred Flame, Light, Bless. Non-combatant by preference -- will not fight unless directly threatened.

Hilde
Thusnelda's apprentice. Cherusci. Scene 2 onward. Frame: grey.
"She said you would come. I did not believe her. I believe her now."
Objective:Learn from this — it is her first encounter with the spear's corruption
Goal:Watch the party; report to Thusnelda; ask questions she shouldn't
Agenda:Follows the party too closely. Asks about the influenced NPC by name, not by type. She notices first.
Secret: She is the first to name the influenced NPC's condition — not to the party, but to Thusnelda, quietly, in Germanic. A character with Germanic language proficiency overhears. This is Clue 2 alternate version.
Stat: Acolyte (CR 1/4). AC 10, HP 9. Spells: Guidance, Light, Spare the Dying. Non-combatant. Runs if attacked.
🦅
Aldric
Germanic observer. Suspected Roman informant. Scene 2. Frame: grey.
"The forest remembers everything. So do I."
Objective:Record what he sees and deliver it — to whom the party must determine
Goal:Stay close without drawing attention; learn who in the party carries the spear
Agenda:Present at the village. Watches too carefully. Leaves before the grove.
Secret: He is reporting to Senator Brutus, not to Corvinus. He has no idea what the spear actually does — he was told it is a Roman military asset. He is not malicious. He is also terrified.
Stat: Spy (CR 1). AC 12, HP 27. Dagger +4 (1d4+2). Sneak Attack 2d6. Skills: Deception +6, Insight +4, Perception +6. Investigation DC 14 to identify him as an informant.
🔥
Arnulf
Cherusci firekeeper. Scene 2–3b. Frame: gold (neutral/helpful).
"The bog does not forgive. It only waits."
Objective:Ensure the party does not defile any offering sites in the bog
Goal:Guide them across the Farbog if they earn it; stand aside if they don't
Agenda:Quietly explains what the bog requires at each step. Never repeats himself. Never warns twice.
Secret: He maintains the offering pole the party finds in the bog — the one with the Roman sandal buckle. He found it two months ago and has been leaving fresh offerings there to keep the Vættir from noticing the Roman intrusion. He does not know who left the original buckle.
Stat: Tribal Warrior (CR 1/8). AC 12, HP 11. Non-combatant role in this chapter. Will fight to defend the bog if absolutely necessary.
🦁
Legate Corvinus
Pursuit — off-screen. Frame: red. Evidence only at Scene 3.
"I gave them a head start. I did not give them a pardon."
Objective:Recover the spear before the Senate learns it is gone
Goal:Pursue with a small patrol; cut off the river crossing
Agenda:Between sessions: he sends scouts ahead. Evidence at Scene 3 river bank — military sandal, DC 11 Perception.
Secret: He does not want the spear for Brutus. He wants it destroyed. He cannot say this openly. He is buying the party time while appearing to pursue them. His scouts are told to report back, not engage.
Does not appear in person in Ch3. Centurion (Knight CR 3) commands the pursuit force at the river. Corvinus himself is one day behind.

# Lucius's Guards — 4 Legionary Milites

## What Each Guard Carries
| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Lorica segmentata | AC 14 |
| Scutum | +2 AC vs one attacker per round |
| Gladius | +3 to hit, 1d6+1 piercing |
| 2 × Pilum | +3 to hit, range 20/60 ft, 1d6+1 piercing. Bend on hit — shield they hit loses +2 AC bonus |
| 3 silver denarii | Standard pay |
| Military tessera | Name, unit, Corvinus countersign |
| 3 days rations | |

**Stats (Legionary Milites):** AC 16, HP 11, Speed 30 ft. STR 13, DEX 10. Passive Perception 10.

---

## What Happens Now Lucius Is Dead

With no officer present, their chain of command runs back to **Corvinus at Vindolanda**.

| Party action | Guard response |
|---|---|
| Show Lucius's **seal ring** | Treat holder as acting Tribune. Follow orders. |
| Show **commendation letters** | Same effect, softer — they comply but aren't happy about it. |
| Show **nothing** | Want to return to Vindolanda immediately to report to Corvinus. |
| DC 14 Persuasion (no authority shown) | May be convinced to stay at the village as rear guard. Will NOT enter the dungeon. |

---

## Best Uses for the Guards

- **Leave at the village** as rear guard — they protect the exit route and Thusnelda if he is wounded
- **Send back to Vindolanda** with Lucius's lockbox — insurance copy of the commendations reaches Corvinus
- **Do not bring into the dungeon** — they are soldiers, not delvers, and they know the difference

---

## If a Guard Dies

Standard death. No special loot beyond the kit above. Corvinus will ask about them when the party returns. He counts his men.

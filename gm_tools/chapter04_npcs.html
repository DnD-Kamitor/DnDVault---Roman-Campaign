The Two Cult Chieftains (Now Wraiths)
Scene 5 Gathering Hall. Tragic antagonists consumed by their own answered prayers. Frame: dark red.
"We asked for victory... it took our blood... it starves still..."
Objective:Originally: win lasting freedom for their clans against Rome. Now: quench the bottomless hunger of the god they awakened.
Goal:None left -- what remains reacts to blood and proximity.
Agenda:Watch the party in tragic silence for Round 1. On Round 2, the bloodlust overtakes them. Their dying screams shatter the necrotic resonance and reveal Mars's starvation.
Secret: They were not deceived by an impostor. Mars genuinely answered them -- and the price of an answered prayer from a war-god was their flesh, their sanity, and their eternal rest.
Stat: 3 × Wraith (CR 5, AC 13, HP 67 each, MM p. 302). Life Drain (+6 to hit, 4d6+3 necrotic, DC 14 Con save vs max HP reduction). Incorporeal Movement, Sunlight Sensitivity. Resist: acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder, nonmagical B/P/S.

The Glabrezu (Core Room Guardian)
Scene 6 Core Room Boss. Fiendish manifestation bound directly to the crystal-star focus. Frame: crimson.
"The harvest has already been gathered. You are only here to be measured."
Objective:Protect the crystal-star focus and funnel every drop of shed blood into the subterranean vault below.
Goal:Crush intruders within the dais darkness; defend the four gargoyle conduits.
Agenda:Opens with Darkness centered on the dais; grapples front-liners; isolates spellcasters with Power Word Stun. Does not pursue fleeing targets beyond the archway.
Secret: It was not summoned by an arcanist's spell. It was pulled across the planar boundary by the sheer volume of slaughter offered in this dungeon -- a direct gauge of how hungry Mars has become.
Stat: Glabrezu (CR 9, AC 17, HP 157, MM p. 58). Multiattack: 2 Pincers (+9, 2d10+5, DC 15 grapple) + 2 Fists (+9, 2d4+5) or 2 Pincers + 1 spell. Innate Spellcasting (DC 16): Darkness, Detect Magic, Dispel Magic at will; Confusion, Fly, Power Word Stun 1/day. Magic Resistance.

The Banshee Dread Mother
Scene 0 Marsh Entrance Alpha. First priestess of the marsh cult. Frame: red.
"Turn back... the water remembers every breath you took..."
Objective:Drown interlopers in the glowing green marsh.
Goal:Channel her Death Wail while her two sisters rise alongside her.
Agenda:Rise mid-causeway; stagger Wails across rounds.
Secret: Causeway hazard: falling into the glowing water requires DC 12 Con save or 1 exhaustion + 1 temp corruption.
Stat: 3 × Banshee (CR 4, AC 12, HP 58 each, MM p. 23). Corrupting Touch (+4, 3d6+2 necrotic). Horrifying Visage (DC 13 Wis). Wail (1/day, DC 13 Con: 0 HP on fail, 3d6 psychic on success).

The Altar Shadow Demons
Scene 3 Minor Altar Stalkers. Frame: red.
(Hissing clicks echoing behind the stone pillars)
Objective:Ambush and drain living creatures from the blind spots of the four pillars.
Goal:Keep the braziers dark; strike from dim light.
Agenda:Shadow Stealth between pillars; flee into shadows if exposed to bright light.
Secret: Lighting the four stone braziers floods the chamber with bright light, triggering Sunlight Weakness (disadvantage on attacks and checks) and disabling Shadow Stealth.
Stat: 3 × Shadow Demon (CR 4, AC 13, HP 66 each, MM p. 64). Claws (+5, 2d6+3 psychic). Vulnerable to radiant damage. Sunlight Weakness.

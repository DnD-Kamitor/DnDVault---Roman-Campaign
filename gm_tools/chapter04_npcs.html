Legate Corvinus
Fort commander. Blunt Force Nemesis. Frame: red.
"This fort answers to Rome. That means it answers to me."
Objective:Secure his legacy and the spear before Rome discovers what happened here
Goal:Control the escort composition and claim credit for the ritual
Agenda:Uses military law to threaten anyone opposing him. Signs side-orders to his bodyguard captain during the council. Arranges to arrest Cassia at session end if he feels sidelined.
Secret: He already sent a report to Rome that blames the party for the siege. He fears they will return and expose this. He is not wrong to fear this.
Stat: Veteran (CR 3). AC 17 (half plate), HP 58. Multiattack: 2 longsword (+5, 1d8+3). Parry reaction. Will not enter tunnels.

Augur Cassia Liviana
Fort augur, Aasimar heritage. Primary ritual support. Frame: gold.
"He wants witnesses, not soldiers. Let me speak plainly about what that costs."
Objective:Prevent Mars from razing the fort; interpret every omen correctly
Goal:Deliver the spear to the antechamber and speak the ritual form Mars requires
Agenda:Demands oaths before the procession begins. Shares visions without softening them. If Brutus' agent reaches her, she defends herself — she will not be a passive target.
Secret: If no one volunteers to kneel at the Three Kneeling Figures moment, she will kneel alone. She has already decided this. She has not told anyone because she knows the party will try to stop her, and she does not want that conversation before it has to happen.
Stat: Priest (CR 2). AC 13, HP 27. Spells (4/3/2): Guiding Bolt, Healing Word, Spiritual Weapon, Dispel Magic, Greater Restoration. Will use her last spell slot on the ritual if needed.

Centurion Varro
Fort centurion. Reliable ally. Frame: gold.
"Tell me who needs to be where. I will handle the rest."
Objective:Keep every soldier and civilian alive through tonight
Goal:Put the right people in the escort and keep the walls manned
Agenda:Appeals directly to the party's track record — he has watched them operate for four sessions. Refuses to abandon civilians even for tactically superior positions. Coordinates triage teams whether or not the party asks him to.
Secret: Morale is near collapse. Varro is the only thing holding it together — with his presence, not with words. Without him (if dead from Session 3 injury or transferred), morale collapses: all soldiers take –1 to Wisdom saves this session.
Stat: Knight (CR 3). AC 18 (plate), HP 52. Leadership recharge 5–6: allies +1d4 attack/saves for 1 min. Multiattack: 2 greatsword (+5, 2d6+3). Will not fight civilians or party members.

Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus
Senate liaison. Morally complicated. Frame: red or grey.
"I am not who you think I am. Neither is Brutus."
Objective:Survive the falling star of Brutus' agenda without being destroyed by it
Goal:Destroy or sabotage the spear en route — "controlled detonation" in the tunnels is his current plan
Agenda:Offers cooperation and plants traps simultaneously. Will approach one character privately before session end to offer a dispatch recommendation (citizenship). The offer is genuine — he has room on the dispatch for real names.
Secret: Still receiving ravens from Brutus ordering Cassia's death. He has not acted on this order. He is deciding whether he will. A high Insight check (DC 16) during the council catches him re-reading a small folded note under the table — he destroys it when he notices he's been seen.
Stat: Spy (CR 1). AC 12, HP 27. Rapier +4, 1d8+2 piercing. Sneak Attack 3d6. Cunning Action. Will not fight the party directly — has no reason to.

Vercingetorix the Red
Warlord. Outside the south ditch under truce. Frame: gold.
"I kept my word. Now I need yours."
Objective:Ensure the spear is sealed forever and his people are protected from the god's anger
Goal:Use the chaos to secure a sworn promise to return the spear to the grove after the ritual
Agenda:Negotiates through runners. Offers his warriors to guard the fort overnight (allowing a long rest) if honored. Names the party in a specific way that only his warriors remember, if the alliance is kept under pressure.
Secret: He is dying faster than he admits. This is his final campaign. If the party honored his alliance in Session 3, he reveals this now — quietly, without theatre. If they learn this before the session ends, it reframes his urgency and his unwillingness to negotiate on any secondary term.
Stat: Gladiator (CR 5). AC 16, HP 112. Multiattack: 3 (spear). Brave, Shield. Will not enter the fort or tunnels — his role is the diversion and the long-rest grant.

Senator Gaius Cassius Brutus
Never Present Villain. Off-screen all session. First "felt" not seen. Frame: red.
"Rome will not mourn a frontier garrison. It will thank you."
Objective:Prevent Mars from being appeased — keep the spear exploitable for political leverage
Goal:Burn the fort or kill Cassia before the ritual can be completed
Agenda:Commands sleeper agents inside the fort (the granary fire, the shrine desecration, the rockfall in the tunnels). Sends ravens through watchtower relays. Has already arranged a Plan B ritual in Session 5 if this session fails. Does not know the party is attempting to destroy the spear entirely — his intelligence ends at the treeline.
Secret: He does not know about the grove ritual. He believes the spear can still be retrieved and used. If he knew the party was attempting permanent destruction rather than relocation, he would have launched the siege earlier with more force. He is acting on incomplete information — this is his one exploitable weakness. A character who reaches him directly in Session 5 and reveals this will break his composure.
Stat: Noble (CR 1/8) with Assassin lieutenant escort (CR 8). Off-screen all Ch4. Token available if players demand a messenger — the messenger is not Brutus. Do not let Brutus be physically present or vulnerable this session.

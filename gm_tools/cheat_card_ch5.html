Read Aloud
The crystal-star's light does not fade when the demon guarding it falls. It gets worse. Cracks spread across its faces like frost across a window... In the silence where Tribune Lucius once stood, the stone floor shudders. Whatever this crystal feeds, it just fed it more than it wanted -- and the only stairs lead down.

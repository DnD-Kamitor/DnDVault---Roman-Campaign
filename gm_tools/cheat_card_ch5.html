Fort Snapshot at Dawn (read before Scene 0)
"Dawn breaks silver. Then the clouds split. The rain stops mid-drop. Every man on the wall blinks and lowers his bow because the arrows of the Germanic archers have frozen in the air. Cassia gasps. The parade ground ripples like water. Sand, black as coal, pushes up through the flagstones, covering everything. The cohort murmurs a single word: 'Mars.'"
All NPCs except party + designated witnesses (Cassia, Varro, Vercingetorix, Lucius, Corvinus, civilians) freeze at arena edge. They observe but cannot interfere.
Level / Advancement
Party arrives at Level 7. No advancement mid-session. Final level of the campaign.

Scene 0: The Silence Before Judgment

Read-Aloud (cold open)
"The air tastes of brass. Wind grazes your face without moving your cloak. Every sound in the fort muffles except your heartbeat. In the center of the parade ground the sand funnels upward into a throne carved from weapons that have never touched rust. Upon it sits a figure the height of the fort wall, armored in plates that drink light. His eyes resemble cooling lava. He speaks your name without sound and the syllables shake loose dust from the barracks beams."
"MORTALS. I WATCHED. I ARRIVED. NOW YOU ANSWER."
Party appears at center of black sand. Unarmed except what they carried into the ritual. Arena weapons hang along the perimeter, embedded in air like museum pieces.

Puzzle: The Stars of Aries (Scene 0)

When
Scene 0 only — before Mars speaks his first sentence. Window closes the instant he addresses the party.
What to See
The twelve arena weapons embedded in the air around the perimeter are not random. They form Aries the Ram — twelve stars, twelve weapons. The Ram's Eye (center of the horns arc) is a plain unadorned spear at the eastern arc, set slightly higher than the others.
Recognition Checks

In Option C
Invoking the Ram's Eye verbally during Option C — "we understood your language before you spoke" — grants +2 to the DC 19 Persuasion roll. One-time use only.

Scene 1: Mars Specifies the Trial

Mars' First Words
"You broke a covenant and mended another. You brought my spear home without my leave. Tell me why I should not salt this fort with your bones."
He offers three paths mid-sentence — no preference, he will accept any. He does not explain them beyond the name.
The Three Options
Option A — Champion Trial: One mortal stands as representative in single combat against a divine champion (Fausta Luperci). → Tab 2A
Option B — Trial of Blades: The entire party faces Mars himself until he is satisfied. → Tab 2B
Option C — Trial of Voice: The party convinces Mars through argument and memory. → Tab 2C
Timing
If the party hesitates longer than 1 minute of table time, Mars chooses Option B without comment.

3 Clues from Previous Sessions
S4 kneeling witnesses demonstrated the correct ritual posture — Mars expects them to speak first if Option C chosen.
S3 grove sacrifice (sincere or token) has been observed — Mars knows which it was.
S4 Antechamber: the spear completed the procession — the act itself was correct. The motivation is what he is judging.
Party names their choice → go to appropriate Scene 2 tab.

Riddle: The Soldier's Oath (Scene 1)

When
Scene 1 — Mars speaks this before announcing the three trial options. No skill roll. Character moment only.
Mars asks (read aloud)
"A soldier swears an oath at induction. He swears it again at each promotion. He swears it to no one present at either swearing. Name what he has actually sworn."
He does not call it a question. He asks it as a man asks something he already knows the answer to. He waits for as long as characters speak, then states the three options.
Answers and Their Effects
AnswerEffect in Scene 2
"To Rome / to the Emperor"Nods once. Standard conditions.
"To the men beside me / to the dead"Mars looks at that PC for one breath. Option C: their opening gains +2 if they invoke this loyalty.
"To something that doesn't need me to name it"Head tilt. Silence. Option B: he targets that PC last. Finds it interesting.
Invokes what the party did (grove, spear, kneeling)He listens without moving. Option C: adv on first Insight DC 15 mid-argument.
"To Mars" (direct flattery)"That is what the priest says. Not the soldier." Standard conditions.
SilenceSelects Option B without naming the other choices.
DM Notes
No wrong answer except silence. Allow everyone who wants to speak to speak. Mars acknowledges each speaker with his eyes. He does not repeat the question.
This is the last quiet moment before the trial. Give each player one beat as their character before the arena begins.

Scene 2C: Trial of Voice — Argument

Valid Arguments (these move him)
Honor: The spear dishonored Mars by sowing madness — cite corrupted worker, Cassia's visions, Varro's men driven to violence.
Discipline: Destroying or sealing it preserved the disciplined war Mars demands — reference sacrifices, escort kneeling, the procession.
War vs. Murder: They chose war's harsh honor over easy cruelty — examples: sparing Vercingetorix, saving civilians in siege, capturing traitors instead of executing.
If kneeling witnesses from S4 are present, have them speak first. Mars expects this. They glow faintly.

Scene 3: The Price — Scene 4: Return to Vindolanda

Scene 3: Regardless of Option — Mars Approaches
"He steps off the throne. Up close he smells of hot iron and cedar smoke. Behind him the fort flickers like a painting held in uncertain hands. Every soldier on the walls stands frozen mid cheer or mid prayer. Mars' gaze passes over them and returns to you. 'You have proven yourselves. I am not appeased, but I am satisfied.'"
Consequence Menu
Mark of Mars (Handout 10): Place face-up without comment. Let silence sit. Mark on the body — glows faintly during Armilustrium, dims in peacetime.
The Chosen: 1 PC (2 on crit success Option C). Benefits and obligations. Call to War mechanic activates. See DM Notes tab.
The Debt: If they bargained: "When I call, you will come. Refuse and the mark burns." Choose one future hook.
The Transformation: Any PC wanting a noble exit: become a guardian spirit of the arena beneath Vindolanda. Fort earns permanent advantage on morale checks.
Prophecy: Vindolanda will be a pilgrimage site for a century. Rome will fall from within in three generations. He says this without drama, as a fact.
Black sand recedes. Time resumes. Cohorts collapse to their knees.
Scene 4: Return to Vindolanda
Party wakes/stands where they were. Witness NPCs rush forward.
"Cassia touches the glowing mark on her arm and whispers, 'He listened.' Varro exhales for the first time in hours. The Germanic horns outside the ditch sound a single long note and then go silent. Vindolanda still stands."
Each player: short epilogue speech (next hour — speech to cohort, promises to vicus, treatment of prisoners).
Citizenship Resolution
If Lucius sent dispatch in S4: rider at the gate with imperial epistula (has been waiting since dawn). Lucius reads it first. Says nothing for a moment. Then hands it to the relevant character. The text confirms new status, unit, Lucius' attestation. One line in different hand: "Distinguished service on the Germanic frontier, confirmed by two independent sponsors. The Emperor's office takes note." — Do not narrate what the player's character feels. Ask them.

NPCs — Chapter 5

Mars, God of War
"I am not appeased, but I am satisfied." — after any valid trial completion
Obj: Receive genuine accounting of what was done with his spear and why.
Goal: Conduct formal divine judgment — he does not want to destroy them, he wants to be convinced or to test them.
Agenda: Has been watching since S1. Already knows what happened. Watching how they tell it.
Secret: He is lonely. His worship has declined. He chose Vindolanda because it is one of the last places soldiers still bleed in his name without irony.
Fausta Luperci — Divine Champion (Option A)
"She held the line at the Frozen Rhine when your Empire would not. She died alone. She serves today."
Obj: Test the party's representative in honest combat.
Goal: Make them prove endurance, not cleverness.
Agenda: She will fight without ego. She rotates targets. She does not negotiate mid-combat.
Secret: She remembers the cold. She remembers dying alone. She hopes the party does not have to.
Augur Cassia
"He listened." — after resolution. "The omens are still loud. I am better at telling which ones are mine and which belong to gods."
Obj: Witness the judgment and survive to record it.
Agenda: Already preparing the shrine text in her head.
Secret: She dreamed this. Exactly this. She has known since S1.
Centurion Varro
"I water the vines myself. Keeps my hands busy. Keeps memories quiet."
Obj: Get his soldiers home safe.
Agenda: Stands at the edge. Exhales when it's over. Awards the final commendatio personally.
Secret: He will carry Mars' scar openly. He wants to be asked about it.
Legate Corvinus
"Rome thanks you. I hope you believe it more than I do."
Obj: Come out of this with his command intact.
Agenda: Watches from the edge. If he tried to seize the spear in S4: his soldiers arrive hostile if Mars is displeased.
Secret: He is genuinely afraid. He has never been afraid of anything Roman. A god is different.
Tribune Lucius
"My father is silent. I am learning what that means."
Obj: Survive his choices. Try to make one good one at the end.
Agenda: If he sabotaged: DCs +2, Mars is angrier. If he sent the citizenship dispatch: he reads the epistula first. One moment of grace.
Secret: He is done taking orders from Brutus. He does not know how to say that.
Vercingetorix the Red
(Does not speak during the trial — watches. If allied: his warriors circle the arena and lend morale.)

Senator Brutus (off-screen)
(A letter, a rumor, a name Lucius flinches at. Not present.)

Agenda: If alive: still plotting, cult will try to steal spear's ashes after. If dead: cult splinters.

Corruption Level 5 PC (if present)
In Option A: DC 16 Wis save each round to act freely. 3 successes = corruption drops to 4.
In Option B: Mars targets them preferentially. Call them back by name 3 times (recreates S3 mechanic).
In Option C: Mars uses them as mouthpiece. Asks "What do you want?" Truthful self-awareness = no penalty. Selfish answer = –5 penalty to DC.
After session: cannot shed corruption without a selfless deed unseen by any authority within one year.

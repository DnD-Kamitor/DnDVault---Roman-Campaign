Read Aloud
The crystal-star's light does not fade when the demon guarding it falls. It gets worse. Cracks spread across its faces like frost across a window... Tribune Lucius says it before anyone else does. "It's not stopping. Whatever this feeds, it just fed it more than it wanted."

The Three Horrible Options — none is secretly correct
OptionCost
1. DestroyGuaranteed full fight to the end. Someone becomes the new guardian, permanently (PC or Lucius). No volunteer = fails, pick another.
2. Give to RomeShorter, angrier fight (~HP150 or a costly tactic). Crisis ends today; happens again to someone else's ancestors.
3. Give to MarsNo fight. No one bleeds today. Mars walks away vindicated, free to repeat this.

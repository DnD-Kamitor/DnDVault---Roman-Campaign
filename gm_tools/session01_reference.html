Session 1: Blood and Omens — GM Reference

Scene Flow

  1. Cold Open — Gaius Metellus is already dead. Corvinus summons the party. Give Handout 1.
  2. Meeting the Legate — He wants the spear. Cassia is present; she warns. Varro watches.
  3. Descent into the Ruins — Hall of Shields → Chamber of Chains → The Vault.
  4. The Spear — Who touches it? First corruption check.
  5. Return + Worker Frenzy — Gallic worker goes berserk. Aquilifer vacancy matters here.

Refusal Contingency

Polite refusal: Corvinus doubles pay → sends another unit → they don't come back → party re-ordered.

Cite Cassia's warning: He removes her from room. Cassia finds them in the hour given.

Outright defiance: DC 18 Persuasion to make him back down. Fail: Varro escorts them with "I'd go if I were you."

Aquilifer Vacancy

Gaius Metellus dead. Key locked in principia. Until role filled: morale checks in Scene 4 at Disadvantage. Failed morale = soldier flees, does not return. If player holds Aquilifer: eagle underground in Ch.3 triggers bonus Religion DC 14 check.

Corruption — Session 1

First touch or attune only Character who touches the spear → Level 1 Corruption. Level 1 effect: disturbing visions of conquest; DC 10 Wisdom save each dawn. Track per-character. Levels carry forward into Session 2.

Legate Marcus Aurelius Corvinus
Fort Commander, issues the mission, wants the spear, not the truth about it
Fifties. Commanding frame, iron-grey. Eyes bloodshot and specific: the look of a man who has not slept and has decided that does not matter. Voice like iron scraping stone. Counts the party like tools before a job.
"He looks at each of you in turn, the way a man counts his tools before a job."
OGAS
ObjectiveControl the most powerful military artifact unearthed in a generation.
GoalSecure the spear in his personal custody before Rome or the Senate learns of it.
AgendaUses the party as deniable retrievers. Silences the augur in front of the party without acknowledging her. Crafts a "routine excavation" cover story for anyone who asks.
SecretAlready dispatched a private rider to his patron in Rome. The party is not the first team he sent down: four men were lost before them.
VOICE
You've been chosen. Not for honor. For survival.
Scene 1: opening the briefing
You're going down there. You're bringing it up. You're not asking questions.
Scene 1: assigning the mission
You found it. Good. Give it to me and your bonus pay is doubled. Tonight.

Augur Cassia Liviana
Fort Augur, plants doubt she cannot act on openly, breaks cover in Scene 5
Pale as death, clutching a bloodstained cloth. Speaks once, quietly, toward the party rather than toward Corvinus. Her expression is not fear: it is the face of someone who has already made their own decision and is watching the party make theirs.
"She looks like someone who has been right about something terrible and cannot yet say so."
OGAS
ObjectivePrevent the divine catastrophe that has haunted her dreams.
GoalKeep the spear below ground or, failing that, keep it out of Corvinus' hands.
AgendaCooperates publicly with Corvinus in the principia; plants private doubt in the party whenever he cannot see her. In Scene 5, when she has run out of options, she drops the performance entirely and acts openly. She has accepted the risk.
SecretPerformed an augury about the party and knows they decide whether Rome burns, but not how.

Mars aversus est. The god of war has turned his face from us.

Do not give it to him. He will use it. I have seen what that means.
Scene 5: arrives at a near-run, breaks cover completely
The bargain was broken the moment your Legate put a shovel in that ground. You didn't break it. You walked into the middle of someone else's debt.

Senior Centurion, skeptical of the timeline, not of the mission, the party's future confidant
One hand on his gladius grip, looking like a man who is waiting for something to go wrong and has been waiting for several hours. Stands near the door throughout Scene 1. Corvinus does not press him to leave.
"A man who has already planned for this to go wrong."

GoalLearn what the spear truly is before the Legate deploys it disastrously.

SecretHeard a young legionary whisper "I understand him" during the worker's frenzy in Scene 4. Reassigned him quietly and told no one.

Whatever you saw down there: you're still here. That's the thing that matters tonight.

GUARDIAN
Scene 3 (Altar Chamber)
The Wight: High Priest of the Binding
Three-century guardian, was human, chose to remain, not the enemy, the last functional part of the bargain
Armored in Germanic plate that has not rusted. Eyes the color of old ice. Not the restless hunger of the walking dead: the settled weight of something that made a choice and is still keeping it. The OTHALAN contract token is pinned at his sternum.
"He does not rage. He does not hunger. He is resolute and tired, the way a soldier is after a very long watch."
OGAS
ObjectiveHonor the three-century oath he chose. Keep the spear contained as long as he draws breath (or its equivalent).
Goal (this scene)Prevent the spear leaving the altar: or, failing that, extract acknowledgment of whose debt it is.
AgendaRises when the party is fully in the chamber. Speaks once before any violence. Fights only if the party insists on taking the spear; holds position and stops if they back away.
SecretHe is not standard undead: he chose to remain after death as guardian (the same voluntary seiðr death as the preserved warrior in the Flooded Gallery). If he falls, the vault's containment weakens permanently.
VOICE (Wight Dialogue Reference)
The bargain was broken. Not by you. But by your kind. I am still bound. You are not.
Opening, when the party enters fully: Ursula understands without a check; highest Wisdom character understands without knowing why
The one who ordered this. His debt is greater than yours. But you carry it now.
DC 14 Persuasion or Insight success: a transfer of responsibility, not forgiveness
Carry it carefully. What is inside it is older than Rome.
On defeat: he folds, sits cross-legged at the altar base, goes still. Shadows dissipate.
STAT BLOCK (with escort)
Wight: CR 3, +1 Cult Fanatic (CR 2), +2 Shadows (CR 1/2 each)
Adjusted XP: 1,800 for 5 players: Hard, approaching Deadly at Level 3 with depleted resources.
Dark Resolve: regains 2 (1d4) HP/turn while any creature within 30 ft is Frightened. Cannot suppress this.
Life Drain: targets the spear-bearer preferentially. He is not trying to kill the party: he is trying to stop the spear leaving.
Exit condition: party backs beyond 15 ft of the altar → he holds position, Shadows stop. This is always available and is the right call for a depleted party.
TRIGGER RESPONSES
Party backs away from the spear → he holds. Combat ends. Mission failure for Corvinus, but the party survives intact: a valid outcome.
Party shows the contract scroll or OTHALAN token from earlier rooms → advantage on the DC 14 communication check.
Wight defeated, party wiped: if the Cult Fanatic is still standing, he takes the spear to Corvinus himself.
POSSESSED
Scene 4 (Courtyard)
Marius Coda
Gallic stone-hauler, a reliable man until today, possessed, not evil, by proximity to the open shaft
Shoulders like pillars, barefoot on the flagstones, eyes rolled back until only the whites show. Breath pours out of him in clouds despite the summer night. Each fist grips a gladius slick with fresh blood.
"He does not shout the words. He lets them fall like lit coals."
OGAS (possession-driven, not personal)
ObjectiveThe spear's compulsion, not his own: reach whoever carries the weapon.
GoalTake the spear from the party or die trying.
AgendaAttacks with two gladii, prioritising the spear-bearer above all else. If no one carries it, attacks the closest party member while screaming for it.
SecretNot responsible for this. He was stationed nearest the excavation shaft for three days, the longest exposure of any worker, and the spear's aura simply won.
VOICE
The spear calls, the spear drinks, the spear wants a champion.
Opening line, courtyard
Do you feel it laughing? / Mars does not wait for cowards. / Give it to me and I will keep you safe. / We were promised rest if we bled enough.
Between attacks
Mars.
Last word, if killed: whispered like a confession
STAT BLOCK
Marius Coda: Berserker, CR 2
AC 13  ·  HP 67  ·  Speed 30 ft
Two gladii attacks. Fully committed stance: weight on front foot, no guard on his left side (Perception DC 11 to read and call it: advantage on the first attack against that side).
Two Cultists of Mars (CR 1/8) stand near him, not yet attacking, muttering "Mars aversus est." Do not have them attack unless directly threatened.
RESOLUTION PATHS
Kill him → standard combat. Morale in camp drops afterward; whispers about the party spread.
Subdue him (Athletics DC 13 with two legionaries assisting) → collapses weeping, vomits dark bile, remembers nothing. Cassia can question him for visions later.
Deny line of sight (move the spear 60 ft away or magical darkness) → he freezes, confused, then sobs: becomes a terrified witness.
He breaks free (fails a grapple, sprints for the gate) → found dead of exposure two days later, both gladii snapped. Corvinus blames the party.
Minor NPCs: short entries, no full OGAS
Sicus
First work crew member sent down three days ago: found alive in the Shield Hall
Missing lorica, wound at the shoulder, stable but two days without water. Perception DC 11 finds his drag trail (someone concealed him, not an ambush); Medicine DC 9 confirms he's stable. Wakes with water or a healing effect within a round; otherwise DC 12 Medicine and two minutes.

What he gives the party: the cultists arrived before the party did (advance knowledge of the mission); a lever exists in the final chamber and one already used it; presses the second OTHALAN bone token into a character's hand: grants advantage on the Wight communication check, stacking with the contract scroll.

He cannot fight and will not enter the Runic Corridor if combat sounds come from ahead. If left to recover, he survives and can be found at the surface on return.
Aelius Rufus
Young signifer, third contubernium: one of the three fallen legionaries in the courtyard
Face-down, shallow gash across the back. DC 12 Medicine (action or bonus action) stabilizes him; failure means a second check next round or he dies. If saved, he survives and appears in Chapter 2 owing the party his life, and remembering what Marius said just before the killing started ("Give it to me and I will keep you safe," spoken directly to whoever held the spear).
Titus Flavinus & Gnaeus Peregrinus
Cultists of Mars: legionaries stationed near the excavation, exposed through the open shaft
Titus stands near the shaft, blade drawn but not raised. Gnaeus stands at the courtyard's edge, hands clasped, eyes fixed on the shaft opening. Neither is a combatant unless directly threatened: they are the first evidence that the spear's corruption spreads through proximity, not just touch. If Marius dies, they gain advantage on saves against the spear's aura for 24 hours. If he's subdued, they snap out of their trance and become terrified witnesses. Both appear in Session 2 as witnesses with fragmented memories.
Gaius Pullus & Marcus Dexter
The two dead legionaries in the courtyard
Gaius: throat wound. Marcus: blunt trauma to the skull. Both dead before the party surfaces. Purely atmospheric: no mechanics attached, but naming them grounds the cost of Scene 4 in something specific rather than abstract.

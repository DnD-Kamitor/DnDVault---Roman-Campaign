Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus
Senate Tribune — Brutus' agent — real legal authority, corrupted purpose
Late twenties. Lean, deliberate build. Purple-trim cloak; rings on every finger — one plain iron band, tarnished, too small for him (wears it to remember something). Walks like the space belongs to him. Never looks back.
"He looks at the fort the way a merchant looks at damaged goods."
OGAS
ObjectivePrevent the spear reaching Rome. Destroy it on Brutus' instructions.
GoalStrip Corvinus of command. Secure the spear in his quarters. Depart within 36 hours.
AgendaIssues orders immediately on arrival. Praetorians guard spear room during raid — not the fort. Accelerates departure after raid, citing "raider intelligence."
SecretNot fully convinced destroying the spear is right. Cassia's arguments reach him. The iron ring belonged to someone he failed. He hesitates before "enemies of Rome" in Scene 4.

You must be the ones who retrieved it. Good. You work for me now.

Augur Cassia Liviana
Fort Augur — knows the spear's true history — barely sleeping — already sent a warning
Forties. Augur's hood. Eyes that have been awake too long processing something large. Cross-legged on shrine floor, surrounded by wax tablets covered in cramped notation — not prayer notes: logs of the Tribune's movements, timed.
"She looks like someone who has been right about something terrible and cannot yet say so."
OGAS
ObjectiveWarn the party. Prevent the spear being destroyed without the proper ritual.
GoalShare the spear's true history. Plant the bog valerian detail for Session 3.
AgendaCross-legged in shrine, documenting Tribune's movements. Says nothing publicly until after the raid. Then comes to the party.
SecretAlready sent a private warning to a contact (Session 4 thread). Tribune's aura is "confused, not corrupt" — something under the mission. Short on bog valerian: one more full rite, then half-strength Session 4 wards.
VOICE
It cannot be destroyed anywhere except a specific grove. One day's march north. My people know the way.
If party asks about the spear directly (plants the Session 3 destination)
It grows in forest clearings near standing water. North of the road you'll be taking. I have enough for one more full rite.
The bog valerian detail — mentioned almost as an afterthought; DC 13 Nature in any wetland clearing in Session 3 finds five stalks
WHAT SHE SHARES (no roll required if asked directly)
Information Cassia holds
Spear's true history: killed a demigod son of Mars, three generations ago.
The curse: wielded with ambition = consumed by war. No exceptions on record.
Tribune's aura: "confused, not corrupt." Something real underneath his mission.
Bog valerian supply: one full rite remaining. After that, Session 4 wards at base level only.
Can identify cipher format on Handout 5 — she has seen similar Senate correspondence before.

Vercingetorix the Red
Germanic chieftain — leads the raid — does NOT attack first — is dying — trust track determines Sessions 3-5
Early fifties. Massive frame, still dense with muscle. Red hair remarkable at his age. Iron-flecked beard. Chieftain's torc thick as your wrist. No helmet. He walks through the breach without hurrying.
"He is the calmest person in the fort right now. That is the most frightening thing about him."
OGAS
ObjectiveReach the spear. Acknowledge his people's three-generation claim to its proper destruction.
GoalWalk through the breach and take the God-Killer — or negotiate with whoever shows him respect.
AgendaRaid begins at nightfall regardless. He leads personally because the spear's reawakening was felt by spirit-sensitive warriors across three tribes.
SecretHe is dying. A sickness, two years slow. He chose this campaign because of the spear — if it is destroyed properly, his people's three-generation wound closes. That is enough for him.
VOICE
The God-Killer is here. I can feel it from here. I came for the God-Killer. I did not come for you.
Scene 3 — walks through the breach, accented but clear Latin, looks at the party
Romans name everything they find. That is how they believe they own it.
I have been dying from a sickness for two years. I fight anyway. Your spear will not frighten me.
Your fort smells of fear. My camp smells of wood smoke. Fear is the worse smell.

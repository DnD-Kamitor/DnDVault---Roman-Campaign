Mars
Divine Antagonist. Genuinely Mars -- diminished, corrupted, starved. Not an impostor. Frame: divine red.
"You have carried my anchor through two ghosts and a king who would not die. Now choose, and choose knowing I will remember which one you make."
Objective:Reclaim the spear that was bound against his will and starve no longer
Goal:Keep this last open tribute-line however the party's decision leaves it
Agenda:Manifests in stages (presence-only rounds 1-2, full body round 3+), summons Wraiths or Wights to test the party before committing fully. Addresses a corruption-5 character directly, by name, before anyone else can answer for them.
Secret: he has genuinely respected this party since Session 1. That respect surfaces explicitly here, in the fact that he lets them choose the ending at all rather than simply taking the spear back by force.
Stat: Diminished Avatar (Mars Sheet — CR 20, AC 22, HP 300). Multiattack: 4 Divine Pilum (+10, 2d8+6 piercing + 1d8 radiant/fire). Divine Smite 1/round (+3d8 radiant). Divine Presence (DC 18 Wis or Frightened). Legendary Resistance 3/day. Legendary Actions 3/round. Interactive Unmaking: locking altar chains drops AC to 16, strips Legendary Resistance, and silences Smite!

Vercingetorix, Bound
His true final form. Not a villain thread -- a victim's thread. Frame: deep red.
"Again. Always again. Tell me this time is the one that ends it."
Objective:End the war he has been fighting since 52 BC
Goal:Fall in a way that finally counts
Agenda:Fights with tragic precision. Accepts a 1-on-1 champion's duel if challenged. Does not hate the party.
Secret: He knows the secret to breaking Mars's invulnerability: the Roman chain winches forged by Caesar's engineers at the granite altar.
Stat: Vercingetorix Bound (CR 11, AC 18, HP 150). Multiattack: 3 Dread Spatha attacks (+9 to hit, 1d8+5 slashing + 2d8 necrotic). Parry (+4 AC reaction). Relentless (1/day drops to 1 HP once, screaming "FINISH IT!" unless radiant/consecrated).

The Altar Chamber Wight (Session 1)
Branch-dependent cameo. The general who bound himself to guard the vault. Frame: grey.
"I held this three hundred years so it would not have to end like this. I am glad I do not have to decide."
Objective:(when alive at the table) See his three-century oath finally discharged
Goal:Witness the spear's fate decided, whatever it is
Agenda:Says his one line, unprompted, then is released the moment the spear's fate is settled. Does not return again in any form after this.
Secret: this beat only exists if he was spared in Session 1. If he was destroyed there, do not manufacture a substitute -- some choices close doors permanently, and this is one of them.
No combat stat needed here -- he is a silent presence, not a fight. His Session 1 stat block (CR 3 Wight) is not reused.

Janus (The Threshold Vision)
Divine Presence. God of beginnings, doors, and transitions. Frame: divine gold.
"Mars is neither good nor evil. He is war itself. Choose how you cross this threshold."
Objective:Frame the fatal choice awaiting the party in the Antechamber.
Goal:Remind the mortals that every doorway looks two directions at once.
Agenda:Manifests as a split-second vision at the Antechamber archway; does not engage in combat.
Secret: Janus reminds them that Destroy looks backward to heal old wounds at a mortal price today; Give to Rome looks forward to preserve today's lives at the cost of tomorrow's peace.
Divine Patron: Non-combat manifestation. Pays off the divine prayer in Session 3.

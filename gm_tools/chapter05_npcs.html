Mars — God of War

Divine Antagonist — The Final Arbiter. Present and physical for the first time.
OBJECTIVEConduct divine judgment over the mortals who took his spear without leave — and determine whether Rome deserves his continued favor.
GOALComplete the Armilustrium judgment ritual in one of three sanctioned forms: champion trial, trial of blades, or trial of voice.
AGENDAMars has been watching since Session 1. He references specific moments. He names the person who made the genuine sacrifice. He is not improvising — this is procedure.
SECRETHe is not angry at the party for moving the spear. He is angry it was moved without protocol. The distinction matters: protocol can be satisfied retroactively. Anger at them personally cannot. If the party figures this out, they can argue on his terms.
He respects what they did in the grove. He will not say this. An Insight DC 18 character who watches him carefully can see it in the one moment his posture softens — exactly when the grove sacrifice is mentioned.
"You broke a covenant and mended another. You brought my spear home without my leave. Tell me why I should not salt this fort with your bones."
Mars (Option B stat block): AC 20 · HP 300 (victory at 150) · STR 26 (+8) · DEX 16 (+3) · CON 24 (+7) · INT 18 (+4) · WIS 22 (+6) · CHA 22 (+6)
Saves: Str +14, Dex +9, Con +13, Wis +12 · Skills: Athletics +14, Perception +12
Immunities: poison, psychic; non-magical B/P/S · Condition immunities: charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed
Multiattack (4): God's Pilum +12/4d8+8+3d6 fire (range 120 ft) · War Blade +14/3d10+8+2d6 radiant (reach 10 ft)
Divine Smite (1/round): +6d8 radiant, DC 18 Con or Stunned until end of next turn
War Cry (Recharge 5-6): DC 18 Wis or Frightened 1 minute (save each turn)
Legendary Actions (3/round): Strike (1) · Summon Fallen — Wight or Shadow within 60 ft, max 3 total (2) · God's Eye — reads one creature's deepest intent, states it aloud, Frightened 1 round (3)
Mars as voice only until Option B or C invites him to step down. He rises from the throne exactly once: when combat begins (Option B) or when the argument is accepted (Option C). Until then, he watches from above.

Fausta Luperci

Divine Champion — Option A only. Honored dead, not construct.
OBJECTIVERepresent Mars in the champion trial. Assess whether the party's champion has the endurance and discipline Mars respects.
GOALFight until defeated (0 HP), 10 rounds complete, or Mars calls Enough — whichever comes first.
AGENDAShe attacks whoever has shown the most discipline. She rotates attention every two rounds. She does not react to taunts or feints — she has seen all of them.
SECRETShe was real. The Battle of the Frozen Rhine. She held the line alone. Mars honors that by giving her this role. A character with History DC 14 knows this and knows that she respects endurance above all. Feints and tricks do nothing. Standing and taking it earns her respect.
[No words. She inhales for the first time in centuries when she forms. She nods. She waits for the party to act first.]
Fausta Luperci (Divine Champion): AC 18 · HP 145 · Speed 35 ft
Multiattack (3): Spear +9 / 1d8+5 piercing + 1d6 radiant
Frozen Advance: Difficult terrain does not slow her. On move within 5 ft: DC 15 Con or speed –10 until end of next turn
Legendary Defiance (1/round): Below 50 HP: +20 temp HP, auto-succeed one save
After defeat or 10 rounds: kneels, presses spear butt to sand, nods. Mars inclines his head. Sand drinks her form. She is gone without drama.

Ally — Witness. Religious authority. Final sponsor.
OBJECTIVEWitness the divine trial and file a valid religious *commendatio* on behalf of every character who completes it.
GOALKeep the trial within protocol. If the party is struggling, spend a spell slot without being asked.
AGENDAStands frozen at the witness edge. Unfreezes the moment Mars says "Enough" or the trial resolves. Touches her mark first.
SECRETShe has already chosen her successor. The vicus girl who brought bread during the siege. She will not announce this until the epilogue if asked.
"He listened. The omens are still loud. I am better at telling which ones are mine and which belong to gods."

Centurion Varro

Ally — Witness. Senior NCO. Last Varro check.
OBJECTIVEStand witness and ensure the cohort does not break ranks during the divine manifestation.
GOALSurvive. Keep the fort stable. Award the second merit *commendatio* personally if a character shows restraint during the trial.
AGENDAStands frozen at the north edge with the cohort. Unfreezes with relief, not awe. He expected this would work. He does not say that.
SECRETHe is retiring after this. Farm outside Eboracum. He has been counting the days since the siege began. If asked, he says "forty-seven." Nobody asks what forty-seven means. He doesn't explain.
"I water the vines myself. Keeps my hands busy. Keeps memories quiet."
Knight CR 3 · AC 18 · HP 52 · Multiattack (2): Greatsword +5/2d6+3 · Leadership (bonus action, recharge after short rest): ally within 30 ft adds 1d4 to one attack or save for 1 minute
Last Varro check: if the party asks him what he thought of the trial, Insight DC 12 reveals he is holding something back. If pressed, one sentence — write it before session based on the actual campaign. "I didn't expect you to [specific thing]. That surprised me. I'm hard to surprise."

Senator Gaius Cornelius Brutus

Never Present Villain — Off-screen all session. Consequences arrive through others.
OBJECTIVEStop Mars from being appeased. His intelligence ends at the treeline — he does not know the trial is happening until it is over.
GOALIf the trial succeeds: pivot. Find another angle. He is already drafting a letter congratulating the party on "their service." He is not done with Vindolanda.
AGENDAOff-screen. If the party killed or arrested him in Ch4 resolution: his cult splinters and sends agents after the spear's ashes (Labyrinth scene trigger). If alive: he hears about the trial from a courier within a week and goes silent for a month.
SECRETHe funded the siege with two chieftains who owed him favors. Vercingetorix did not know this. If Vercingetorix learns it before he dies, it changes his last words to the party. DM discretion whether to deploy this.
Brutus does not fear the party. He fears what they represent: soldiers who answered to something higher than the Senate. That is not controllable. He has been trying to find the right price to name for three months.
[Letter, arriving one month after the trial]: "Congratulations on your distinguished service. I trust the frontier is quieter. I have always found quiet to be the most productive condition. — G.C.B."

Thusnelda

Ally (distant) — Not in the fort. Grove guardian. Epilogue contact.
OBJECTIVEGuard the grove. Send word when the trial is resolved.
GOALReceive the spear back, or receive confirmation it is in safe hands. Either is acceptable.
AGENDANot present during the trial. Thusnelda feels the moment Mars steps through the veil — she is in the grove when it happens and goes very still. She knows.
SECRETShe chose not to come. She could have. She decided the fort was Roman business. That distinction matters to her in a way she would not explain to Romans.
"The grove is quiet. Quiet is what you get when a wound has closed."
Epilogue gift: amber, runes, occasional letter carved on bark. If invited back to the fort, she comes once. She looks at the sand scar where the parade ground was and says nothing for a long time. Then: "Good."

Mars
Divine Antagonist. Genuinely Mars -- diminished, corrupted, starved. Not an impostor. Frame: divine red.
"You have carried my anchor through two ghosts and a king who would not die. Now choose, and choose knowing I will remember which one you make."
Objective:Reclaim the spear that was bound against his will and starve no longer
Goal:Keep this last open tribute-line however the party's decision leaves it
Agenda:Manifests in stages (presence-only rounds 1-2, full body round 3+), summons Wraiths or Wights to test the party before committing fully. Addresses a corruption-5 character directly, by name, before anyone else can answer for them.
Secret: he has genuinely respected this party since Session 1. That respect surfaces explicitly here, in the fact that he lets them choose the ending at all rather than simply taking the spear back by force.
Stat: CR 20 (`npcs/mars.json`). AC 22, HP 300. Multiattack: 4 Divine Pilum (+12, 2d8+7 piercing+divine). Legendary Resistance 3/day. War Cry (recharge 5-6, DC 18 Wis or Frightened). Divine Smite 1/round (+4d8 radiant). Legendary Actions ×3/round: Strike, God's Eye, Summon Fallen.

Vercingetorix, Bound
His true final form. Not a villain thread -- a victim's thread. Frame: deep red.
"Again. Always again. Tell me this time is the one that ends it."
Objective:End the war he has been fighting since 52 BC
Goal:Fall in a way that finally counts
Agenda:Whatever Mars commands, until commanded to stop. He does not negotiate, does not hesitate, does not hate the party for what they're about to do to him.
Secret: he is Vercingetorix. He has known it the entire time. He has simply had no one to tell -- until his Final Death gives him the chance.
Stat: CR 11 (`npcs/vercingetorix_bound.json`). AC 18, HP 150. Multiattack (2, Sword of the Last Charge, 2d6+5 slashing + 2d8 necrotic). Fear Aura. Aura of the Unfinished War. Command the Fallen (1/day). Relentless (1/day -- only radiant damage or a Belatucadrus-consecrated weapon prevents it). Legendary Resistance (1/day). Parry (reaction).

The Altar Chamber Wight (Session 1)
Branch-dependent cameo. The general who bound himself to guard the vault. Frame: grey.
"I held this three hundred years so it would not have to end like this. I am glad I do not have to decide."
Objective:(when alive at the table) See his three-century oath finally discharged
Goal:Witness the spear's fate decided, whatever it is
Agenda:Says his one line, unprompted, then is released the moment the spear's fate is settled. Does not return again in any form after this.
Secret: this beat only exists if he was spared in Session 1. If he was destroyed there, do not manufacture a substitute -- some choices close doors permanently, and this is one of them.
No combat stat needed here -- he is a silent presence, not a fight. His Session 1 stat block (CR 3 Wight) is not reused.

Tribune Lucius Valerius Maximus
The only fort NPC who travels with the party this session. His arc closes here. Frame: grey.
"It's not stopping. Whatever this feeds, it just fed it more than it wanted."
Objective:Reconcile Rome's orders (bring the spear back) with his own conviction (it must never leave)
Goal:See this decided one way or the other, by the party, not by him alone
Agenda:Advocates for whichever ending the party leans toward without pushing his own preference until directly asked. If asked, he is honest: he does not want to be the one who decides this.
Secret: he will not touch the spear himself under any circumstance -- except one. If the party chooses Destroy and needs a guardian, and the party trusts him enough to let him take it, he accepts without hesitation. It is the only clean resolution his divided loyalty ever had available to it.
Stat: reused CR 1 Spy block from Chapter 4, unchanged. Will not fight the party or Vercingetorix directly -- has no reason to and no combat role this session beyond staying alive.
